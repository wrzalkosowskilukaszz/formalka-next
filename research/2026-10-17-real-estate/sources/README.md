# Sources: buying real estate in Poland as a foreigner, doc 19 matter 21

Collected on 17.10.2026 by Claude, for the map `docs/formalka-next/maps/real-estate.md`. Level R1 under doc 19: sources only, with provenance. Production's registry has no procedure for this, so nothing here reaches it.

**Scope:**
- whether a foreigner needs the interior minister's permit to buy a flat, a house or land;
- the exemptions, and the border zone that cancels some of them;
- the permit: conditions, the application, the documents, the fees, the time, how long it is valid, the promesa;
- what happens without a permit; inheritance; the notary's duty and its change on 19.11.2026.

Not covered:
- agricultural land under the act on shaping the agricultural system (REA art. 1a ust. 6), beyond saying that it applies too;
- shares in companies that own real estate (REA art. 3e), beyond one row;
- taxes on buying (PCC, VAT), notary fees and the land register (matter 12 is set aside, and these are not the foreigner's own rules);
- mortgages and banks.

**Not yet checked by the separate checking agent** (`.claude/agents/official-fact-checker.md`): that check comes before anything here is relied on.

## What they say (FACT)

### Who needs a permit

| Topic | Content | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| The rule | Buying real estate as a foreigner needs a permit, issued by decision of the minister for internal affairs if the defence minister does not object, and, for agricultural land, the minister for rural development too | REA art. 1 ust. 1 |
| A foreigner | A natural person without Polish citizenship; a legal person seated abroad; a company of such persons seated abroad; a legal person or a commercial company seated in Poland that they control | REA art. 1 ust. 2–3 |
| Buying | Acquiring ownership or perpetual usufruct, by any legal event | REA art. 1 ust. 4 |
| EEA and Swiss | Citizens and entrepreneurs of the EEA states and Switzerland need no permit, except for agricultural and forest land for 12 years from Poland's accession to the EU, and for a second home for 5 years from it | REA art. 8 ust. 2 |
| Those periods | Poland joined the EU on 1.05.2004, so the 12 years ended on 1.05.2016 and the 5 years on 1.05.2009 (INFERENCE from the act's words; the accession date is not quoted here) | REA art. 8 ust. 2 |

### No permit needed (for others), unless in the border zone

| Topic | Content | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| A flat | A separate residential unit (samodzielny lokal mieszkalny) under the act on the ownership of units | REA art. 8 ust. 1 pkt 1 |
| A garage | A separate garage unit, or a share in one, if it serves the buyer's housing | REA art. 8 ust. 1 pkt 1a |
| After 5 years | A foreigner living in Poland at least 5 years since being granted permanent residence or EU long-term residence | REA art. 8 ust. 1 pkt 2 |
| A Polish citizen's spouse | Living in Poland at least 2 years since being granted permanent residence or EU long-term residence, for property that becomes the spouses' joint property | REA art. 8 ust. 1 pkt 3 |
| From a relative | A foreigner who, on the day of buying, is the seller's statutory heir under Polish law, when the seller has owned the property at least 5 years | REA art. 8 ust. 1 pkt 4 |
| Companies, banks | A controlled company's undeveloped urban land up to 0.4 ha in total; a bank taking property after an unsuccessful auction; a bank's shares when recovering claims | REA art. 8 ust. 1 pkt 5–7 |
| The border zone | None of these exemptions applies to property in the border zone, nor to agricultural land over 1 ha | REA art. 8 ust. 3 |
| What the zone is | All the municipalities adjacent to the state border, and on the sea section the area up to the shore; if that is narrower than 15 km, the next municipalities too. The minister lists them by regulation | Border act art. 12 ust. 1–2, 4 |
| The list | The 2005 regulation lists, among others, Gdańsk, Gdynia, Sopot, Szczecin, Świnoujście, Słupsk, Kołobrzeg, Przemyśl, Zgorzelec and Zakopane | `border-zone-2005-1580-decoded.txt` (a transcription; OPEN-R2) |
| Inheritance | The act does not apply to inheritance or a legacy (zapis windykacyjny) by those entitled to inherit by law; Polish law decides who they are when the applicable law has no statutory inheritance | REA art. 7 ust. 2 |
| A will | A foreigner who inherits by will and is not a statutory heir: without a permit applied for within 2 years of the opening of the succession, the property passes to the statutory heirs | REA art. 7 ust. 3 |
| Other cases | Transformations of commercial companies; certain closed-end investment funds | REA art. 7 ust. 1, 5 |

### The permit

| Topic | Content | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Conditions | Issued on the foreigner's application if buying does not threaten defence, state security or public order, social policy and public health do not stand against it, and the foreigner shows ties with Poland | REA art. 1a ust. 1 |
| Ties with Poland | Among others: Polish nationality or origin; marriage to a Polish citizen; a temporary residence permit (except those for victims of trafficking, UoC art. 176, and for a short stay, UoC art. 181 ust. 1), a permanent residence permit, or an EU long-term resident permit; a seat on the board of a controlled company in Poland; running a business or a farm in Poland | REA art. 1a ust. 2; UoC art. 176, 181 |
| Area | Property bought for one's own living needs may not exceed 0.5 ha; for a business, the area must match its real needs | REA art. 1a ust. 5 |
| Agricultural land | Also under the act on shaping the agricultural system | REA art. 1a ust. 6 |
| The application | The applicant, the property, the seller, the legal form of buying, the purpose; details in the regulation: identity, the property's data, the seller, the kind of transaction, the purpose and, for a business, the planned use, and the source of the money | REA art. 1a ust. 3; regulation § 2 |
| The documents | An identity document with citizenship, date and place of birth (and a register extract for a business); for land, the land register extract, the land registry extract and map, the zoning plan extract, each issued within 6 months; the seller's statement of will to sell; for a flat, only the flat's land register extract (or the building's) | Regulation § 3 |
| Objections | The defence minister and, for agricultural land, the rural development minister object within 14 days, which may be extended to 2 months | REA art. 1 ust. 1a |
| Checks | The minister may ask for evidence and check, with government bodies, the threat to defence, security, order and the state's interest; may set special conditions | REA art. 2 |
| A special economic zone | A decision within a month of the application | REA art. 3a |
| Valid | 2 years from issue | REA art. 3 ust. 2 |
| The promesa | A promise of the permit, valid 1 year; during it the permit may not be refused unless the facts changed | REA art. 3d |
| Ending the case | Discontinued at the applicant's request, or if the owner declares not to sell to the applicant | REA art. 3b |
| Fees | The permit: 1570 zł; a promise of a permit (the promesa): 98 zł | Stamp duty act, annex part III item 8 and part I item 20 |
| Without it | No legal act and no land register entry without the permit (and proof of its special conditions); a purchase against the act is void, and a court rules so also at the request of the local, regional or central authorities named | REA art. 5, 6 |
| The notary | The notary sends the minister a copy of each deed by which a foreigner bought property, within 7 days; from 19.11.2026 through the notaries' central repository, for a fee the parties pay, set by regulation | REA art. 8a ust. 1; Dz.U. 2026 poz. 1099 art. 1 pkt 3, art. 5 |
| A register | The minister keeps registers of property bought with and without a permit | REA art. 8 ust. 4 |

## Inferences (to be confirmed)

- **A non-EEA foreigner buying a flat in Gdańsk, Gdynia, Sopot or Szczecin needs a permit,** because those cities are in the 2005 list of the border zone (art. 8 ust. 3). The list is a transcription and dates from 2005 (OPEN-R2).
- **A flat outside the border zone needs no permit for anyone,** whatever the residence status (art. 8 ust. 1 pkt 1); a house or a plot does, unless another exemption applies.
- **The 5 years of art. 8 ust. 1 pkt 2 count from the grant of the permanent or long-term residence permit,** not from arrival in Poland.
- **An EEA or Swiss citizen buying agricultural land needs no REA permit now,** but the act on shaping the agricultural system applies (not read; OPEN-R1).
- **Buying without a permit when one is needed leaves the purchase void** (art. 6 ust. 1), and the notary cannot draw up the deed (art. 5). So the app treats "Do I need a permit?" as the first question, before any contract.
- **A decision of the minister** is challenged by a request for reconsideration (KPA art. 127 § 3), then in the administrative court (not read here).
