# Sources for wave 2: temporary residence permit and residence card (doc 19)

Collected on 16.10.2026 by Claude, from api.sejm.gov.pl:
- **The act on foreigners**, consolidated text Dz.U. 2025 poz. 1079 (25.07.2025). It is the base text.
- **Amendments after the consolidated text** (FACT, api.sejm.gov.pl `references` of DU/2013/1650):
  - Dz.U. 2025 poz. 1794, the act of 21.11.2025 amending the act on foreigners (in force 1.01.2026). Its main change is the MOS reform: the electronic application, personal appearance, and the voivode's certificate that the application was filed (with a QR code, through MOS) replacing the stamp;
  - Dz.U. 2026 poz. 203, the act of 23.01.2026 winding down the special act on aid to Ukrainian citizens (in force 5.03.2026). It matters for wave 3 (CUKR, UKR status).
