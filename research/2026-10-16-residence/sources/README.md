# Sources for wave 2: temporary residence permit and residence card (doc 19)

Collected on 16.10.2026 by Claude, from api.sejm.gov.pl. **Checked independently on 17.10.2026** by the separate agent `.claude/agents/official-fact-checker.md`, at the owner's request; its verdicts and what changed after each provision was re-read here are in `CHECK.md`. The corrections are changed in the lab; not independently verified.
- **The act on foreigners**, consolidated text Dz.U. 2025 poz. 1079 (25.07.2025). It is the base text.
- **Amendments after the consolidated text** (FACT, api.sejm.gov.pl `references` of DU/2013/1650):
  - Dz.U. 2025 poz. 1794, the act of 21.11.2025 amending the act on foreigners (in force 1.01.2026 by api.sejm.gov.pl, but **by point**, its art. 19: its art. 1 pkt 3–5, 8–41, 44 lit. b, 47–50 and 52, among them the MOS rules and the new art. 104–112a, from 27.04.2026 under M.P. 2026 poz. 370; pkt 42, 43, 44 lit. a, 45 and 46 (art. 226–229 and 231–237 cross-references) from 1.04.2026; so art. 228 pkt 2 (pkt 44 lit. b) is from 27.04.2026). Its art. 12 keeps the old rules for cases opened before a changed provision took effect, and art. 14 the old wording of the work act art. 3 for some long-term-resident cases. Its main change is the MOS reform: the electronic application, personal appearance, and the voivode's certificate that the application was filed (with a QR code, through MOS) replacing the stamp;
  - Dz.U. 2026 poz. 203, the act of 23.01.2026 winding down the special act on aid to Ukrainian citizens (in force 5.03.2026). It matters for wave 3 (CUKR, UKR status), and for every temporary permit too: its art. 12 pkt 5 adds art. 113c (the stamp duty is not refunded after a refusal or a discontinuance; art. 29 applies it to fees paid with applications from 5.03.2026), and its art. 45 lets a UKR status holder apply for permits under art. 114, 127, 142, 158 ust. 1 and 159 ust. 1 with special rules.
