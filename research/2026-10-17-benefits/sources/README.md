PA below is the 800+ act as in force on 17.10.2026.

## What they say (FACT)

### Who may get it

| Topic | Content | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| The amount | 800 zł a month per child, to the 18th birthday | PA art. 4 ust. 3, 5 ust. 1 |
| Who receives it | The mother or father the child lives with and is kept by; a de facto carer who applied to adopt; a legal guardian; a temporary guardian or one named by the authorities of the country of origin (protection act art. 113, 113a); foster care and institutions | PA art. 4 ust. 2, art. 2 pkt 10–10b |
| Foreigners covered | Among others: those covered by EU social security coordination or a bilateral agreement (lit. a–b); a temporary permit as a Blue Card holder (art. 127, 137a; lit. c); temporary protection with a PESEL UKR, living with family members (lit. ca, from 5.03.2026); **a residence card marked "dostęp do rynku pracy"**, living with the children, except a work permit for up to 6 months, students, seasonal workers and those working on a visa (lit. d); intra-corporate transfers (lit. e) and researchers (lit. f), with exclusions; UK Withdrawal Agreement beneficiaries (lit. g) | PA art. 1 ust. 2 pkt 2 |
| Living in Poland | For the whole time the benefit is paid, unless EU coordination or an agreement says otherwise | PA art. 1 ust. 3 |
| Not paid | Among others, when a family member gets a similar benefit abroad for the child (unless coordination or an agreement says otherwise) | PA art. 8 ust. 1 pkt 4 |

### The extra conditions for foreigners of lit. c–f (from 1.02.2026, first for the period from 1.06.2026)

| Topic | Content | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Work activity | The benefit for a month is due only if the person was "aktywne zawodowo" in Poland in the month before. Not required for a child who is a Polish citizen, a child with a disability certificate, or a child with a moderate or severe disability degree (or under a carer named by the country of origin) | PA art. 1 ust. 2a |
| What counts as work activity | Among others: compulsory pension and disability insurance as an employee, a contractor or in some other titles, on a contribution base of at least 50 % of the minimum wage; running a business; maternity, sickness and similar benefits, if the base or the benefit is at least 50 % of the minimum wage (30 % for those running a business); compulsory health insurance in listed titles; farmers' insurance; voluntary pension insurance | PA art. 2 pkt 1a; art. 2 pkt 8a (the minimum wage act) |
| Without work, but insured as unemployed | If in that month the person had health insurance under the health care act art. 66 ust. 1 pkt 24: the benefit for 3 months, or 6 months for three or more children; once in a period | PA art. 1 ust. 2b–2c |
| The child's school duty | The child must meet the one-year preschool duty, the school duty or the education duty, confirmed in the education information system (SIO), unless the child is too young or too old, the duty was deferred, or the child studies at a Polish university, a vocational course or a graduate placement (with the institution's certificate) | PA art. 1 ust. 2d–2e |
| Withholding | ZUS withholds payment when in doubt about the conditions, especially living in Poland; the Border Guard gives ZUS the border crossings | PA art. 1 ust. 2f–2g |
| School not confirmed | Payment is withheld; once SIO confirms it, the benefit is paid from the month the conditions were met | PA art. 23 ust. 3a |
| Monthly check | Payment follows the check of the work condition for the month before | PA art. 21 ust. 1a |
| Which periods | The changed rules apply first to the period from 1.06.2026; earlier periods keep the old rules | Dz.U. 2025 poz. 1301 art. 14 |

### Temporary protection (PESEL UKR, lit. ca, from 5.03.2026)

| Topic | Content | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| From when | Not earlier than the month in which the person and the child got a PESEL UKR | PA art. 18 ust. 2e |
| An in-person call | ZUS may call the person to appear within 3 working days; not appearing leaves the application unexamined or withholds payment; appearing late, the benefit is paid from the month after | PA art. 23 ust. 3b–3e |
| Leaving Poland | Payment is withheld if the person or the child leaves Poland, and resumes from the month it was withheld once they return, if the conditions are met | PA art. 23 ust. 3f–3g |
| The work and school conditions | ZUS's pages apply them to PESEL UKR holders too, from 1.02.2026, with a new application | ZUS 27.01.2026; zielonalinia 28.01.2026 (OPEN-B2) |

### Applying and after

| Topic | Content | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Where | To ZUS | PA art. 13 ust. 2, 10 |
| How | Only online: the ZUS profile ("profil informacyjny"), a bank's or credit union's system, the family ministry's system, or another named by ZUS. Signed with a qualified, trusted or personal signature or ZUS's own method, or after the bank's login | PA art. 13 ust. 5, 7 |
| What a foreigner of lit. c–f gives | Besides the usual data: the document used to cross the border, the document of legal stay (type, series, number, dates), and the work activity or the insurance of art. 1 ust. 2b; the same data for the child | PA art. 13 ust. 3 pkt 1–2 |
| Attachments | Among others: for lit. c, the residence card; the residence card and the decision on the permit, or another document giving a stay with the right to work | PA art. 13 ust. 4 pkt 3 lit. cb–cc |
| The period | 1 June to 31 May. Applications for the next period from 1 February. Filed by 30 April: decided and paid by 30 June; later months have later dates (to 31 October for August) | PA art. 18 ust. 1, 21 ust. 3–5c |
| From which month | From the month the application arrives; after a birth, if filed within 3 months, from the month of birth | PA art. 18 ust. 2–2a |
| A grant | No decision: information on the ZUS profile; a refusal, a change or an undue benefit is a decision | PA art. 13a ust. 1–4 |
| Letters | Only on the ZUS profile; delivered when opened, or 14 days after they are placed there | PA art. 13a ust. 5–7 |
| Gaps | A wrong form: 14 days to fix; missing documents: 14 to 30 days; otherwise left unexamined | PA art. 19 ust. 1–2 |
| Changes | Notify ZUS on the profile within 7 days | PA art. 20 ust. 1 |
| Undue benefit | Returned when paid on false statements or without the right to it; interest in some cases | PA art. 25 ust. 1–3 |
| Appeal | The administrative procedure code applies; the higher authority over ZUS is the President of ZUS; then the voivodeship administrative court of the place of residence | PA art. 28 ust. 1, 3 |

## Where the pages and the act differ

| Point | Page | Act |
|---|---|---|
| The time to explain after withholding | ZUS 22.09.2026: "3 dni od otrzymania wezwania", for every foreigner | 3 working days, and only for an in-person call to a temporary protection holder (art. 23 ust. 3b); otherwise "w wyznaczonym terminie" (art. 23 ust. 1a–1b) |
| The legal stay of PESEL UKR holders | MRPiPS 26.03.2026: "do 4 marca 2027 r." | Now 4.03.2028 (the EU decision 2026/1912; UKR sources) |

## Inferences (to be confirmed)

- **A foreigner on a residence card without "dostęp do rynku pracy"** (for example a student) is not among lit. c–f and gets no 800+ on that basis (INFERENCE from art. 1 ust. 2 pkt 2). Other routes (coordination, an agreement) may still apply: OPEN-B3.
- **A permanent or EU long-term resident card** carries "dostęp do rynku pracy" (UoC art. 244 ust. 1 pkt 11, granted when the person may work), so such a holder is under lit. d and the extra conditions (INFERENCE; to be confirmed on the card's own text: OPEN-B4).
