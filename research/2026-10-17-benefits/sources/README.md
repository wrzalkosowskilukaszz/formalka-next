PA below is the 800+ act as in force on 17.10.2026.

## What they say (FACT)

### Who may get it

| Topic | Content | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| The amount | 800 zł a month per child, to the 18th birthday | PA art. 4 ust. 3, 5 ust. 1 |
| Who receives it | The mother or father the child lives with and is kept by; a de facto carer who applied to adopt; a legal guardian; a temporary guardian or one named by the authorities of the country of origin (protection act art. 113, 113a); foster care and institutions | PA art. 4 ust. 2, art. 2 pkt 10–10b |
| Foreigners covered | Among others: those covered by EU social security coordination or a bilateral agreement (lit. a–b); a temporary permit as a Blue Card holder (art. 127, 137a; lit. c); temporary protection with a PESEL UKR, living with family members (lit. ca, from 5.03.2026); **a residence card marked "dostęp do rynku pracy"**, living with the children, except a work permit for up to 6 months, those "przyjętych w celu podjęcia studiów lub pracy sezonowej" and those working on a visa (lit. d); intra-corporate transfers (lit. e) and researchers (lit. f), with exclusions; UK Withdrawal Agreement beneficiaries (lit. g) | PA art. 1 ust. 2 pkt 2 |
| Living in Poland | For the whole time the benefit is paid, unless EU coordination or an agreement says otherwise | PA art. 1 ust. 3 |
| Not paid | Among others, when a family member gets a similar benefit abroad for the child (unless coordination or an agreement says otherwise) | PA art. 8 ust. 1 pkt 4 |

### The extra conditions for foreigners of lit. c–f

The dates differ by group (corrected after the check):
- **lit. c, d, e, f:** art. 1 ust. 2a–2g from 1.02.2026, applied first to the period from 1.06.2026 (1301 art. 29 pkt 2, art. 14); art. 23 ust. 3a, added by Dz.U. 2026 poz. 203, first for the period from 1.06.2026 (203 art. 31);
- **lit. ca (PESEL UKR):** the act as changed by 1301 and 203 applies from 5.03.2026 to the period from 5.03.2026 (203 art. 30). From 1.02.2026 to 4.03.2026 the special act art. 26 ust. 1a applied the same work condition (1301 art. 10 pkt 13 lit. b); benefits under the old wording ran to 31.01.2026 and a new application could be filed from 1.02.2026 (1301 art. 23). Benefits granted under the special act until 4.03.2026 run to the end of their period if the 800+ act's conditions are met (203 art. 43 ust. 1). The "lit. c–f" of art. 1 ust. 2a–2g includes lit. ca (INFERENCE, from 203 art. 30 and 31, which lists "c, d, e i f" to leave it out);
- **lit. a, b, g:** not subject to these conditions.

Not required for a child who is a Polish citizen, a child with a disability certificate ("orzeczenie o niepełnosprawności"), or a child with a moderate or severe disability degree (a light degree does not exempt), or a child under a carer named by the country of origin | PA art. 1 ust. 2a |

### Temporary protection (PESEL UKR, lit. ca, from 5.03.2026)

| Topic | Content | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| From when | Not earlier than the month in which the person and the child got a PESEL UKR | PA art. 18 ust. 2e |
| An in-person call | ZUS may call the person to appear within 3 working days; not appearing leaves the application unexamined or withholds payment; appearing late, the benefit is paid from the month after | PA art. 23 ust. 3b–3e |
| Leaving Poland | Payment is withheld if the person or the child leaves Poland, and resumes from the month it was withheld once they return, if the conditions are met. More than 30 days abroad ends temporary protection itself | PA art. 23 ust. 3f–3g; the protection act art. 109b ust. 1 pkt 2 |
| The work and school conditions | They apply to PESEL UKR holders, from 1.02.2026 under the special act and from 5.03.2026 under the 800+ act | The special act art. 26 ust. 1a (1301 art. 10 pkt 13); 1301 art. 23; 203 art. 30, 43; ZUS 27.01.2026 |
| Moving to a CUKR card or another permit | Temporary protection ends when a temporary, permanent or EU long-term resident permit is granted; the CUKR card states the grant of a temporary permit. Whether the holder then falls under lit. d is an INFERENCE (from the card's annotation) | The protection act art. 109b ust. 1 pkt 4; the special act art. 42m pkt 2 |

## Inferences (to be confirmed)

- **Corrected after the check:** lit. c, e and f depend on the type of permit, not on the annotation. A student (UoC art. 144) has free access to work (the work act art. 3 ust. 1 pkt 14), so the card carries the annotation; such a person is excluded by lit. d's own exclusion ("przyjętych w celu podjęcia studiów"), not by a missing annotation.
- **Permanent and EU long-term resident permits:** the annotation is placed when the holder may work or is exempt from a work permit (UoC art. 244 ust. 1 pkt 11), and both holders have free access to work (the work act art. 3 ust. 1 pkt 6–7). So they fall under lit. d with the extra conditions (the law; what a given card shows is practice: OPEN-B4 narrowed).
