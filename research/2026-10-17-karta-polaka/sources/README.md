## What they say (FACT)

### Who may get it

| Topic | Content | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| The conditions | A person who declares belonging to the Polish Nation and: (1) shows a link with Polishness by at least a basic knowledge of Polish, which they consider their mother tongue, and knowledge and practice of Polish traditions; (2) signs a written declaration before the consul or the voivode; (3) shows Polish nationality, or that a parent or grandparent, or two great-grandparents, were of Polish nationality, or presents a certificate of a Polish or Polonia organisation on active involvement for at least the last 3 years; (4) states that they and their ancestors were not repatriated under the 1944–1957 agreements | KP art. 2 ust. 1 |
| Not for | A person with Polish citizenship or a permanent residence permit is refused | KP art. 2 ust. 2, 19 pkt 4 |
| Polish origin found under the repatriation act | Enough, with at least basic Polish | KP art. 2 ust. 3 |
| The language | Shown by the Polish certificate (the language act art. 11a), a certificate of a school or studies in Poland, or of a school abroad teaching in Polish; otherwise the consul or the voivode assesses it in an interview | KP art. 13 ust. 7–8; the language act art. 11a ust. 1–2 |
| The evidence | Among others: Polish identity documents; civil status records, baptism records, school certificates; military, deportation or rehabilitation documents noting Polish origin; foreign IDs stating Polish nationality; an organisation's certificate; a repatriation decision; a parent's or grandparent's Karta Polaka | KP art. 13 ust. 2–3 |
| The organisations | The list of organisations allowed to issue the certificate is announced by the foreign minister; the current one is M.P. 2025 poz. 264 (18.03.2025) | KP art. 13 ust. 4; M.P. 2025 poz. 264 |
| A minor | On the parents' application if both, or one with the other's consent, hold the card; a child of 16 or more must agree; special rules after a parent's death; a parent whose card lapsed on permanent residence counts as a holder | KP art. 16 |

### Who decides, and how

| Topic | Content | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Who | The consul of the place of residence; or a voivode designated by the Council of Ministers | KP art. 12 ust. 2, 4 |
| Which voivodes, for whom | All 16 voivodes, for citizens of Ukraine, Belarus and Russia, and stateless persons in those states | REG § 1–2 |
| The form | A written application, with the data of art. 13 ust. 1, the evidence and a copy of a valid identity document | KP art. 12 ust. 1, 13, 14 |
| Fees | None, consular fees included | KP art. 12 ust. 3 |
| The checks | The head of the Internal Security Agency reports within 30 days on security grounds; the decision follows within 30 days of that report or of the 30 days' end | KP art. 19a |
| Refusal | Among others: the conditions not met; false data or documents; repatriation of the person or ancestors; Polish citizenship or permanent residence; defence, security or public order; acting against Poland or human rights | KP art. 19 |
| Appeal | To the Council for Polish Affairs Abroad ("Rada do Spraw Polaków poza Granicami Kraju"), filed through the consul or the voivode within 30 days of delivery | KP art. 9 ust. 2–3, 21 |
| The procedure | The administrative procedure code; for consuls, the consular law | KP art. 4 |

### What it gives, and what not

| Topic | Content | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| What it is | It confirms belonging to the Polish Nation and the rights of the act. It is not citizenship or Polish origin under other acts, and it does not allow crossing the border or settling | KP art. 3, 7 |
| The rights | Work under the work act; business on the terms of Polish citizens; studies and doctoral schools; education; emergency health care (unless coordination applies); a 37 % rail discount on single tickets; free entry to state museums; the consul's help when life or safety is threatened | KP art. 6 ust. 1, 4 |
| Visas and citizenship | No consular fee for a national visa to use the card's rights, or for a citizenship application | KP art. 5, 5a |
| Showing it | The rights of art. 6 ust. 1 pkt 3–5 need the card and a valid identity document | KP art. 18 ust. 3 |
| Settling | A holder who applies for permanent residence on the card's ground (UoC art. 195 ust. 1 pkt 9) may get a monthly benefit for up to 9 months, applied for to the same voivode within 3 months of the residence application; 50 % of the minimum wage for the applicant and the spouse and half that for each minor child for 3 months, then 60 % of those amounts; suspended on a refusal of the permit and lost when it is final; paid within the year's budget: when the applications reach 75 % of it, the minister announces, and the voivodes stop examining applications with payment that year until funds are secured | KP art. 8a–8d, 24b–24c |

### Validity

| Topic | Content | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| How long | 10 years; renewal for another 10 on application, not earlier than 6 months before expiry; indefinite if granted at 65 or more | KP art. 17 ust. 1–2a |
| A minor's card | 10 years, but at most until a year after coming of age; renewed if, at least 3 months before that, the holder applies and signs the declaration | KP art. 17 ust. 3–4 |
| Changes, loss | A new card on changed data; a duplicate if lost or destroyed; each for 10 years | KP art. 17 ust. 6–7, 18 ust. 4 |
| Annulment | By decision, among others for conduct degrading Poland or Poles, false data, security, or renunciation | KP art. 20 ust. 1 |
| Lapse by law | On the day the document of Polish citizenship or of the permanent residence permit is issued; the card goes back to the voivode within 14 days | KP art. 20 ust. 1a, 3 |

## Inferences (to be confirmed)

- **Waiting for a visit is the main practical barrier.** Offices ration visits by online booking or e-mail registration windows (Opole, Podlasie; PAGES). This is practice, not the act.
- **A temporary protection holder from Ukraine may apply to a voivode**, as a Ukrainian citizen (REG § 2); protection status itself is not a condition.
- **Applying for permanent residence ends the card's use:** the card lapses when the permit's document is issued (KP art. 20 ust. 1a), and the permanent permit map covers the rest (PS4).
