# Sources: the Karta Polaka, doc 19 matter 18

Collected on 17.10.2026 by Claude, for the map `docs/formalka-next/maps/karta-polaka.md`. Level R1 under doc 19: sources only, with provenance. Production's registry has no Karta Polaka procedure (its citizenship facts only mention a permanent residence permit obtained through a Karta Polaka, `reference/citizenship/facts-recognition.yaml`), so nothing here reaches the app.

**Scope:**
- who may get the Karta Polaka, from whom (a consul abroad or, for some citizens, a voivode in Poland), how, and what it gives;
- its validity, renewal, loss and annulment;
- the settlement benefit for holders applying for permanent residence.

Not covered:
- the permanent residence permit itself (`maps/permanent-residence.md`);
- repatriation;
- citizenship.

## What they say (FACT)

### Who may get it

| Topic | Content | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| The conditions | A person who declares belonging to the Polish Nation and: (1) shows a link with Polishness by at least a basic knowledge of Polish, which they consider their mother tongue, and knowledge and practice of Polish traditions; (2) signs a written declaration before the consul, or the voivode or a worker the voivode designates; (3) shows Polish nationality, or that a parent or grandparent, or two great-grandparents, were of Polish nationality, or presents a certificate of a Polish or Polonia organisation on active involvement for at least the last 3 years; (4) states that they and their ancestors were not repatriated under the 1944–1957 agreements | KP art. 2 ust. 1 |
| Not for | A person with Polish citizenship or a permanent residence permit is refused | KP art. 2 ust. 2, 19 pkt 4 |
| Polish origin found under the repatriation act | Enough, with at least basic Polish | KP art. 2 ust. 3 |
| The language | Shown by the Polish certificate (the language act art. 11a), a certificate of a school or studies in Poland, or of a school abroad teaching in Polish; otherwise the consul or the voivode assesses it in an interview | KP art. 13 ust. 7–8; the language act art. 11a ust. 1–2 |
| The evidence | Among others: Polish identity documents; civil status records, baptism records, school certificates; military, deportation or rehabilitation documents noting Polish origin; foreign IDs stating Polish nationality; an organisation's certificate; a repatriation decision; a parent's or grandparent's Karta Polaka | KP art. 13 ust. 2–3 |
| The organisations | The list of organisations allowed ("uprawnionych") to issue the certificate is announced by the foreign minister; the current one is M.P. 2025 poz. 264 (18.03.2025). It names organisations in 49 states, none in Poland | KP art. 13 ust. 4; M.P. 2025 poz. 264 |
| A minor | On the parents' application if both, or one with the other's consent (given before a consul, a notary or the voivode), hold the card, unless the other parent has no parental authority; a child of 16 or more must agree; special rules after a parent's death; a parent whose card lapsed on permanent residence counts as a holder. The child's application may be attached to a parent's; the child's case starts when the parent is granted the card | KP art. 16 |

### Who decides, and how

| Topic | Content | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Who | The consul for the applicant's place of residence (the application gives an address abroad); or a voivode designated by the Council of Ministers | KP art. 12 ust. 2, 4, 13 ust. 1 pkt 4 |
| Which voivodes, for whom | All 16 voivodes, whose competence citizens of Ukraine, Belarus and Russia, or persons stateless in those states, "mogą" use: it is an option, and it names no territorial rule. The KPA applies unless the act says otherwise; it sets territorial competence by the place of residence in Poland, or without one by the place of stay; an office observes its competence and forwards an application it is not competent for | REG § 1–2; KP art. 4 ust. 1; KPA art. 19, 21 § 1 pkt 3, 65 § 1 |
| The form | A written application, with the data of art. 13 ust. 1, the evidence and a copy of a valid identity document, on the form of Dz.U. 2016 poz. 1382: filled in in Polish; a photo 35 x 45 mm taken within 6 months | KP art. 12 ust. 1, 13, 14, 22; Dz.U. 2016 poz. 1382 |
| Fees | None, consular fees included | KP art. 12 ust. 3 |
| The checks | The head of the Internal Security Agency reports on security grounds within 30 days of the application's entry in the register (other bodies may also be asked); no report in time counts as no such grounds; the decision follows within 30 days of the reports or of the 30 days' end. A case not decided in time allows a ponaglenie | KP art. 19a; KPA art. 37 § 1 pkt 1 |
| Refusal | Among others: the conditions not met; false data or documents; repatriation of the person or ancestors; Polish citizenship or permanent residence; defence, security or public order; acting against Poland or human rights | KP art. 19 |
| No suspension of time limits | The special act's suspension of the voivodes' time limits to 4.03.2027 lists residence permits only (art. 100d ust. 1), not the Karta Polaka or its settlement benefit. For a permanent residence application on the card's ground the time limits do not run; acts done meanwhile stay effective (`maps/permanent-residence.md`) | The special act art. 100d ust. 1–2 (`research/2026-10-16-residence/sources/`) |
| Appeal | Against a decision granting, refusing, renewing or annulling the card: to the Council for Polish Affairs Abroad ("Rada do Spraw Polaków poza Granicami Kraju"), filed through the consul or the voivode within 30 days of delivery. Against a decision on the settlement benefit: the higher authority is the interior minister | KP art. 9 ust. 2–3, 21; 8b ust. 4 |
| The procedure | The administrative procedure code; for consuls, the consular law | KP art. 4 |

### What it gives, and what not

| Topic | Content | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| What it is | It confirms belonging to the Polish Nation and the rights of the act. It is not citizenship or Polish origin under other acts, and it does not allow crossing the border or settling | KP art. 3, 7 |
| The rights | Work under the work act (without a permit only for a holder staying on a basis of its art. 3 ust. 3 or visa-free: its art. 3 ust. 5 pkt 1); business on the terms of Polish citizens; studies and doctoral schools; education; emergency health care (unless coordination applies); a 37 % rail discount on single tickets; free entry to state museums; the consul's help when life or safety is threatened | KP art. 6 ust. 1, 4 |
| Visas and citizenship | No consular fee for a national visa to use the card's rights, or for accepting an application for the grant ("nadanie") of citizenship | KP art. 5, 5a |
| Showing it | The rights of art. 6 ust. 1 pkt 3–5 need the card and a valid identity document | KP art. 18 ust. 3 |
| Settling | A holder who applies for permanent residence on the card's ground (UoC art. 195 ust. 1 pkt 9) may get a benefit for up to 9 months, applied for to the same voivode within 3 months of the residence application; 50 % of the minimum wage for the applicant and the spouse and half that for each minor child for 3 months, then 60 % of those amounts, counting only family members staying with the applicant in Poland; suspended on a refusal of the permit and lost when it is final. The voivode decides; the starosta named in the decision pays it monthly, in cash at the office or by transfer on request. It is paid within the year's reserve: when the applications with payment that year reach 75 % of the amount planned in the budget act, the minister announces it in the BIP, and from the next day the voivodes stop examining such applications until the budget act secures funds | KP art. 8a–8d, 24a–24c; Dz.U. 2016 poz. 2274 § 3 |
| Now: on hold | The minister's notice of 14.01.2026: the 2026 reserve is 55 000 000 zł, 75 % is 41 250 000 zł, and the applications reached it; applications filed from 15.01.2026 are not examined. A voivode's office: they are still accepted, and will be examined in order of receipt once funds return. No later notice on the benefit in the ministry's list up to 29.09.2026 | MSWiA notice of 14.01.2026; the Świętokrzyskie office (PAGES) |

### Validity

| Topic | Content | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| How long | 10 years; renewal for another 10 on application, not earlier than 6 months before expiry; indefinite if granted at 65 or more | KP art. 17 ust. 1–2a |
| A minor's card | 10 years, but at most until a year after coming of age; renewed for 10 years if, after coming of age and at least 3 months before that end, the holder applies and signs the declaration | KP art. 17 ust. 3–4 |
| Changes, loss | A new card on changed data; a duplicate if lost or destroyed; each for 10 years, with the rules for 65 and over and for a minor's card applied accordingly | KP art. 17 ust. 6–7, 18 ust. 4 |
| Annulment | By decision, among others for conduct degrading Poland or Poles, false data, security, or renunciation | KP art. 20 ust. 1 |
| Lapse by law | On the day the document of Polish citizenship or of the permanent residence permit is issued. The card goes back to the voivode of the place of stay within 14 days of the citizenship document's delivery, or of the permanent residence decision becoming final; for a permit on the card's ground, the residence card is issued after the Karta Polaka is returned | KP art. 20 ust. 1a, 3; UoC art. 229 ust. 7 |

## Inferences (to be confirmed)

- **Waiting for a visit is the main practical barrier.** Offices ration visits by online booking or e-mail registration windows, and some have stopped booking for now (Opole's online booking is suspended; Podlasie takes no registrations and is setting visits for its January 2026 window; PAGES). This is practice, not the act. The settlement benefit is on hold too (FACT, above).
- **A temporary protection holder from Ukraine may apply to a voivode**, as a Ukrainian citizen (REG § 2); protection status itself is not a condition, and REG names no condition of stay in Poland.
- **The card stays in use while the permanent residence case runs:** the application needs a valid card (UoC art. 195 ust. 1 pkt 9); the card is returned within 14 days of the decision becoming final and lapses when the permit's document is issued (KP art. 20 ust. 1a, 3; UoC art. 229 ust. 7). The permanent permit map covers the rest (PS4).
- **The voivode is the one for the place of residence in Poland**, else of stay, since the KPA applies and REG gives no other rule (KP art. 4 ust. 1; KPA art. 21 § 1 pkt 3). A wrong office should forward the application (KPA art. 65 § 1). No text applies art. 21 to this regulation expressly.
- **Only a listed organisation's certificate counts**, from the word "uprawnionych" (KP art. 13 ust. 4); the act does not say so expressly. The list has no organisation in Poland.
- **A benefit decision's appeal has the KPA's 14 days:** the 30 days of KP art. 21 cover the decisions of art. 12, 19 and 20 only (KPA art. 129 § 2, quoted in `research/2026-10-17-unemployment/sources/rynek-pracy-2025-620-extract.txt`).
