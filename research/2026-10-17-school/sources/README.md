# Sources: school and preschool for a child from abroad, doc 19 matter 16

Collected on 17.10.2026 by Claude, for the map `docs/formalka-next/maps/school.md`. Level R1 under doc 19: sources only, with provenance. Production's registry has nothing on schools, so nothing here reaches the app.

**Scope:**
- preschool from 3 years, the preschool year at 6, primary school, and upper-secondary school to 18, for a child who comes from abroad;
- the rules for foreigners in Prawo oświatowe and in the regulation on their education, and what remains of the special act's rules for Ukrainian children.

Not covered:
- nurseries ("żłobek") under the act on care for children under 3;
- adult and post-secondary schools beyond who may attend them;
- universities;
- the details of the exams (OPEN-S3).

## What they say (FACT)

### The duties and the right to a place

| Topic | Content | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Preschool | From the school year of the calendar year the child turns 3 to that of the year it turns 7; exceptionally from 2.5 years | PO art. 31 ust. 1, 3 |
| A right at 3–5 | Children of 3 to 5 have the right to preschool; the municipality must provide it, in a public or a listed non-public preschool on its area | PO art. 31 ust. 6–9 |
| No place | When a preschool cannot take every child of the municipality, the head tells the municipality's head ("wójt, burmistrz, prezydent miasta"), who must name in writing another preschool that can take the child, before the supplementary round | PO art. 31 ust. 10–11 |
| The preschool year at 6 | A child of 6 must do one year of preschool, from the school year of the calendar year it turns 6 | PO art. 31 ust. 4–5 |
| Free hours | A public preschool is free for the time its running body sets, at least 5 hours a day | PO art. 13 ust. 1 pkt 2 |
| Compulsory education | Education is compulsory to 18. The school duty starts with the school year of the calendar year the child turns 7 and lasts to the end of primary school, at most to 18. After primary school, the education duty is met at an upper-secondary school or in vocational training with an employer | PO art. 35, 36 ust. 9 |
| An earlier start | At the parents' request, at 6, if the child went to preschool the year before or has a counselling centre's opinion; such a child is exempt from the preschool year | PO art. 36 ust. 1–3 |
| A deferral | At the parents' request, in the year the child turns 7, by 31 August, with a counselling centre's opinion, by the head of the district public primary school, for one year; the child continues preschool | PO art. 36 ust. 4–7 |
| School abroad or at an embassy | The duties may be met at a school abroad or at a foreign state's mission in Poland; the parents tell the head of the district school by 30 September each year | PO art. 36 ust. 10, 40 ust. 1 pkt 4 |
| Education outside school | At the parents' request, by the head's decision, with the parents' statement on the conditions and their commitment to yearly exams | PO art. 37 ust. 1–2 |
| The parents' duties | Enrol the child, ensure regular attendance, give the conditions to prepare, and the notice of art. 36 ust. 10 | PO art. 40 ust. 1 |
| Who checks | The head of the district public primary school, for the school duty; the municipality, for the education duty; the municipality gives the heads its register of children aged 3–18 | PO art. 41 |
| Not meeting the duty | Unexcused absence on at least 50 % of the days in one month; enforced under the administrative enforcement act | PO art. 42 |
| The way to school | At most 3 km (grades I–IV) or 4 km (grades V–VIII); beyond that the municipality gives free transport with care, or refunds public transport | PO art. 39 ust. 2–3 |
| The way to preschool | For a child of 5, or one in the preschool year, it should not exceed 3 km; beyond that the municipality gives free transport with care, or refunds the child's and the carer's public transport | PO art. 32 ust. 3, 5 |
| The district school | The municipal council sets the districts of its public primary schools by resolution, naming streets in towns; each address belongs to one district | PO art. 39 ust. 5, 5aa |

### A child from abroad

| Topic | Content | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| On what terms | Persons who are not Polish citizens attend public preschools and, when subject to the school duty, public primary schools "na warunkach dotyczących obywateli polskich"; under the education duty, public upper-secondary schools on the same terms, to 18 or the end of that school | PO art. 165 ust. 1–2 |
| When status matters | Only for public adult schools, second-stage vocational schools, post-secondary schools, art schools, institutions, colleges and vocational courses. Free for the 14 listed groups, among them EU citizens and their families, Karta Polaka holders, permanent and EU long-term residents, refugees and protected persons, temporary protection, applicants for international protection, and holders of a card marked "dostęp do rynku pracy" or a work visa; others as scholarship holders or for a fee | PO art. 165 ust. 3–6 |
| Post-secondary schools | A Polish certificate at least at B1 is a condition; the regulation adds it to the documents from 12.09.2026 | PO art. 165 ust. 5a; REG § 6 pkt 4 lit. g |
| Preschool | Admitted on the terms and by the recruitment of Polish citizens; during the school year, the head decides | REG § 3 ust. 1–3 |
| Grade I | The district public primary school of the child's place of residence admits "z urzędu"; another public primary school if it has free places | REG § 4 ust. 1 pkt 1–2 |
| Grades II–VIII | The district school admits "z urzędu" and places the child on the documents; another school on the documents, if it has free places | REG § 4 ust. 2 pkt 1–2 |
| Upper-secondary | A general secondary school on the documents; a technical or first-stage vocational school on the documents and a doctor's certificate; if the school has free places | REG § 6 pkt 1–2 |
| The documents | A certificate or other document of finishing a school or stage abroad; or one confirming attendance and the class finished, with a document of the years of schooling or the parent's written statement of them | REG § 2 pkt 3 |
| Placement | Also by the child's age and the parent's opinion, oral or written | REG § 11 |
| No documents | The child is placed and admitted after an interview, held in a foreign language the child speaks, with an interpreter if needed; not for second-stage vocational or post-secondary schools | REG § 12 |
| A refusal (not the district school) | The parent may ask the head for reasons within 7 days of the refusal; the reasons within 5 days; an appeal to the school's appeal committee within 7 days of the reasons, decided within 7 days; then the administrative court | REG § 13–15 |
| The application | The child's PESEL or, without one, the series and number of a passport or other identity document; the parents' names; the address; the preferred preschools or schools | PO art. 150 ust. 1 pkt 1–5 |
| The notification to the district school | The same identity data and the parents' names, with a statement of where the parents and the child live, made under criminal liability | PO art. 151 |
| Recruitment | Preschools and grade I of other schools by recruitment, on the parent's application; preschool for the municipality's residents first, by criteria; grade I of the district school on notification; during the year the head decides, and district residents are admitted by law | PO art. 130 ust. 1–5, 131, 133 |
| Dates | The running body sets the recruitment dates by the end of January and publishes the criteria then; a child already in a preschool needs a yearly declaration to continue, within 7 days before recruitment starts | PO art. 153 ust. 2, 154 ust. 1 pkt 1, ust. 3 |
| A refusal (recruitment) | Ask the committee for reasons within 3 days of the list; reasons within 3 days; an appeal to the head within 3 days; decided within 3 days; then the administrative court | PO art. 158 ust. 6–9 |

### Help at school

| Topic | Content | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Polish lessons | Free extra Polish for pupils under the school or education duty who do not know Polish well enough, for at most 24 months, organised by the school's running body; at least 2 lesson hours a week | PO art. 165 ust. 7; REG § 17 |
| Polish citizens schooled abroad | The same right, for at most 24 months | PO art. 165 ust. 9 |
| Catch-up classes | For at most 12 months; 1 lesson hour a week per subject; with Polish, at most 5 hours a week in all | PO art. 165 ust. 10; REG § 18–19 |
| A language assistant | A person who speaks the child's language, employed by the head as a teacher's assistant, for at most 12 months | PO art. 165 ust. 8 |
| An intercultural assistant | May be employed; helps with the school and works with the parents | PO art. 165 ust. 8a |
| A preparatory class | The running body may organise it; to the end of the school year in which the pupil is placed there, shortened or extended by at most one year; at most 25 pupils; at least 6 hours of Polish a week; not in special, art, adult, post-secondary or second-stage vocational schools | PO art. 165 ust. 11–14; REG § 16 |
| Another school | Where admitting language learners needs organisational changes, the municipality may name another school, or the education superintendent one | PO art. 166 |
| The language of the country of origin | An embassy, consulate or association may organise it at the school, for at least 7 pupils, at most 5 hours a week | PO art. 165 ust. 15; REG § 20 |
| Finishing primary school | A foreign pupil finishes it with positive final grades from the classes taken in Poland and after taking the eighth-grade exam; otherwise the pupil repeats the last class and takes the exam then | The system of education act art. 44q ust. 1a, 3b (Dz.U. 2026 poz. 203 art. 2 pkt 1, from 5.03.2026) |

### Ukrainian children (the special act)

| Topic | Content | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Most rules ended | The rules on extra branches and classes, transport, inter-school preparatory classes and Polish groups, extra teaching hours, and teacher's assistants without Polish citizenship applied to 31.08.2026 | UA art. 51, 52, 55, 55b ust. 1, 56, 56a, 57 (each "Do dnia 31 sierpnia 2026 r.") |
| Education outside school | Until 31.08.2026 art. 37 of PO did not apply to Ukrainian children who came after 24.02.2022 | UA art. 54 |
| 36 months of Polish | For pupils who started extra Polish in 2022/2023 or 2023/2024, the right lasts at most 36 months | UA art. 55b ust. 1a |
| Removal from the list | The head may, by decision, remove such a Ukrainian child who does not live in the municipality and has at least 50 % unexcused absence over two months. No end date | UA art. 55c |
| An intercultural assistant | To 4.03.2027 one may be employed without Polish citizenship | UA art. 57a |

## Where the pages and the acts differ

| Point | Page | Act |
|---|---|---|
| How long the extra Polish lasts | MEN (08.01.2025): "nie jest ograniczone czasowo" for foreigners; 12 months for Polish citizens | 24 months for both (PO art. 165 ust. 7, 9) |
| Who organises it | MEN (08.01.2025): "gmina lub powiat właściwe ze względu na miejsce zamieszkania" | "organ prowadzący szkołę" (PO art. 165 ust. 7) |

## Inferences (to be confirmed)

- **The school duty applies to a foreign child living in Poland.** PO art. 31 ust. 4 and 35 name no citizenship, and art. 165 ust. 1–2 speak of foreigners "podlegające obowiązkowi szkolnemu". MEN's page of 18.06.2024 says the same of Ukrainian children from 1.09.2024.
- **No PESEL, residence card or registration of address is needed to enrol.** The application and the notification take a passport number when there is no PESEL (PO art. 150 ust. 1 pkt 1, 151 ust. 1 pkt 1), the notification takes a statement of residence (art. 151 ust. 2), and art. 165 ust. 1–2 sets no status condition. What a given school asks for is practice.
- **From 1.09.2026 Ukrainian children fall under the general rules**, from the end dates of UA art. 51–57, except art. 55c, 57a and, perhaps, art. 55b ust. 1a (OPEN-S2).
- **From 1.09.2026 PO art. 37 applies to Ukrainian children**, so education outside school is possible for them on its terms (from the end date of UA art. 54).
