## What they say (FACT)

"In force" means on 17.10.2026, with the amendments applied by their own dates.

### Registering a vehicle

| Topic | Content | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Who registers | The starosta of the owner's place of permanent or temporary residence ("stałego zamieszkania (siedzibę) lub czasowego zamieszkania"), by a decision, with the registration certificate and legalised plates. In a city with county rights, the city office; in Warsaw, the district office (GOV) | PRD art. 73 ust. 1; GOV |
| The deadline | Apply **within 30 days** of: acquiring the vehicle in Poland ("nabycia": for example a purchase or a gift); its release by the customs and tax administration (KAS) when brought from outside the EU; bringing it from an EU state | PRD art. 73aa ust. 1 |
| Special starts and terms | An inherited vehicle: 60 days from the final court ruling or the notarial deed of inheritance (ust. 2, from 24.12.2025). A vehicle bought while temporarily withdrawn from traffic: the term runs from the end of the withdrawal (ust. 2a, from 24.12.2025). A vehicle dealer: 90 days (ust. 3) | PRD art. 73aa ust. 2, 2a, 3 (1734 pkt 5) |
| When there is no duty | The owner who sells the vehicle before the term ends ("dokona zbycia tego pojazdu przed upływem terminów"; since 24.12.2025 for every case of ust. 1, before only for a vehicle acquired in Poland). For a vehicle acquired in Poland, also: a dismantling certificate, an incomplete-vehicle acceptance certificate, a theft report certificate or the discontinuation of the theft inquiry issued before the term ends (ust. 6), or a documented permanent loss of possession (ust. 6a). Vehicles taken over by a commune, county or the State (ust. 5a) | PRD art. 73aa ust. 5a, 6, 6a, 7 (1734 pkt 5) |
| Penalties | 500 zł for a late application; 1 000 zł if it is not filed within 180 days (dealers: 1 000 zł and 2 000 zł); the same penalty for not completing a deficient application by the date set in a summons, unless that date falls before the end of the term; 250 zł for a sale not reported in time. Co-owners are jointly liable | PRD art. 140mb ust. 1–7 (with 1734 pkt 17) |
| How a penalty is imposed | By the starosta, by an administrative decision; paid within 14 days of the day the decision becomes final. The Code of Administrative Procedure's art. 189d–189f do not apply: those are the circumstances an office weighs in setting a penalty, no penalty in force majeure, and waiving a penalty with a warning. For a natural person, only this administrative liability applies when the act is also a petty offence | PRD art. 140n ust. 1, 2, 5, 6, 7; KPA art. 189d–189f |
| Documents | Proof of ownership; the certificate of conformity or approval with the data statement, where required; the technical inspection (a Polish certificate, or the EU registration certificate or another EU document showing a valid inspection); the previous registration certificate (from outside the EU, another registration document may do); the customs clearance for a vehicle from outside the EU registered for the first time; for a passenger car and some others brought from the EU and registered for the first time, the excise document (paid, not due, or exempt) | PRD art. 72 ust. 1, 2a |
| Since 10.06.2026 | No inspection certificate is needed for a vehicle registered in Poland when a valid result is in the central register | PRD art. 72 ust. 2 pkt 9 (1734 pkt 2 lit. a) |
| Temporary registration | Done ex officio when the application is filed; for at most 30 days, once extendable by 14 days "w celu wyjaśnienia spraw związanych z rejestracją". It can be done without the customs and excise documents, which must be filed before the certificate is issued (ust. 2a). It is done "warunkowo" (conditionally) when the owner states that ownership will not change before the certificate is issued (ust. 2b). The owner must pick up the decision and certificate within 30 days of filing (ust. 2f). Plates are returned when it ends, to the office that issued them or, since 10.06.2026, the office that registers the vehicle (ust. 5) | PRD art. 74 ust. 2 pkt 1, 2a, 2b, 2f, 3, 5 (ust. 5 with 1734 pkt 7 lit. f) |
| No temporary registration when the number is kept | When the vehicle was last registered in Poland, the owner asks to keep the number, the office's check of the old certificate's data in the central register is positive, and the old certificate has room for the office's annotation, there is no temporary registration (ust. 2d). Since 10.06.2026 the office issues a temporary registration and permit in that case on the owner's request (ust. 2g); the regulation issues a temporary permit when the conditions are not met or on that request | PRD art. 74 ust. 2d, 2g (1734 pkt 7 lit. b, e); RR § 18 ust. 3 (891 § 1 pkt 4) |
| Without residence in Poland (since 10.06.2026) | A temporary registration to take the vehicle abroad, or for an inspection or repair, is done by the office of the place of purchase or pickup. A trip from the place of purchase or pickup may be registered there too (ust. 1a, earlier) | PRD art. 74 ust. 1a, 1b (1734 pkt 7 lit. a) |
| Keeping the number | A vehicle already registered in Poland, with compliant plates in good condition, may keep its number and plates (this was possible before 10.06.2026). **Since 10.06.2026** the owner attaches a statement under criminal liability that the plates are in good condition and readable and the legalisation sticker is undamaged; without it, or if the plates do not comply, the ordinary registration of ust. 1 applies (with plates from the office). The old plates are no longer re-legalised in that case | PRD art. 73 ust. 1a, 1aa as given by 1734 pkt 4; art. 74 ust. 2e pkt 1 repealed (1734 pkt 7 lit. d) |
| Electronic applications (since 4.07.2026) | The office handles applications for registration, temporary registration or deregistration sent "za pośrednictwem adresu do doręczeń elektronicznych" (an e-Doręczenia address); before, by "środków komunikacji elektronicznej" with the office's electronic inbox. The applicant attaches the documents or their electronic copies, photographs of the plates and the payment confirmation | RR § 20 as given by 891 § 1 pkt 6; § 21 pkt 1 repealed (891 § 1 pkt 7); § 23 |
| Online registration | Comes on **18.01.2027** (a minister's e-service, which the minister "może udostępnić" in mObywatel; or an electronic form filled by the starosta and signed on a signature pad) | PRD art. 73 ust. 1ab–1al (1734 pkt 4 lit. b) |
| Translations | Foreign-language documents with a Polish translation by a sworn translator, a Polish consul, or a sworn translator of the state the vehicle came from or was sold in. No translation is needed of an EU registration certificate (the office may ask for national data without harmonised codes), of EC and other conformity or approval certificates, or of a document under PRD art. 79 ust. 1 pkt 1 or 6 issued in another member state when the office has a translation of a similar document from that state | RR § 5 ust. 1–2; GOV (registration page) |
| Fees | Registration certificate 54 zł; temporary permit 13,50 zł (18,50 zł in some cases); car plates 80 zł, motorcycle 40 zł, moped 30 zł, individual plates 1 000 zł; legalisation stickers 12,50 zł | Fee regulation 2025/553 § 2–4 |
| Insurance | "Przed rejestracją ubezpiecz pojazd (kup ubezpieczenie OC)" | GOV (the act on compulsory insurance is not collected: OPEN-V4) |
| Refusal | A written decision; appeal to the local government appeal board (SKO) | GOV; PRD art. 73 ust. 1e (a refusal decision) |

### After registration

| Topic | Content | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| A sale | The owner of a vehicle registered in Poland notifies the starosta of the owner's place of residence **within 30 days**; online with a trusted profile or a qualified signature, or in person or by post. One co-owner's notice suffices. Since 1.01.2024 a buyer does not notify a purchase; the buyer registers the vehicle | PRD art. 78 ust. 2 pkt 1, 2a, 2b; GOV (sale page, 14.01.2026) |
| Changed data, such as a new address | Notify the starosta within 30 days of a change that requires changing the data in the registration certificate | PRD art. 78 ust. 2 pkt 2; GOV (certificate page: "W terminie 30 dni od zmiany adresu zamieszkania") |

### The driving licence

| Topic | Content | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Valid foreign licences | International licences under the 1949 (Geneva) and 1968 (Vienna) conventions; national or international licences under the 1968 convention; national licences from the EU, EEA and Switzerland; those under a treaty | AVD art. 4 ust. 1 pkt 2 |
| Not recognised | An EU, EEA or Swiss licence that was retained, or whose driving rights were withdrawn for a time or for good, in any of those states | AVD art. 4 ust. 3 |
| Six months | A licence under the 1949 or 1968 convention entitles the holder to drive "odpowiednim pojazdem silnikowym" in Poland "w okresie 6 miesięcy od dnia rozpoczęcia stałego lub czasowego pobytu". A moped is not a "pojazd silnikowy", so it is not covered by these words | AVD art. 5 ust. 4; AVD art. 2 pkt 1; PRD art. 2 pkt 32 |
| An EU, EEA or Swiss licence | Its holder may drive "w okresie ważności prawa jazdy" | AVD art. 5 ust. 6 |
| A treaty licence | Valid "w zakresie określonym w tym prawie jazdy i umowie międzynarodowej" | AVD art. 5a |
| A Polish international licence | "Jest ważne łącznie z krajowym prawem jazdy". The act says nothing of this kind about a foreign international licence (OPEN-V12). GOV: an international licence cannot be exchanged | AVD art. 5 ust. 3; GOV |
| Exchange | On application and for a fee, a valid foreign national licence is exchanged for a Polish one, handing in the foreign document. If it "nie jest zgodne ze wzorem i zakresem danych wymaganych w konwencji" of 1968 (from 17.12.2025), the theory part of the state exam and a certified translation are also required; not for EU, EEA and Swiss licences | AVD art. 14 ust. 1 (1676 art. 4 pkt 8 lit. a) |
| An expired licence | An expired foreign national licence can be exchanged (from 17.12.2025) if its holder meets the residence condition of art. 11 ust. 1 pkt 5, after its data are confirmed by the issuing state and the person brings the documents needed to extend a licence's validity. The same holds for an EU, EEA or Swiss licence (ust. 2a, earlier) | AVD art. 14 ust. 1a (1676 art. 4 pkt 8 lit. b), ust. 2a |
| Residence condition | A licence is issued to a person who "posiada miejsce zamieszkania na terytorium Rzeczypospolitej Polskiej", that is mainly at least 185 days in each calendar year for personal and work ties (or the other cases listed). Before 3.09.2026 the act asked for a statement of it under criminal liability. **From 3.09.2026** the condition itself must be met, and declared under criminal liability with the clause the act gives, at the latest on the day of pickup at the office or of the planned mailing, or on the day of the state exam for categories AM, A1, A2, A, B1, B, B+E or T | AVD art. 11 ust. 1 pkt 5, ust. 1a–1b (1676 art. 4 pkt 7) |
| Confirming the data | For a licence from outside the EU, the office asks the issuing authority or that state's embassy to confirm its data, or uses the issuing state's portal where there is one; the person may bring a confirmation; the embassy's document counts. GOV: it "może trwać nawet kilka tygodni" | LDR § 14 ust. 8–11; GOV |
| No confirmation needed | For the licence of a person granted in Poland refugee status, asylum, subsidiary protection, a humanitarian stay or a tolerated stay; for a licence issued in a state with an armed conflict on its territory, if the driver shows work as a driver for a Polish road transport firm; for a Belarusian citizen's Belarusian licence with the visa or permit listed. Temporary protection is not in the list | LDR § 14 ust. 12 |
| Documents for an exchange (convention or EU licence) | The application, the fee proof, a photograph, a copy of the licence with a translation by a sworn translator or a Polish consul (none for the EU model) | LDR § 14 ust. 1 pkt 5 |
| Residence documents | The applicant makes the statement of art. 11 ust. 1a pkt 2 (since 3.09.2026); "cudzoziemiec dołącza ponadto … kopię karty pobytu, wizy lub innego dokumentu potwierdzającego posiadanie prawa pobytu na terytorium Rzeczypospolitej Polskiej albo zaświadczenie, że studiuje co najmniej od sześciu miesięcy" | LDR § 14 ust. 4 (with 1162 § 1 pkt 4) |
| Refusal | No exchange if the data cannot be confirmed or the rights were withdrawn | AVD art. 14 ust. 2e |
| Probation after an exchange (from 3.03.2026) | For category B obtained by an exchange under art. 14, the probation period of art. 91 ust. 1 (2 years for an adult) applies when the Polish licence is issued sooner than that after the foreign one, counted from the foreign licence's issue; when that day cannot be established, from the Polish licence's issue. During it, for example, the alcohol limit is 0,0 ‰ | AVD art. 91 ust. 1, 3 pkt 3, 4 (1676 art. 4 pkt 20) |
| Fee | 100 zł for a licence | Fee regulation 2025/953 § 1 |
| GOV's list for a foreigner | A passport, a residence card or another listed document, and "dokument, który potwierdzi adres zameldowania"; "Jeśli nie masz meldunku – zapytaj w urzędzie gminy, co zrobić" | GOV (licence page, 2023) |

## Inferences (to be confirmed)

- **A foreigner needs a place of residence in Poland to register a vehicle,** because the competent starosta is the one of permanent or temporary residence (PRD art. 73 ust. 1). The act names the office; it does not state residence as a condition. Without residence, only a temporary registration to take the vehicle abroad or for an inspection or repair is possible at the office of purchase or pickup (art. 74 ust. 1b, since 10.06.2026), and a trip from there (ust. 1a).
- **The six months for a convention licence run from the start of stay,** not from entry or a permit; what counts as that start is OPEN-V5.
- **Exchanging a licence needs the residence condition of AVD art. 11 ust. 1 pkt 5,** since a licence is issued only to such a person (art. 11 ust. 1); for an expired licence the act says so (art. 14 ust. 1a, 2a).
- **The offices may still ask for an address registration,** as the gov.pl licence page does, though the acts speak of residence and the regulation of right-of-stay documents (OPEN-V1).
