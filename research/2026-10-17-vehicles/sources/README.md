## What they say (FACT)

| Topic | Content | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Who registers | The starosta of the owner's place of permanent or temporary residence ("stałego zamieszkania … lub czasowego zamieszkania"), by a decision, with the registration certificate and legalised plates | PRD art. 73 ust. 1 |
| Keeping the plates | For a vehicle already registered in Poland, the owner may ask to keep the number and the plates, if they meet the rules and are readable | PRD art. 73 ust. 1a |
| The deadline | Apply for registration **within 30 days** of buying the vehicle in Poland, of its release by the customs and tax administration (KAS) when brought from outside the EU, or of bringing it from an EU state. Inherited: 60 days from the court ruling or the notarial deed (from 24.12.2025) | PRD art. 73aa ust. 1; ust. 2 as changed by Dz.U. 2025 poz. 1734 |
| The penalty | 500 zł for a late application; 1 000 zł if it is not filed within 180 days. 250 zł for not reporting a sale on time. The starosta imposes them by decision | PRD art. 140mb ust. 1, 5, 6; art. 140n |
| A sale, changed data | The owner of a vehicle registered in Poland notifies the starosta **within 30 days** of selling it, or of a change that requires changing the data in the registration certificate | PRD art. 78 ust. 2, 2a |
| A foreign licence | Licences issued abroad that are valid in Poland: international licences under the 1949 and 1968 conventions, national licences under the 1968 convention, national licences from the EU, EEA and Switzerland, and those under a treaty | AVD art. 4 ust. 1 pkt 2 |
| Six months | A licence under the 1949 or 1968 convention entitles its holder to drive in Poland **for 6 months from the start of permanent or temporary stay** ("w okresie 6 miesięcy od dnia rozpoczęcia stałego lub czasowego pobytu") | AVD art. 5 ust. 4 |
| Exchange | On application and for a fee, a valid foreign national licence can be exchanged for a Polish one, handing in the foreign document. If it does not follow the 1968 convention's model, the theory part of the state exam and a certified translation are also required (not for EU, EEA and Swiss licences). From 17.12.2025 an expired foreign licence can be exchanged after its data are confirmed | AVD art. 14 ust. 1, 1a, as changed by Dz.U. 2025 poz. 1676 |

## Inferences (to be confirmed)

- **A licence from the EU, the EEA or Switzerland has no six-month limit.** Art. 5 ust. 4 names only the convention licences; art. 4 ust. 1 pkt 2 lit. c lists EU, EEA and Swiss licences without a period.
- **Registering a vehicle needs a place of residence in Poland,** because the competent starosta is the one of permanent or temporary residence. Whether offices ask for an address registration (zameldowanie) to prove it is OPEN-V1.
