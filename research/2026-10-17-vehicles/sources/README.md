## What they say (FACT)

"In force" means on 17.10.2026, with the amendments applied by their own dates.

### Registering a vehicle

| Topic | Content | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Who registers | The starosta of the owner's place of permanent or temporary residence ("stałego zamieszkania (siedzibę) lub czasowego zamieszkania"), by a decision, with the registration certificate and legalised plates. In a city with county rights, the city office; in Warsaw, the district office (GOV) | PRD art. 73 ust. 1; GOV |
| The deadline | Apply **within 30 days** of: buying the vehicle in Poland; its release by the customs and tax administration (KAS) when brought from outside the EU; bringing it from an EU state. An inherited vehicle: 60 days from the court's ruling or the notarial deed (from 24.12.2025) | PRD art. 73aa ust. 1; ust. 2 as changed by 1734 |
| Penalties | 500 zł for a late application; 1 000 zł if it is not filed within 180 days; 250 zł for a sale not reported in time. The starosta imposes them by decision | PRD art. 140mb ust. 1, 5, 6 (with 1734 pkt 17); art. 140n |
| Documents | Proof of ownership; the certificate of conformity or approval with the data statement, where required; the technical inspection (a Polish certificate, or the EU registration certificate or another EU document showing a valid inspection); the previous registration certificate (from outside the EU, another registration document may do); the customs clearance for a vehicle from outside the EU registered for the first time; for a passenger car and some others brought from the EU and registered for the first time, the excise document (paid, not due, or exempt) | PRD art. 72 ust. 1, 2a |
| Since 10.06.2026 | No inspection certificate is needed for a vehicle registered in Poland when a valid result is in the central register | PRD art. 72 ust. 2 pkt 9 (1734 pkt 2 lit. a) |
| Temporary registration | Done ex officio when the application is filed; for at most 30 days, once extendable by 14 days to clear up the case. It can be done without the customs and excise documents, which must be filed before the certificate is issued. Plates are returned when it ends | PRD art. 74 ust. 2 pkt 1, 2a, 3, 5 |
| Without residence in Poland (since 10.06.2026) | A temporary registration to take the vehicle abroad, or for an inspection or repair, is done by the office of the place of purchase or pickup | PRD art. 74 ust. 1b (1734 pkt 7 lit. a) |
| Keeping the plates (since 10.06.2026) | For a vehicle registered in Poland with compliant plates, the owner may keep the number and plates, **with a statement under criminal liability** that the plates are in good condition and readable and the legalisation sticker is undamaged | PRD art. 73 ust. 1a, 1aa as given by 1734 pkt 4 |
| Online registration | Comes on **18.01.2027** (a minister's e-service or mObywatel; or an electronic form filled by the starosta and signed on a signature pad) | PRD art. 73 ust. 1ab–1al (1734 pkt 4 lit. b) |
| Translations | Foreign-language documents with a Polish translation by a sworn translator or a Polish consul; the EU registration certificate needs none for its harmonised codes | GOV (registration page) |
| Fees | Registration certificate 54 zł; temporary permit 13,50 zł (18,50 zł in some cases); car plates 80 zł, motorcycle 40 zł, moped 30 zł, individual plates 1 000 zł; legalisation stickers 12,50 zł | Fee regulation 2025/553 § 2–4 |
| Insurance | "Przed rejestracją ubezpiecz pojazd (kup ubezpieczenie OC)" | GOV (the act on compulsory insurance is not collected: OPEN-V4) |
| Refusal | A written decision; appeal to the local government appeal board (SKO) | GOV |

### After registration

| Topic | Content | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| A sale | The owner of a vehicle registered in Poland notifies the starosta of the owner's place of residence **within 30 days**; online with a trusted profile or a qualified signature, or in person or by post. One co-owner's notice suffices. Since 1.01.2024 a buyer does not notify a purchase; the buyer registers the vehicle | PRD art. 78 ust. 2 pkt 1, 2a, 2b; GOV (sale page, 14.01.2026) |
| Changed data, such as a new address | Notify the starosta within 30 days of a change that requires changing the data in the registration certificate | PRD art. 78 ust. 2 pkt 2; GOV (certificate page: "W terminie 30 dni od zmiany adresu zamieszkania") |

### The driving licence

| Topic | Content | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Valid foreign licences | International licences under the 1949 (Geneva) and 1968 (Vienna) conventions; national or international licences under the 1968 convention; national licences from the EU, EEA and Switzerland; those under a treaty | AVD art. 4 ust. 1 pkt 2 |
| Six months | A licence under the 1949 or 1968 convention entitles the holder to drive in Poland "w okresie 6 miesięcy od dnia rozpoczęcia stałego lub czasowego pobytu" | AVD art. 5 ust. 4 |
| An international licence | "Jest ważne łącznie z krajowym prawem jazdy" (for a Polish international licence); GOV: an international licence cannot be exchanged | AVD art. 5 ust. 3; GOV |
| Exchange | On application and for a fee, a valid foreign national licence is exchanged for a Polish one, handing in the foreign document. If it does not follow the 1968 convention's model (from 17.12.2025), the theory part of the state exam and a certified translation are also required; not for EU, EEA and Swiss licences. An expired foreign licence can be exchanged after its data are confirmed (from 17.12.2025) | AVD art. 14 ust. 1, 1a (1676 art. 4 pkt 8) |
| Residence condition | A licence is issued to a person who "posiada miejsce zamieszkania na terytorium Rzeczypospolitej Polskiej", that is mainly at least 185 days in each calendar year for personal and work ties (or the other cases listed). From 3.09.2026 the condition must be met, and declared under criminal liability, at the latest on the day of pickup, mailing, or the state exam | AVD art. 11 ust. 1 pkt 5, ust. 1a–1b (1676 art. 4 pkt 7) |
| Confirming the data | For a licence from outside the EU, the office asks the issuing authority or that state's embassy to confirm its data; the person may bring a confirmation; the embassy's document counts. GOV: it "może trwać nawet kilka tygodni" | Regulation 2025/1073 § 14 ust. 8–11; GOV |
| Documents for an exchange (convention or EU licence) | The application, the fee proof, a photograph, a copy of the licence with a translation by a sworn translator or a Polish consul (none for the EU model) | Regulation 2025/1073 § 14 ust. 1 pkt 5 |
| Refusal | No exchange if the data cannot be confirmed or the rights were withdrawn | AVD art. 14 ust. 2e |
| Fee | 100 zł for a licence | Fee regulation 2025/953 § 1 |
| GOV's list for a foreigner | A passport, a residence card or another listed document, and "dokument, który potwierdzi adres zameldowania"; "Jeśli nie masz meldunku – zapytaj w urzędzie gminy, co zrobić" | GOV (licence page, 2023) |

## Inferences (to be confirmed)

- **A foreigner needs a place of residence in Poland to register a vehicle,** because the competent starosta is the one of permanent or temporary residence (PRD art. 73 ust. 1). Without it, only a temporary registration to take the vehicle abroad is possible (art. 74 ust. 1b, since 10.06.2026).
- **The six months for a convention licence run from the start of stay,** not from entry or a permit; what counts as that start is OPEN-V5.
- **A licence from the EU, EEA or Switzerland has no six-month limit** (AVD art. 5 ust. 4 names only the convention licences).
- **Exchanging a licence needs the residence condition of AVD art. 11 ust. 1 pkt 5,** since a licence is issued only to such a person (art. 11 ust. 1); the gov.pl page asks for 185 days.
- **The address registration (wave 1) is asked for both procedures in practice** (GOV's licence page names "adres zameldowania"), though the acts speak of residence, not registration (OPEN-V1).
