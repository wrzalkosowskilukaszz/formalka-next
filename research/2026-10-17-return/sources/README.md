# Sources: a return decision or voluntary return, doc 19 matter 26

Collected on 17.10.2026 by Claude, for the map `docs/formalka-next/maps/return-decision.md`. Level R1 under doc 19: sources only, with provenance. Doc 19 sets this matter as a handoff to legal aid only, never a plan. Production's registry has no return procedure, so nothing here reaches it.

**Scope:**
- what a return decision (decyzja o zobowiązaniu cudzoziemca do powrotu) is, who issues it, and the time limits it sets: the appeal, the voluntary departure period;
- the duty to leave after a final refusal of a residence permit or protection;
- help with voluntary return, as a route the person may ask for;
- free legal aid, the handoff.

Not covered:
- the grounds for a return decision, the cases where none is issued or it is not executed (UoC art. 302 ust. 1 and 4–6, 303, 330), and the withdrawal of an entry ban (art. 320): these are for a lawyer;
- detention, guarded centres and costs (art. 336–347, 394–427);
- international protection (asylum) and its own appeal, beyond its link to voluntary return;
- EU citizens' family members and Turkish workers under decision 1/80, to whom the EU citizens act applies instead (art. 308–308b);
- the EU Pact on Migration and Asylum, the Return Directive 2008/115/EC and the Schengen Information System: not read.

**Not yet checked by the separate checking agent** (`.claude/agents/official-fact-checker.md`): that check comes before anything here is relied on.

## What they say (FACT)

### The duty to leave without a return decision

| Topic | Content | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| After a final refusal | A foreigner must leave within 30 days of the day a refusal (to extend a visa, or of a temporary, permanent or EU long-term residence permit), a discontinuance, or a withdrawal of such a permit becomes final; also after a refusal of protection or a withdrawal of a humanitarian stay. When a higher body decides, from its delivery | UoC art. 299 ust. 6 pkt 1 |
| After temporary protection | From 5.03.2026, within 30 days of the end of temporary protection's maximum term under the EU Council's decision, or of the term set under the protection act's art. 107 ust. 1 | UoC art. 299 ust. 6a (Dz.U. 2026 poz. 203 art. 12 pkt 9, art. 54) |
| The stay in that time | Counts as legal, unless the application was filed after the legal stay had ended | UoC art. 299 ust. 7 (as changed from 5.03.2026) |
| Where it does not apply | With a valid document allowing the stay, or where a law treats the stay as legal; nor where return proceedings had begun, or a return decision had been issued, before the refusal (and in three cases for a refusal of protection) | UoC art. 299 ust. 8–9 |
| The Border Guard told | The voivode, the Head of the Office or the Refugee Board tells the Border Guard of the final decision; the Border Guard then checks whether to issue a return decision | UoC art. 299 ust. 10–11 |
| No decision in that time on some grounds | No return decision on the grounds of art. 302 ust. 1 pkt 1–3 during the 30 days of art. 299 ust. 6, nor on pkt 2–3 while the person holds a valid document or the stay counts as legal | UoC art. 302 ust. 2–3 |

### The return decision

| Topic | Content | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Who issues it | The commander of a Border Guard unit (oddział) or post (placówka): on its own finding, or at the request of the voivode, the defence minister, the ABW, the Intelligence Agency, the National Revenue Administration or the Police | UoC art. 310 ust. 1 |
| Protection | The Border Guard tells the person that they may apply for international protection; the return case is suspended once protection proceedings or proceedings for an art. 176 permit (victims of trafficking) begin, but not for a subsequent application | UoC art. 304–305 |
| Language | An interpreter if the person does not speak Polish well enough; the decision's legal basis, outcome and appeal instruction are translated, orally or in writing, into a language the person understands | UoC art. 327 |
| Help | The Border Guard tells the person of non-governmental organisations that help foreigners, legal help included | UoC art. 328 |
| Voluntary departure | The decision sets a period for voluntary departure: 8 to 30 days from its delivery. None is set when there is a probability of absconding, or for defence, state security or public order; such a decision on those security grounds is enforced at once | UoC art. 315 ust. 1–2, 5 |
| A longer period | The issuing body may set a longer period, or extend it once later on the person's request, for a duty to appear before a Polish authority, Poland's interest, or an exceptional personal situation (the length of stay, family and social ties, a child's schooling); at most 1 year | UoC art. 316 |
| The entry ban | The decision bans re-entry to Poland and the other Schengen states and sets the ban's period: from 6 months to 10 years, by the decision's ground | UoC art. 318 ust. 1, 319 ust. 1 |
| When it becomes final | A national visa is annulled, a temporary residence permit and a work permit expire, and a work declaration's entry is annulled | UoC art. 307 ust. 1 |
| Delivery | Letters may also be sent by electronic means or by fax and delivered in that form | UoC art. 322 |
| The appeal | The higher body is the Border Guard Commander-in-Chief; the appeal is filed within 7 days of the decision's delivery | UoC art. 321 |
| Through whom | The appeal goes to the appeal body through the body that issued the decision | KPA art. 129 § 1 |
| Its content | An appeal needs no detailed reasons; it is enough that it shows the person is not satisfied with the decision | KPA art. 128 |
| Enforcement | Before the appeal time ends the decision is not enforced, and an appeal filed in time suspends it, except where the decision is immediately enforceable | KPA art. 130 § 1–3; UoC art. 315 ust. 5 |
| A waiver | A person who waives the appeal makes the decision final on the day the office receives the waiver | KPA art. 127a |
| Counting | The day of delivery is not counted; a time limit ending on a Saturday or a public holiday ends on the next working day; a letter posted at a Polish post office of the designated operator, or sent to the office's e-delivery address with a receipt, before the end, is in time | KPA art. 57 § 1, 4–5 |
| A missed time limit | It is restored on request if the person makes it plausible that the delay was not their fault; the request is filed within 7 days of the cause ending, together with the appeal itself | KPA art. 58 § 1–2 |
| Other rules | Turkish workers under decision 1/80 of the EEC–Turkey Association Council, and family members of EU, EEA, Swiss, Polish or UK citizens who no longer live with them, fall under the EU citizens act's chapter 5, not these rules | UoC art. 308–308b |

### Help with voluntary return

| Topic | Content | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Who organises it | The Border Guard Commander-in-Chief, on the person's request | UoC art. 334 ust. 1, 3 |
| Who may get it | Among others: a person with a return decision that sets a voluntary departure period; a person refused protection, or whose protection case was discontinued or left unexamined; a holder of the art. 170 certificate or an art. 176 permit (victims of trafficking); from 5.03.2026, a person staying after temporary protection ends | UoC art. 334 ust. 2 (pkt 7: Dz.U. 2026 poz. 203 art. 12 pkt 10) |
| Through whom | A Border Guard unit or post commander; the Head of the Office for a person refused protection who receives the Office's social help; or the entity the Commander-in-Chief has commissioned to organise voluntary returns | UoC art. 334 ust. 4, 8 |
| By when | Before the voluntary departure period in the return decision ends; 7 days before the end of the 30 days after a refusal of protection; 5 days after a discontinuance of protection becomes final, or after the notice that the application is left unexamined. A late request is left unexamined | UoC art. 334 ust. 5–6 |
| How often | Not again within 2 years of leaving with this help, except for the persons of ust. 2 pkt 2 and the art. 170 and 176 holders | UoC art. 334 ust. 10–11 |
| The decision | Within 5 days of the request; no complaint lies against it. Letters as in art. 322, and from 1.01.2026 also to the e-mail address given in the request (delivered on the day it was sent) | UoC art. 334a ust. 1; ust. 2 as replaced by Dz.U. 2025 poz. 1794 art. 1 pkt 57, art. 19 |
| What it covers | Travel; the fees for a travel document, visas and permits; food before and during the journey; accommodation before it; medical care; the organising entity's costs; other costs of a safe and humane return | UoC art. 335 ust. 1 |
| Being told | The body that issues a return decision, a refusal of protection or a discontinuance tells the person of this help | UoC art. 335 ust. 3 |

### Free legal aid

| Topic | Content | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Who | A natural person who cannot bear the cost of paid legal help, on a statement saying so: in writing at a point, orally when the help is remote | NPP art. 3 ust. 1, 4 ust. 1–2 |
| What | Information on the law and the person's rights and duties, also in a pending administrative or administrative court case; a way to solve the problem; a draft letter (not procedural letters in pending court cases, nor letters in a pending administrative court case); a draft request for exemption from court costs or for an appointed lawyer | NPP art. 3 ust. 1 |
| Where and when | In a point or remotely; by appointment, in the order of booking, with another order allowed for important reasons, and pregnant women out of turn; booking by the phone number the starosta gives, and electronically where the starosta allows | NPP art. 8 ust. 3a–5 |

## Inferences (to be confirmed)

- **The appeal time is short:** 7 days (UoC art. 321 ust. 2), half the KPA's usual 14 (art. 129 § 2). Booking free legal aid "in the order of booking" may not fit it; the act allows another order "z ważnych powodów" (NPP art. 8 ust. 4), and whether a running 7-day limit counts is the point's decision (OPEN-D4).
- **The voluntary departure period and the appeal run from the same delivery** (UoC art. 315 ust. 1, 321 ust. 2), so the first days after delivery decide both.
- **Signing a waiver of the appeal ends it** (KPA art. 127a); the app should never present a waiver as a formality.
- **Free legal aid can draft the appeal to the Border Guard Commander-in-Chief,** since it is a letter in an administrative case, not in a court case (NPP art. 3 ust. 1 pkt 3).
- **The person's language decides nothing here:** a return decision follows the person's stay and status (UoC art. 302), never their language or nationality (product rule 4).
