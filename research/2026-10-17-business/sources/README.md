# Sources: business registration (CEIDG) by a foreigner, doc 19 matter 20

Collected on 17.10.2026 by Claude, for the map `docs/formalka-next/maps/business.md`. Level R1 under doc 19: sources only, with provenance. Production's registry has no business registration procedure (no CEIDG text in `reference/`), so nothing here reaches it.

**Scope:**
- who may run a sole proprietorship (jednoosobowa działalność gospodarcza, JDG) on the same terms as Polish citizens, and who may use only some companies;
- registering in CEIDG: online or on paper, the status document, the PESEL from 1.11.2026, and the end of paper filing for a new business after 31.10.2026;
- unregistered activity (działalność nierejestrowana) and foreigners;
- Ukrainian citizens after the special act's art. 23 was repealed (5.03.2026);
- deletion when the right ends;
- what the application does on its own (REGON, the tax identification, the ZUS notification) and the 6 months without social insurance.

Not covered:
- taxes, the choice of tax form and VAT (matter 12, which the owner set aside on 17.10.2026);
- ZUS contributions beyond PP art. 18;
- registering a company in KRS (only who may use one);
- branches, representative offices, and cross-border services by EU providers (FE act art. 5 and after);
- licensed and regulated activities;
- the temporary residence permit for business (UoC art. 142): `research/2026-10-16-residence/sources/`.

## What they say (FACT)

### Who may run a business on the same terms as Polish citizens

|---|---|---|
| From member states | Foreign persons from member states: the EU states other than Poland, and the EFTA states that are parties to the EEA agreement | FE art. 3 pkt 6, 4 ust. 1 |
| With a residence title | Citizens of other states who hold in Poland: a permanent residence permit; an EU long-term resident permit; a temporary permit granted under UoC art. 127, 137a, 144, 151 ust. 1, 159 ust. 1 or 186 ust. 1 pkt 3, 4 and 7; refugee status; subsidiary protection; a stay for humanitarian reasons or a tolerated stay; a temporary permit while married to a Polish citizen living in Poland; a temporary permit for business granted to continue a business already entered in CEIDG | FE art. 4 ust. 2 pkt 1 |

| Temporary protection | Those who enjoy temporary protection in Poland | FE art. 4 ust. 2 pkt 2 |
| The Karta Polaka | A valid card | FE art. 4 ust. 2 pkt 3 |
| EU citizens' family | Family members (EU entry act art. 2 pkt 4) who join, or stay with, citizens of the member states | FE art. 4 ust. 2 pkt 4 |
| While an application runs | A stay under UoC art. 108 ust. 1 pkt 2, 206 ust. 1 pkt 2 or 222a ust. 1 pkt 2, if right before the application the person was entitled under pkt 1 lit. c or g. In this wording from 27.04.2026 (footnote 3) | FE art. 4 ust. 2 pkt 5 |
| What that stay needs | The application filed in time (temporary permit) or during a legal stay (permanent residence, EU long-term resident), with no formal gaps or with them filled in time; no such stay while the case is suspended at the person's own request | UoC art. 108 ust. 1–2, 206 ust. 1–2, 222a ust. 1 |
| The proof of the application | From 27.04.2026 the voivode issues a certificate (zaświadczenie) confirming the application, delivered through MOS (or handed over at the personal attendance), instead of a stamp in the passport | UoC art. 108 ust. 1 pkt 1, 206 ust. 1 pkt 1, 222a ust. 1 pkt 1 (Dz.U. 2025 poz. 1794 art. 1 pkt 14, 32, 39; art. 19 pkt 2; M.P. 2026 poz. 370) |

| Support programmes | Participants of programmes for foreigners that public bodies run or co-run; the Chancellery of the Prime Minister lists them in its BIP | FE art. 4 ust. 2 pkt 6, ust. 8 |

| A treaty person's family | A family member (UoC art. 159 ust. 3) with a temporary permit, of a foreign person whom the international agreements of ust. 3 cover: on the same terms as that person | FE art. 4 ust. 4 |
| A family reunification permit | A family member (UoC art. 159 ust. 3) with a temporary permit for joining family: "w takim samym zakresie jak cudzoziemcy, którzy posiadają zezwolenie na pobyt czasowy i wykonują działalność gospodarczą na podstawie wpisu do ewidencji działalności gospodarczej dokonanego na zasadzie wzajemności" | FE art. 4 ust. 5 |
| Foreign requirements | The competent body recognises what an entrepreneur from a member state met there, and its insurance and guarantees | FE art. 4 ust. 6–7 |

### Registering in CEIDG

| Topic | Content | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Online | The electronic form on the CEIDG site, signed with a qualified, trusted or personal signature or another way CEIDG accepts | CEIDG art. 3 ust. 2, 10 ust. 7 |
| On paper (a new business: until 31.10.2026) | In the same layout as the form: at any municipality, in person, with the identity confirmed (without a PESEL: a passport or another document of identity and citizenship), signed by hand; or by registered post to any municipality, with the signature notarised. The municipality sends it on by the next working day | CEIDG art. 8 ust. 2–4, 10 ust. 8–9 |
| The status document | Persons of FE art. 4 ust. 2: on paper, the original of the document confirming their current status, or a copy certified by a notary or an office; online, the document in electronic form with a qualified, trusted or personal signature | CEIDG art. 8 ust. 8 |
| From 1.11.2026: a PESEL | Foreign persons who apply for an entry in CEIDG must have a PESEL, or, if none was assigned, the unique identifier an EU member state assigns for cross-border identification (Regulation 2015/1501). The entry then holds the PESEL or that identifier, not the date of birth | FE art. 4 ust. 9; CEIDG art. 5 ust. 1 pkt 1 as changed (Dz.U. 2026 poz. 507 art. 1 pkt 4 lit. a tiret 1); dates in art. 31 pkt 2 |
| From 1.11.2026: online only for a new business | From 1.11.2026 to 31.10.2028 the application for an entry with information that the business is started is filed only on the electronic form, except one that names a minor's legal representative (that one only on paper, until 31.10.2028). One filed at a municipality or posted to one after 31.10.2026, and not yet entered, is left unexamined | Dz.U. 2026 poz. 507 art. 15 ust. 1, 16 |
| Applications in progress | Filed and not entered before 14.10.2026: the old wording of the CEIDG act (art. 13). Paper ones filed by 31.10.2026 and not entered: the new wording (art. 14 ust. 1). A foreign person's (FE art. 4 ust. 1, 2, 4 and 5) filed before 1.11.2026 and not entered by then: the old wording (art. 29) | Dz.U. 2026 poz. 507 art. 13, 14 ust. 1, 29 |
| Existing entries without a PESEL | A foreign person's entry that holds neither a PESEL nor the EU identifier must be completed by 1.06.2030; after that CEIDG deletes it and tells the tax, statistics and social insurance bodies | Dz.U. 2026 poz. 507 art. 22 (from 1.11.2026) |
| What "wniosek o wpis" covers | From 14.10.2026: an entry with information that the business is started; a change; a suspension; a resumption; an entry with information that it is not started; deletion | CEIDG art. 3 ust. 2a (Dz.U. 2026 poz. 507 art. 1 pkt 2 lit. b) |
| Statements | With the application, under criminal liability: no ban on the activity; a legal title to the addresses entered. From 14.10.2026, for a minor, also the representative's statements (ust. 1a–1b), covered by ust. 2 | CEIDG art. 7 ust. 1–2; Dz.U. 2026 poz. 507 art. 1 pkt 6 |
| The e-delivery address | Through CEIDG the applicant gives the data to create an e-delivery address, or to enter a qualified provider's address, unless a current one is already in the address base; from 14.10.2026 the duty of the e-delivery act art. 152 ust. 1 covers an application "z informacją o podjęciu działalności gospodarczej" | CEIDG art. 6a; e-delivery act art. 152 ust. 1; Dz.U. 2026 poz. 507 art. 10 |
| A paper application of an FE art. 4 ust. 2 person | Until 31.10.2028 the municipality's staff who convert it to electronic form attach a scan of the document confirming the person's current status | CEIDG art. 66b (Dz.U. 2026 poz. 507 art. 1 pkt 54, from 14.10.2026) |
| The data | Among them: the PESEL "o ile taki posiada" (until 31.10.2026), and the citizenship | CEIDG art. 5 ust. 1 pkt 1, 5 |
| When it is entered | When the data are placed in CEIDG, at the latest on the working day after the application arrives, except for an incorrect one (art. 10 ust. 5–6) | CEIDG art. 6 ust. 1 |
| An incorrect application | Among the grounds: one filed by a person not entitled, including a foreign person without the right to run a business in Poland under other provisions. Online, the system says so at once; on paper, the municipality names the faults and asks for a correction within 7 working days, else the application is left unexamined | CEIDG art. 10 ust. 2 pkt 8, ust. 5–6 |
| What else it does | It always includes the REGON entry, the tax identification notification and the contribution payer's ZUS notification (or the farmers' KRUS statement). CEIDG sends the data to the tax office by the working day after the entry, and, once the NIP is assigned, to GUS and to ZUS or KRUS | CEIDG art. 6 ust. 3, 12 ust. 1 |
| What may be attached | The registration for social and health insurance (and family members'), the VAT registration, the choice of income tax form and the notice on keeping accounting books: "przedsiębiorca może dołączyć"; once attached they become part of the application | CEIDG art. 6 ust. 4, 9 |
| A PESEL on application | Filed on paper, signed by hand, at any municipality; a foreigner who is not an EU, EFTA-EEA or Swiss citizen, a Withdrawal Agreement UK citizen, or such a person's family member, must come in person (from 1.01.2026), except children born in Poland | UEL art. 18 ust. 1–1a |
| Free | The application is free of charge | CEIDG art. 13 |
| The start | A business may start on the day the application is filed (from 14.10.2026: "z informacją o podjęciu działalności gospodarczej"), unless special provisions say otherwise | PP art. 17 ust. 1; Dz.U. 2026 poz. 507 art. 7 |
| Changes | A change of the entry within 7 days of a change of the data of art. 5 ust. 1 pkt 1 (except the PESEL), 2 and 5–8, among them the address; deletion when the business ends | CEIDG art. 15 ust. 1 |
| Not started | After an entry, a natural person may apply for an entry with information that the business is not started. Art. 8 ust. 8 (the status document) and art. 10 ust. 2 pkt 3–6 do not apply to it; pkt 8 does | CEIDG art. 14 |

### Unregistered activity

| Topic | Content | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| What it is | Not a business: a natural person's activity whose due revenue does not exceed, in any quarter, 225 % of the minimum wage, and who has not run a business in the last 60 months. Quarterly from 1.01.2026 (footnotes 2–3); before, 75 % in any month | PP art. 5 ust. 1 |
| In 2026 | The minimum wage is 4806 zł, so the quarterly limit is 10 813,50 zł (225 % of 4806 zł, a computation). Page 00115 gives the same amount | Dz.U. 2025 poz. 1242 § 1 |
| In 2027 | The minimum wage is 4950 zł from 1.01.2027, so the quarterly limit is 11 137,50 zł (225 % of 4950 zł, a computation) | Dz.U. 2026 poz. 1213 § 1 |
| Over the limit | It becomes a business from the day the limit is exceeded, and the application is due within 7 days | PP art. 5 ust. 3–4 |
| Not for | A civil-law partnership (ust. 5); an activity that needs a licence, a permit or a regulated-activity entry (art. 44 ust. 3); foreign persons other than those of FE art. 4 ust. 1–2 (ust. 7) | PP art. 5 ust. 5, 7, 44 ust. 3 |

### Ukrainian citizens

|---|---|---|

### The first months

| Topic | Content | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| No social insurance for 6 months | A natural person who starts a business for the first time, or again at least 60 months after its last suspension or end, and does not do it for a former employer for whom, in the current or the previous calendar year, they did the same work under an employment contract: no compulsory social insurance for 6 months from the start. ## Inferences (to be confirmed)

- **From 1.11.2026 a non-EU foreigner without a PESEL cannot register a new JDG:** online needs the PESEL or the EU identifier (FE art. 4 ust. 9), and paper filing ends (Dz.U. 2026 poz. 507 art. 15–16). Whether a non-EU citizen can hold the EU identifier is OPEN-B7.
- **The PESEL for this is available on application:** a person obliged by other provisions to have one is given it on application (UEL art. 7 ust. 2, 16 ust. 2), and FE art. 4 ust. 9 is such an obligation. The application is on paper at any municipality, and a non-EU foreigner comes in person (UEL art. 18 ust. 1–1a, FACT). A foreigner who lives in Poland and registers gets one ex officio (UEL art. 7 ust. 1 pkt 3, 16 ust. 1; `research/2026-10-16-service/sources/`).
- **A temporary permit for business (UoC art. 142) does not by itself allow a JDG.** Art. 4 ust. 2 pkt 1 lit. c does not name art. 142; only lit. h does, for a permit to continue a business already in CEIDG. Such a holder, without another title, uses a company (art. 4 ust. 3).
- **A permit for work does not allow a JDG** (for example "pobyt czasowy i praca", UoC art. 114): art. 4 ust. 2 does not name it, unless the holder is married to a Polish citizen living in Poland (lit. g). Page 001466 says so for Ukrainian citizens.
- **From 1.11.2026 FE art. 4 ust. 9 may reach a foreign entrepreneur's change or suspension too,** since "wniosek o wpis do CEIDG" covers them (CEIDG art. 3 ust. 2a); Dz.U. 2026 poz. 507 art. 22 gives existing entries until 1.06.2030 (OPEN-B4).
- **An entry "not started" (CEIDG art. 14) is no way round FE art. 4:** it skips the status document, but the check of a person not entitled (art. 10 ust. 2 pkt 8) still applies.
- **When the title ends** (temporary protection ends, a permit expires without art. 4 ust. 2 pkt 5), the minister deletes the entry by decision (CEIDG art. 29 ust. 1 pkt 2), and the person may no longer run the business as a JDG. For a CUKR card holder, art. 29 ust. 1 pkt 2 does not name the special act's art. 42w, so the ground is OPEN-B8.
- **31.10.2026 is a Saturday** (a computation): filing in person that day depends on the municipality's hours; a registered letter sent to a municipality on 31.10.2026 still counts, since Dz.U. 2026 poz. 507 art. 14 ust. 1 covers applications "wysłanych przesyłką rejestrowaną ... do dnia 31 października 2026 r.".
- **Since 27.04.2026 the proof of a pending application is a certificate from MOS,** not a stamp; the app asks for the certificate (or, for a case begun before, the stamp).
- **Ukrainian citizens who began before 5.03.2026** keep the old rule (legal stay and a PESEL) while their stay is legal (Dz.U. 2026 poz. 203 art. 42), whatever their title.
