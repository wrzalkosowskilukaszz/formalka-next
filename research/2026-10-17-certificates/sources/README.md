# Sources: certificates for other procedures (the KRK certificate, an apostille), doc 19 matter 24

Collected on 17.10.2026 by Claude, for the map `docs/formalka-next/maps/certificates.md`. Level R1 under doc 19: sources only, with provenance. Production's registry has no certificate procedure, so nothing here reaches it.

**Scope:**
- the criminal record certificate ("zaświadczenie o niekaralności"; in the act, "informacja z Krajowego Rejestru Karnego") that a foreigner in Poland asks for about themselves: who may ask, how, what it costs and what it shows;
- one Polish procedure that asks a foreigner for more than the Polish certificate: work or other activity with children (the act of 2016, art. 21);
- an apostille on a Polish document to be used abroad, and when an EU state may not ask for one (Regulation 2016/1191).

Not covered:
- an apostille or legalisation of a foreign document for use in Poland (the maps that need one name it, for example `maps/recognition.md`);
- a certificate on a company (informacja o podmiocie zbiorowym) and the queries of courts and offices (KRK art. 6);
- the multilingual standard form (the KRK page: for Polish citizens only);
- a criminal record certificate from another state (that state's office or its consul).

## What they say (FACT)

### The KRK certificate about oneself

| Topic | Content | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Who | Everyone may learn whether their data are in the register; a person whose data are there gets, on request, the content of all records about them | KRK art. 7 ust. 1 |
| Other EU registers | A person who is or was a citizen of an EU state, or lives or lived in one, may ask the register to query another EU state's central authority about them, if they live or lived in that state or are or were its citizen; the bureau makes such queries. So a third-country national living in Poland may ask about an EU state they live or lived in | KRK art. 7 ust. 1a, art. 4 ust. 1 pkt 5 (Dz.U. 2026 poz. 421 art. 1 pkt 4 lit. a and pkt 8, from 14.04.2026) |
| An EU citizen | The information for a citizen of another EU state holds the Polish register's data and that of the register of the state of citizenship; the bureau asks that register first | KRK art. 20 ust. 1d; information regulation § 8 |
| A third-country national, later | From the day the EU's ECRIS-TCN system starts, the information for a third-country national (or an EU citizen who also holds a third-country citizenship) will also hold the data of the EU registers that have some about them | KRK art. 20 ust. 1da (Dz.U. 2026 poz. 421 art. 1 pkt 17 lit. c; its art. 10 pkt 1) |
| When ECRIS-TCN starts | On the day a Commission decision sets (Regulation 2019/816 art. 35 ust. 4). The Commission's report of 19.06.2026 says exchanges on third-country nationals "are expected to increase with the entry into operation of ECRIS-TCN in 2027" | Dz.U. 2026 poz. 421 art. 10 pkt 1; COM(2026) 284 (ST 11076/26) |
| How | A request on paper or online; at the KRK Information Bureau or the register's information points at the common courts | KRK art. 19 ust. 1, 1b; information regulation § 2 ust. 1 |
| The request | Surname (also an adopted one), first names, family name, date and place of birth, parents' first names, the mother's family name, address, citizenship or citizenships, or that the person is stateless or of unknown citizenship, the PESEL, and the signature; without a PESEL, the number of a passport or another identity document. The data come from the identity document | KRK art. 19 ust. 3 (Dz.U. 2026 poz. 421 art. 1 pkt 16 lit. e, from 14.04.2026); information regulation § 3 ust. 1 |
| Online | Signed with a qualified electronic, trusted or personal signature; the answer and other letters go back online | KRK art. 19 ust. 3a, 19a |
| The online account | Name, the PESEL "o ile został nadany", a user name, a password and an e-mail address; then sign in, fill in, sign electronically and pay | 2021 regulation § 4 ust. 1, § 9 |
| Gaps | 14 days to fill them, else the request is left unexamined, unless the gaps concern only the data of ust. 2 pkt 1 (names, dates, parents, address, citizenship, the PESEL or passport number) and the person's identity is not in doubt | KRK art. 19 ust. 4 |
| What it shows | The identity data, with the citizenship or citizenships, or that the person is stateless or of unknown citizenship; the data the request covers, or that the person "nie figuruje w Rejestrze"; the date; the issuing person's name and signature; the seal. With no record, the request comes back marked "NIE FIGURUJE" | KRK art. 20 ust. 1 (pkt 1: Dz.U. 2026 poz. 421 art. 1 pkt 17 lit. a); information regulation § 12 |
| Foreign convictions | When a special provision sets a scope, convictions from foreign courts are given in that scope on the data foreign bodies sent; the same when the person set the scope | KRK art. 20 ust. 1a–1b |
| Its nature | A certificate (zaświadczenie) under the KPA's division VII; the KPA applies unless the KRK act says otherwise | KRK art. 23 (Dz.U. 2026 poz. 421 art. 1 pkt 18), art. 9 |
| Time | Without undue delay, at the latest within seven days; the office may first run an explanatory procedure where needed | KPA art. 217 § 3, 218 § 2, through KRK art. 9 and 23 |
| Collecting it | In person with an identity document; another person shows their authorisation too | Information regulation § 5 |
| Fee | 30 zł; 20 zł when filed online. Paid with a court fee stamp, in cash at the ministry's or a court's cash desk, or to the ministry's account (proof attached; the transfer names the person and the request's date); online, through the system's own payment, or, if the system cannot take it, with a PDF proof of a transfer to the ministry's account. Refunded when the request is left unexamined | Fee regulation § 3–9 |
| Exempt | Courts, offices and other bodies of KRK art. 6 ust. 1 pkt 1–9 and 11–13; volunteer candidates for leaders of children's holidays | KRK art. 24 ust. 1 (Dz.U. 2026 poz. 421 art. 1 pkt 19), 1a |
| Requests filed before 14.04.2026 | The old rules | Dz.U. 2026 poz. 421 art. 7 |
| Which online rules apply | The 2021 regulation. The 2023 regulation that would replace it was to apply from 1.03.2024; four amendments moved its start, now to 30.06.2027 | 2023 regulation § 24–25; Dz.U. 2024 poz. 279, 1396; 2025 poz. 721; 2026 poz. 716 |

### Work with children

| Topic | Content | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| When | Before a person is employed for, or admitted to, an activity with minors: upbringing, education, holidays, treatment, psychological advice, spiritual development, sport or other interests, or care | Act of 2016 art. 21 ust. 1 |
| The Polish certificate | The KRK information on the offences of chapters XIX and XXV of the Criminal Code, art. 189a and 207, and the act on counteracting drug addiction, or the matching foreign offences | Act of 2016 art. 21 ust. 3 |
| A foreign citizen | Also the information from the criminal register of the state of citizenship, issued for work or volunteering with children | Act of 2016 art. 21 ust. 4 |
| Other states of residence | A statement on the states other than Poland and the state of citizenship where the person lived in the last 20 years, with those states' register information | Act of 2016 art. 21 ust. 5 |
| No such certificate there | If that state's law issues no information for work with children, its ordinary register information (ust. 6); if it issues none at all or keeps no register, a statement of that, and that there is no final conviction there for the matching offences and no ban (ust. 7); the statements of ust. 5 and 7 are made under criminal liability, with the clause "Jestem świadomy odpowiedzialności karnej za złożenie fałszywego oświadczenia" (ust. 8) | Act of 2016 art. 21 ust. 6–8 |
| The employer's own check | The employer or organiser checks whether the person is in the Register of sex offenders with restricted access or in the State Commission's register | Act of 2016 art. 21 ust. 2 |
| If not presented | A person who does not present the information or statements of art. 21 ust. 3–7 before employment or admission is liable to arrest, restriction of liberty or a fine of at least 1000 zł | Act of 2016 art. 23 ust. 3 |
| Not needed | For a family member of the minor, or a person the parent knows personally, when the parent admits them for their own child | Act of 2016 art. 21 ust. 10–11 |

### An apostille on a Polish document

| Topic | Content | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| What it covers | Public documents made in one contracting state and to be produced in another: court documents, administrative documents, notarial acts, and official certificates on private documents (such as certified signatures). Not documents of diplomatic or consular agents, or administrative documents dealing directly with commercial or customs operations | Hague Convention art. 1 |
| What it replaces | Legalisation: each state exempts such documents from it; the only formality that may be asked is the apostille of the issuing state's authority, and not even that where the receiving state's law or practice, or an agreement, has abolished or simplified it | Hague Convention art. 2–3 |
| Its form | On the document or an allonge, on the convention's model; issued at the request of the signatory or the bearer; it certifies the signature, the signatory's capacity and the seal | Hague Convention art. 4–5 |
| In Poland | In force from 14.08.2005; Poland designated the Ministry of Foreign Affairs for all public documents | Hague Convention art. 6; government statement of 27.04.2005 |
| Fee | 60 zł for an apostille; 26 zł for a legalisation. 17 zł for filing a power of attorney; exempt among others: one certified by a notary or an authorised body that authorises collecting documents, and one given to a spouse, ascendant, descendant or sibling. Paid to the tax body (the mayor) of the place where the power is filed | Stamp duty act, annex part II items 5–6, part IV; art. 8 ust. 1, 12 ust. 1, ust. 2 pkt 2 |
| Within the EU | Public documents of a member state to be presented to another member state's authorities, whose main purpose is birth, life, death, name, marriage, divorce, a registered partnership, parenthood, adoption, residence, citizenship, or no criminal record, are exempt from all legalisation and the apostille. For no criminal record, only when the document is issued to a Union citizen by the member state of their citizenship. The person may still use the apostille or legalisation if they wish | Regulation 2016/1191 art. 1 ust. 1 (second subparagraph), art. 2 ust. 1, art. 3 pkt 4, art. 4 |
| The multilingual form | Attached on request to documents on birth, life, death, marriage, a registered partnership, residence and no criminal record; a translation aid with no legal value of its own; used only in another member state | Regulation 2016/1191 art. 7 ust. 1, 8 ust. 1, 3 |
| Not within it | Documents of third states, and their certified copies made by a member state | Regulation 2016/1191 art. 2 ust. 3 |
| Since | 16.02.2019 | Regulation 2016/1191 art. 27 ust. 2 |

## Inferences (to be confirmed)

- **Until ECRIS-TCN starts, a third-country national's Polish certificate shows only the Polish register.** Art. 20 ust. 1d covers EU citizens, and ust. 1da is not yet in force. A separate request under art. 7 ust. 1a may already ask an EU state the person lives or lived in.
- **Online without a PESEL needs a qualified electronic signature.** The trusted profile and the personal signature need a PESEL or a Polish identity card (`maps/e-government-access.md`); KRK art. 19 ust. 3a names only these three (OPEN-C6).
- **For an apostille, the paper certificate is the one to ask for,** since the MSZ page refuses printouts from ePUAP.
- **A Polish KRK certificate of a non-Polish citizen, used in another EU state, is outside Regulation 2016/1191 art. 2 ust. 1 lit. m,** so that state may ask for the apostille.
- **For work with children, a third-country citizen gets the home-state certificate from that state;** the Polish Bureau asks EU states only (KRK art. 4 ust. 1 pkt 5–5a: for an EU citizen, its own state; on request, an EU state the person lives or lived in), and the UK for UK citizens (the ECRIS page).
