| The trusted profile holds a PESEL | "Profil zaufany zawiera dane identyfikujące osobę fizyczną obejmujące: … numer PESEL", verified automatically against the PESEL register. It is only for a person with full or limited legal capacity | Informatization act art. 20ad ust. 1–2, art. 3 pkt 14 |
| How a profile is confirmed | In person at a confirmation point (with an ID card or passport that holds the PESEL, or another identity document that identifies a person who has a PESEL); online with a qualified signature whose certificate holds the PESEL; online through a bank's or another company's e-ID that confirms the PESEL, where the minister has approved it; online with a personal profile. At a point, the person applies online first; the application is deleted if not confirmed within 30 days | Art. 20c ust. 1, 8; trusted profile regulation (Dz.U. 2023 poz. 2551) § 3 ust. 2, § 4 ust. 2 |
| Confirmation points | Consul, head of a tax office, voivode, ZUS, NFZ (and primary-care staff it authorises); with the minister's consent other public bodies, banks, postal operators, branches of credit institutions, credit unions. Staff authorised by NFZ confirm only their own primary-care patients | Art. 20c ust. 2–3, 2a–2b |
| Video identification | Compares the person's live image with the image in the **register of ID cards**, and checks an ID card or passport | Art. 20cb ust. 2 |
| The trusted signature | Data signed with it are legally equal to a handwritten signature, unless a law says otherwise | Art. 20ae ust. 2 |
| Logging in to public services | An eID means from the national node, a notified eID means, or a qualified certificate; a public body may also allow other technologies | Art. 20a ust. 1–2 |

| PESEL in person | Since 1.01.2026 a foreigner who is not an EU, EFTA-EEA or Swiss citizen, a UK Withdrawal Agreement beneficiary or a family member of one applies for a PESEL in person, except children born in Poland; registering without a PESEL is also in person (with the exceptions of art. 41 ust. 1–2a) | Act on population records art. 18 ust. 1a, 40 ust. 3 (Dz.U. 2025 poz. 1301 art. 6, 29 pkt 1) |
| e-Doręczenia: who must, who may | Professionals (advocates, legal advisers, tax advisers and others) and businesses in KRS or CEIDG must have an address; anyone else may. KRS and the professions from 1.01.2025; CEIDG from 1.10.2026, with earlier entries by 30.09.2026 | E-delivery act art. 9–10, 151, 152, 166 pkt 5 and 9; communiqué Dz.U. 2024 poz. 1078 |
| Effect of an address | Entering an address in the database "jest równoznaczny z żądaniem doręczania korespondencji przez podmioty publiczne na ten adres"; public bodies deliver there, with the exceptions of art. 6; without it, the hybrid (paper) service. A letter not opened counts as delivered on the day after 14 days from its arrival. Government bodies, among them the voivode, deliver this way from 1.01.2025; courts, bailiffs and prosecutors from 1.10.2029. Since 1.01.2026 an ePUAP box no longer counts for letters to or from a person | Art. 4, 5, 6, 7 ust. 1, 41–42, 147 ust. 2–3, 155 ust. 1, 7; KPA art. 39 § 1–2 |

It holds, among others, **the PESEL or, if none, an EU member state's cross-border identifier**, and is signed with a qualified, trusted or personal signature. The box is activated after logging in with a means under the informatization act art. 20a ust. 1 pkt 1 or 2 | Art. 14 ust. 1 pkt 7, ust. 3 pkt 1; art. 15 (ust. 1a from 1.06.2025); art. 19 ust. 4 |

## Inferences (to be confirmed)

- **A person without a PESEL cannot have a trusted profile.** It follows from art. 20ad ust. 1, from every method in art. 20c ust. 1, and from the regulation § 4 ust. 2 ("posiadającej numer PESEL").
- **Without a PESEL, an e-Doręczenia address is open only to someone with an EU cross-border identifier,** in practice an EU citizen with a notified eID (art. 14 ust. 1 pkt 7; activation needs a means under art. 20a ust. 1 pkt 1 or 2, art. 19 ust. 4).
- **The personal profile and video identification rely on the Polish ID card and its register.** A foreigner, who has no Polish ID card, uses a confirmation point, a bank's e-ID, or a qualified signature (art. 20c ust. 1 pkt 4, 20cb ust. 2). The ID card act issues the card to Polish citizens (art. 6 ust. 1), and the video route rejects an application when the ID card register has no image of the person (regulation § 6 ust. 4).
