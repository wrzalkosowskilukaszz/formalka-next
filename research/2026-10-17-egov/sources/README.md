## What they say (FACT)

| Topic | Content | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| The trusted profile holds a PESEL | "Profil zaufany zawiera dane identyfikujące osobę fizyczną obejmujące: … numer PESEL", verified automatically against the PESEL register | Informatization act art. 20ad ust. 1–2 |
| How a profile is confirmed | In person at a confirmation point (with an ID card or passport that holds the PESEL, or another identity document that identifies a person who has a PESEL); online with a qualified signature whose certificate holds the PESEL; online through a bank's or another company's e-ID that confirms the PESEL; online with a personal profile | Art. 20c ust. 1 |
| Confirmation points | Consul, head of a tax office, voivode, ZUS, NFZ (and primary-care staff it authorises); with the minister's consent other public bodies, banks, postal operators, credit institutions, credit unions | Art. 20c ust. 2–3, 2a |
| Video identification | Compares the person's live image with the image in the **register of ID cards**, and checks an ID card or passport | Art. 20cb ust. 2 |
| The trusted signature | Data signed with it are legally equal to a handwritten signature, unless a law says otherwise | Art. 20ae ust. 2 |
| Logging in to public services | An eID means from the national node, a notified eID means, or a qualified certificate | Art. 20a ust. 1 |
| e-Doręczenia: who must, who may | Professionals (advocates, legal advisers, tax advisers and others) and businesses in KRS or CEIDG must have an address; anyone else may | E-delivery act art. 9–10 |
| Effect of an address | Entering an address in the database "jest równoznaczny z żądaniem doręczania korespondencji przez podmioty publiczne na ten adres"; public bodies deliver there; without it, the hybrid (paper) service | Art. 4, 5, 7 ust. 1 |
| A person's application | Online, through the minister's service or the designated operator's (art. 15 ust. 1, 1a). It holds, among others, **the PESEL or, if none, an EU member state's cross-border identifier**, and is signed with a qualified, trusted or personal signature | Art. 14 ust. 1 pkt 7, ust. 3 pkt 1; art. 15 as changed by 1841 |
| Temporary protection beneficiaries | A trusted profile is confirmed automatically if the PESEL UKR application held contact data and consent; otherwise on request at any municipal office | Protection act art. 110c |

## Inferences (to be confirmed)

- **A person without a PESEL cannot have a trusted profile.** It follows from art. 20ad ust. 1 and from every method in art. 20c ust. 1.
- **Without a PESEL, an e-Doręczenia address is open only to someone with an EU cross-border identifier,** in practice an EU citizen with a notified eID (art. 14 ust. 1 pkt 7).
- **The personal profile and video identification rely on the Polish ID card and its register.** A foreigner, who has no Polish ID card, uses a confirmation point, a bank's e-ID, or a qualified signature (art. 20c ust. 1 pkt 4, 20cb ust. 2).
