# Sources: EU citizens in the register of voters, doc 19 matter 25

Collected on 17.10.2026 by Claude, for the map `docs/formalka-next/maps/voters.md`. Level R1 under doc 19: sources only, with provenance. Production's registry has no voter procedure, so nothing here reaches it.

**Scope:** who among foreigners may vote and stand in Polish elections (EU citizens; UK citizens in local elections), how such a person gets into the Central Register of Voters (Centralny Rejestr Wyborców, CRW), what it costs, how long it takes, how to check and correct it, and how to leave it.

Not covered:
- elections for Polish citizens (Sejm, Senate, President, district and regional councils), and voting abroad;
- standing as a candidate beyond who may (the nomination rules);
- local referenda and local auxiliary units;
- the election calendar: each election's order sets its dates.

## What they say (FACT)

### Who may vote and stand

| Topic | Content | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| The European Parliament | A citizen of another EU state who is 18 at the latest on election day and lives permanently in Poland may vote | Code art. 10 § 1 pkt 2 |
| The gmina council and the wójt | A citizen of another EU state, or of the United Kingdom, who is 18 at the latest on election day and lives permanently in the gmina may vote for its council and its wójt | Code art. 10 § 1 pkt 3 lit. a, pkt 4 |
| Not | The Sejm, the Senate, the President, district and regional councils: Polish citizens only | Code art. 10 § 1 pkt 1, pkt 3 lit. b |
| Who may not vote | A person deprived of public rights by a final court ruling, of electoral rights by a final ruling of the State Tribunal, or legally incapacitated by a final court ruling | Code art. 10 § 2 |
| Standing | The European Parliament: a voter there who turns 21 at the latest on election day and has lived permanently in Poland or another EU state for at least 5 years; councils: a voter for them; the wójt: Polish citizens only. Nobody may stand who was finally sentenced to imprisonment for an intentional offence prosecuted publicly or an intentional fiscal offence (for the wójt, finally sentenced for such an offence), or whose loss of the right to stand under the lustration act was finally ruled | Code art. 11 § 1 pkt 4–6, § 2 |
| A ban at home | An EU or UK citizen deprived of the right to stand in their own state may not stand | Code art. 11 § 3 |

### The register

| Topic | Content | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| What it is | The CRW holds the voters, those over 17, those deprived of the right to vote, and the permanent voting districts and constituencies; it confirms the right to vote | Code art. 18 § 1–2 |
| Part B | EU and UK citizens entitled to vote in Poland: name, PESEL, date of birth, citizenship, passport or other identity document number, the permanent address given in the art. 19a application, and the districts. Those not in part B are kept only by their registered permanent address | Code art. 18a § 3–4 |
| Not automatic | EU and UK citizens living permanently in a gmina are entered in part B in the permanent voting district of their address "jeżeli złożą w tej sprawie wniosek" to the gmina office of their residence | Code art. 19a § 1 |
| The application | Surname, first names, citizenship, the passport or other identity document number, the PESEL, and a statement on the permanent address; optionally an e-mail or mobile number. Without a PESEL, the data needed to assign one | Code art. 19a § 2–4 |
| How | On paper, signed by hand; or online through the ministry's service, signed with a qualified electronic signature or a trusted signature. Online since 1.09.2023 | Code art. 19a § 5; Dz.U. 2023 poz. 497 art. 11; Dz.U. 2023 poz. 1514 |
| Discrepancies | If the application's data differ from the PESEL register, the wójt clears them up in 7 days | Code art. 19a § 9 |
| Living nowhere | A voter who lives nowhere but stays permanently in the gmina gives, instead of a permanent address, an address where the office can reach him | Code art. 19a § 10 |
| The decision | The wójt decides in 5 days from the application (or from clearing up a discrepancy), after checking the permanent residence at the address. A refusal, with reasons, goes to the applicant; a complaint goes to the district court through the wójt within 3 days of the decision's delivery, and the wójt may change or revoke the decision himself if he finds the complaint fully justified; the court decides in 3 days, finally | Code art. 20 |
| A PESEL | The gmina that decides positively on the art. 19a application asks for a PESEL ex officio | UEL art. 17 ust. 1 pkt 2a |
| Leaving | On request to the gmina office (surname, first names, PESEL), on paper or with a qualified or trusted signature; the data are also removed on death or loss of the EU or UK citizenship | Code art. 19a § 6–8, 18d |
| The old register | An EU or UK citizen added to the register of voters under the old rules who has no PESEL files the art. 19a application to be placed in a permanent district | Dz.U. 2023 poz. 497 art. 14 |
| A complaint | About not being entered, or being entered wrongly: to the wójt, orally or in writing (on paper, or electronically with a qualified, trusted or personal signature); decided in 3 days; against a decision that does not uphold it or strikes the voter off a district, a complaint to the district court through the wójt within 3 days of its delivery | Code art. 22 |
| The home state | Before the European Parliament elections the ministry sends the home state the data of EU citizens voting in Poland, at the latest 5 days before | Code art. 23 § 1–2 |

### At election time

| Topic | Content | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| The list | For each election the gmina draws up the list of voters from the CRW, part A and part B; part B in the European Parliament, gmina council and wójt elections; UK citizens only in the local ones. The list is drawn up at the latest on the 2nd day before the election, from the register as it stands on the 3rd day before | Code art. 26 § 1–9 |
| A one-time change of place | From the 44th to the 3rd day before an election, on application; not in local elections | Code art. 28 § 1–2 |
| Checking one's data | Online through the ministry's service; or on request from the wójt, during an election at the latest on the 5th day before it; from the 44th to the 5th day before, whether one is on the list, by a written request on paper, signed by hand, at the gmina office that drew up the list | Code art. 36 |
| Voting | The voter shows a document that proves identity | Code art. 52 § 1 |

## Inferences (to be confirmed)

- **An EU citizen registered for permanent stay in Poland is not on the voters' list until they apply** (art. 19a § 1; part B holds only applicants). A Polish citizen is entered ex officio by the registered address (art. 19 § 2, FACT).
- **Apply well before an election:** the decision takes up to 5 days, or 12 with a discrepancy, and the list follows the register on the 3rd day before the election (art. 20 § 1, 19a § 9, 26 § 9). So an application must be filed at least 15 days before the election to reach the list even with a discrepancy (12 days plus the 3rd day before), and more to leave time for a refusal and a complaint (3 days from delivery, then 3 days at the court, art. 20 § 4–5). The app could suggest filing at least 3 weeks before as a margin (PROPOSED; not a rule).
- **Without a PESEL the application still works,** and the gmina then asks for one (art. 19a § 4; UEL art. 17 ust. 1 pkt 2a); online needs a trusted or qualified signature, which without a PESEL means a qualified one (`maps/e-government-access.md`).
- **A move to another gmina needs a new application** there, since part B follows the permanent address the person declared (art. 18a § 3 pkt 7, 19a § 1; INFERENCE).
- **A family member of an EU citizen who is not an EU citizen may not vote** (art. 10 names citizenship only); the page's "CUE" status is a PESEL category, not a voting right.
