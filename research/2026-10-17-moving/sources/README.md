# Sources: moving, deregistering and leaving Poland, for a foreigner, doc 19 matter 19

Collected on 17.10.2026 by Claude, for the map `docs/formalka-next/maps/moving.md`. Level R1 under doc 19: sources only, with provenance. Production's registry covers registering at an address (`reference/address-registration/`), not deregistering, a departure or a move, so nothing here reaches the app.

**Scope:**
- the registration duty after the first registration: moving to a new address, deregistering, the end of a temporary registration, a departure abroad and a return;
- what a move changes elsewhere: the residence card, a pending case, a vehicle.

Not covered:
- registering itself (production's registry; `research/2026-10-16-service/sources/`);
- the PESEL;
- a Polish citizen's own rules, except where the act applies them to foreigners.

## What they say (FACT)

### Who and what

| Topic | Content | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| The duty | Registering at the place of permanent or temporary stay; deregistering from it; reporting a departure abroad and a return | UEL art. 24 ust. 2 |
| For foreigners | The same duty, on the rules of art. 24, 25 ust. 1–3, 26, 27 ust. 2, 28 and 30–39, unless the chapter on foreigners says otherwise; for the foreigners of art. 41 ust. 1–2a (EU, EEA and Swiss citizens, their family members, and a Polish citizen's family member of the EU entry act), art. 24–26. Art. 25 ust. 4 (the 3 months) speaks of a Polish citizen; that it reaches them through art. 40 ust. 2 is INFERENCE | UEL art. 40 ust. 1–2 |
| Not for | A foreigner whose stay in Poland ("na terytorium Rzeczypospolitej Polskiej") does not exceed 30 days; diplomats and consuls with their families, on reciprocity | UEL art. 42 |
| Stays | Permanent: living at an address with the intention to stay; temporary: staying at another address without changing the permanent one. One permanent and one temporary place at a time | UEL art. 25 ust. 1–2, 27 ust. 2 |
| What it is | Registration serves records only and confirms that the person stays at the address | UEL art. 28 ust. 4 |
| A proxy | The duty may be done by a proxy with a written power of attorney (KPA art. 33 § 2) | UEL art. 24 ust. 4 |

### Moving to a new address

| Topic | Content | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Registering at the new address | EU, EEA and Swiss citizens and their family members (and a Polish citizen's family member of the EU entry act): by the 30th day from arriving at the place; other foreigners: by the fourth day | UEL art. 41 ust. 1–3 |
| The declared period | A temporary registration states the declared period; for a foreigner other than those of art. 41 ust. 1–2a, it may not exceed the legal stay under the document of the right to stay | UEL art. 28 ust. 3, 41 ust. 4 |
| In person | A foreigner other than those of art. 41 ust. 1–2a (and the UK Withdrawal Agreement beneficiaries) without a PESEL registers in person | UEL art. 40 ust. 3 |
| The documents for a temporary registration | A visa; or, for a visa-free entry or a stay while an application is pending (UoC art. 108 ust. 1 pkt 2, 206 ust. 1 pkt 2, 222a ust. 1 pkt 2), the travel document, a temporary identity certificate, a residence card, a tolerated stay document, or the permit or decision; in a special case without a travel document, another identity document | UEL art. 43 ust. 3, 5 (from 27.04.2026; M.P. 2026 poz. 370) |
| The old address | A person "może wymeldować się" from the old place by registering at the new one, on the registration form (art. 33 ust. 3, 34 ust. 3). The forms: registering a permanent stay ends the old permanent registration automatically, and a temporary one only if its box is ticked; registering a temporary stay ends the old temporary one automatically, and the permanent one only if its box is ticked | UEL art. 33 ust. 3, 34 ust. 3; the forms regulation, annexes 1–2 (EL/ZPS/1, EL/ZC/1), quoted below |
| The certificate | A permanent registration certificate ex officio; a temporary one on request; valid until the registration changes, a temporary one at most until its end | UEL art. 32 ust. 1–4 |
| Doubts | The municipality decides doubts about a registration or deregistration, or the permanent or temporary nature of a stay, by decision | UEL art. 31 |

### Deregistering

| Topic | Content | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| When | On leaving the place of permanent stay, or a place of temporary stay before the declared period ends. The act names no day; the pages say "najpóźniej w dniu, w którym wyprowadzasz się" | UEL art. 33 ust. 1; PAGES |
| How | On paper at the municipality of the old address, with an identity document shown; or electronically with a qualified, trusted or personal signature, if the e-delivery proof is received; or by the minister's online service after logging in | UEL art. 33 ust. 2, 4 |
| A child | A parent may deregister a child online if the parent's PESEL is entered at the child's data, with a statement under criminal liability | UEL art. 33 ust. 5–7 |
| By decision | The municipality deregisters by decision, of its own motion or on the owner's request, a person who left and did not deregister | UEL art. 35 |
| A death | Reporting a death at the civil status office replaces deregistration | UEL art. 38 |

### Leaving Poland and returning

| Topic | Content | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Leaving for good | Report the departure; it deregisters from the permanent and the temporary place | UEL art. 36 ust. 1 |
| Leaving for more than 6 months | Without the intention to stay abroad: report the departure and the return. The departure form says the report ends the temporary registration; the temporary deregistration form tells such a person to file only the departure form | UEL art. 36 ust. 2; the forms regulation, annexes 4–5 (EL/WPC/2, EL/ZW/3), quoted below |
| By when | At the latest on the day of leaving the place of stay; on paper at the municipality, or electronically, or online | UEL art. 36 ust. 3–4 |

### If not done

| Topic | Content | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| A fine | A foreigner who is not an EU, EEA or Swiss citizen, nor such a citizen's family member, and does not meet the registration duty, is liable to a fine ("karze grzywny"), under the petty offences procedure | UEL art. 59–60 |

### What a move changes elsewhere

| Topic | Content | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| The residence card | It shows the registered permanent or temporary address, except when the person did not register a temporary stay of over 2 months (art. 244 ust. 4), and except on a card issued under the special act's art. 42c or 42d (the CUKR card), which shows no registered address (SA art. 42m pkt 3, from 4.05.2026). A change of the card's data requires its exchange; the exchange is applied for within 14 days of the reason, and costs the card fee. The voivode of the place of stay exchanges it (art. 245 ust. 3; a CUKR card: SA art. 42q); for a humanitarian stay, the Border Guard (art. 245 ust. 4) | UoC art. 244 ust. 1 pkt 3, ust. 4; 241 pkt 1; 230 ust. 1; 235 ust. 1; 245 ust. 3–4 (`research/2026-10-16-residence/sources/`, `other-acts-moving-extract.txt`); SA art. 42m, 42q |

## Inferences (to be confirmed)

- **A temporary registration ends with its declared period without any step**, as the pages say; the act sets the duty to deregister only before that end and dates the certificate to it (UEL art. 32 ust. 4, 33 ust. 1).
- **A foreigner's temporary registration ends with the legal stay at the latest** (UEL art. 41 ust. 4). After a new permit, or while waiting on an application filed in time (UoC art. 108 ust. 1 pkt 2), registering again needs the document of art. 43 ust. 3 for the new period.
- **Moving means a new residence card,** because the card shows the registered address and a change of its data requires the exchange within 14 days (UoC art. 244, 241, 230). Not for a CUKR card, which shows no address (SA art. 42m pkt 3), nor where art. 244 ust. 4 left the address out. The 14 days run from the change of the card's data, so from the new registration, not from the move itself. The card's fee and the exchange procedure are in the residence map's sources.
- **Registering again while a permit application runs** works only if the application was filed in time and has no formal defects (or they were made good in time), and not while the case is suspended at the person's request (UoC art. 108 ust. 1–2); since 27.04.2026 the voivode gives a certificate instead of the stamp (Dz.U. 2025 poz. 1794 art. 1 pkt 14 lit. a).
- **A non-EU foreigner who lives in Poland and stays a few weeks at another address in Poland** has, on the act's words, a temporary-stay duty from the fourth day: art. 42 ust. 2 counts the whole stay in Poland, not the stay at one address, and art. 40 ust. 1 does not apply the 3 months of art. 25 ust. 4 (OPEN-M5).
- **Moving during a residence case needs a notice to the voivode** (KPA art. 41); otherwise a letter to the old address counts as delivered, and a time limit to answer or appeal can pass.
- **The fine covers deregistering and departure reports too,** since art. 59 speaks of "obowiązku meldunkowego", which art. 24 ust. 2 defines as all three.
