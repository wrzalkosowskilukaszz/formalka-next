# Sources: registering as unemployed, for a foreigner, doc 19 matter 17

Collected on 17.10.2026 by Claude, for the map `docs/formalka-next/maps/unemployment.md`. Level R1 under doc 19: sources only, with provenance. Production's registry has nothing on labour offices, so nothing here reaches the app.

**Scope:**
- who may register at the county labour office ("powiatowy urząd pracy", PUP) as unemployed ("bezrobotny") or as a job seeker ("poszukujący pracy");
- how, from which day, the duties, and losing the status;
- the unemployment benefit ("zasiłek dla bezrobotnych"): who gets it, how much, how long.

Not covered:
- the forms of help one by one (training, internships, grants);
- the pre-retirement benefits;
- the EU coordination of benefits beyond what the act says (OPEN-U2).

## What they say (FACT)

### Who may register

| Topic | Content | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Unemployed: the foreigners | Citizens of the EU, the EEA and Switzerland, and UK Withdrawal Agreement beneficiaries; holders of a permanent or EU long-term resident permit; refugees; subsidiary protection; humanitarian or tolerated stay; temporary protection; applicants for international protection with the certificate of the protection act art. 35; temporary permits under UoC art. 127, 137a or 186 ust. 1 pkt 3; temporary permits under UoC art. 114 ust. 1 or 1a or 126 ust. 1, or a work visa; a visa under UoC art. 60 ust. 1 pkt 23 or a permit under art. 186 ust. 1 pkt 9 | RP art. 2 pkt 1 with art. 1 ust. 3 pkt 2 lit. a–l, n, p |
| Unemployed: family members | Family members accompanying an EU, EEA or Swiss citizen; family members of Polish citizens with a temporary permit, or staying legally while their application is pending (UoC art. 108 ust. 1 pkt 2, 206 ust. 1 pkt 2 or 222a ust. 1 pkt 2), if they had a temporary permit before; family members accompanying a Polish citizen. From 27.04.2026 the passport stamp is no longer a basis in pkt 5; it still counts in a long-term resident case begun before then with that stamp, and pending cases follow the old rules | RP art. 2 pkt 1 with art. 1 ust. 3 pkt 3, 5, 6; pkt 5 as changed by Dz.U. 2025 poz. 1794 art. 10, from 27.04.2026 (M.P. 2026 poz. 370); 1794 art. 12, 14 |
| Job seeker only | Everyone the act covers (art. 1 ust. 3), over 18 and looking for work, may register as a job seeker. So those of art. 1 ust. 3 pkt 2 lit. m (among others the permits of UoC art. 144, 151 and 159 ust. 1, for studies, research and family reunification, and student and research visas), lit. o (seasonal work) and pkt 4 (family members of protected persons) may register only as job seekers. A family member of a Polish citizen with a temporary permit is in pkt 5 whatever the permit's purpose, so may be unemployed | RP art. 2 pkt 1, 24; art. 1 ust. 3; UoC art. 144 ust. 1, 151 ust. 1, 159 ust. 1 (their purposes) |
| The other conditions | Not employed and not doing other paid work; not at school (with exceptions); no "stałe źródło dochodu"; able and ready to work full time (half time for a person with a disability); 18 or over, and under 60 (a woman) or 65 (a man); no business entered in CEIDG (unless suspended or not yet started); not in custody; no permanent social assistance benefit; no company functions; not insured socially on another title (except farmers); not compulsorily insured against unemployment, or compulsorily insured socially, in a state of art. 1 ust. 3 pkt 2 lit. a–d (the EU, the EEA, Switzerland, the United Kingdom); no business abroad | RP art. 2 pkt 1 lit. a–n (lit. d–e as worded from 14.10.2026, Dz.U. 2026 poz. 507 art. 11) |
| Temporary protection | Lit. j covers those "korzystających w Rzeczypospolitej Polskiej z ochrony czasowej". For 3 years from 5.03.2026, Ukrainians staying legally without temporary protection are treated under the work act's art. 3 ust. 1 pkt 12 (free access to the labour market) and art. 5a (the employer's notice to the PUP); that rule is about work, and lit. j does not name them | RP art. 1 ust. 3 pkt 2 lit. j; Dz.U. 2026 poz. 203 art. 41; the work act art. 3 ust. 1 (`research/2026-10-16-residence/sources/praca-cudzoziemcow-2025-621-art3-extract.txt`) |
| A steady income | Among others: a pension or disability pension; a sickness, maternity or rehabilitation benefit after work ends; a foreign pension at least the lowest Polish one; a monthly income above half the minimum wage (interest excepted); a carer's benefit | RP art. 2 pkt 42 |
| One office | A registered unemployed person cannot register as a job seeker or at another office | RP art. 57 ust. 1 |
| The forms of help | Open to those of art. 1 ust. 3 pkt 1, pkt 2 lit. a–j and l, and pkt 3, 5 and 6; to those of lit. k, m, n and p without the education loan (art. 111) and the training stipend (art. 234 ust. 1); to those of lit. o only the forms on seasonal work, with the same two left out. The forms themselves are not covered here | RP art. 72 |

### Registering

| Topic | Content | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| How | On application: online, by the form on the ministry's system (praca.gov.pl), or in person at the PUP of the place of residence | RP art. 55 ust. 1–2 |
| The data | Among others: citizenship; the PESEL, or without one the date and place of birth, sex and the identity document's type, series and number; the address; education, jobs and periods of work; for a foreigner, the status under art. 1 ust. 3 pkt 2–6 | RP art. 55 ust. 3 pkt 2–3, 8, 24 |
| The documents | Online: digital copies of certificates of education, work certificates and other documents needed for the rights; in person: the identity card, or another identity document, and the same documents to be seen | RP art. 55 ust. 5–6 |
| Statements | Able and ready to work, and no steady income, under criminal liability; the statement that one was told the conditions | RP art. 55 ust. 9–10, 60 |
| From which day | Online: the day a complete application is filed; in person: the day of the visit, after signing the data and the statements | RP art. 61 ust. 1 |
| No registration | When data or a signature is refused or missing; the starosta may allow it in especially justified cases without all documents | RP art. 61 ust. 2–3 |
| Signatures | Online: a qualified, trusted or personal signature | RP art. 44 ust. 3, 61 ust. 2 pkt 3 lit. a |
| The account | The individual account may be set up at the PUP with help; logging in by the state login methods or the mObywatel certificate; a foreigner without a PESEL gives an identity document's data or an EU identifier. Accounts set up earlier on praca.gov.pl became individual accounts | RP art. 43 ust. 2, 4–5; 432 ust. 6 |
| Letters | Where an individual account was set up, letters are delivered on it, and count as delivered when opened or after 14 days | RP art. 45 ust. 1, 3 |
| Who decides | The starosta, "jeżeli ustawa nie stanowi inaczej"; the director or, on the director's request, other staff of the PUP may act for the starosta under a written authorisation; the higher authority is the voivode. The act names the decisions granting, refusing and losing the status. A decision removing the status is enforced at once | RP art. 31, 38 ust. 1 pkt 14 lit. a–b, ust. 2, 39 ust. 2 |
| Periods abroad | Where periods of insurance, work or self-employment in the EU, the EEA, Switzerland, the UK or a state with a bilateral agreement matter or could matter for the benefit, the marshal of the voivodeship decides on it (except under art. 223), and the higher authority is the minister for social security | RP art. 32 ust. 1 pkt 11, 34 ust. 2, 35 ust. 1 |
| The appeal | Through the authority that issued the decision, within 14 days of delivery; where an individual account was set up, appeals go through the account. Before that time ends, and while the appeal runs, a decision is not enforced, unless it is enforceable by law, as a removal of status is | KPA art. 129 § 1–2, 130 § 1–3 (Dz.U. 2025 poz. 1691); RP art. 44 ust. 1, 38 ust. 2 |

### Keeping the status

| Topic | Content | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| How long | 3 years from registration, unless lost earlier; longer by the time needed to finish a form of help, and after a birth by the maternity benefit's period; again after 90 days, counted anew. For those who had the status on 1.06.2025, the 3 years run from that day | RP art. 63; 433 ust. 7 |
| Changes | Notify the PUP of any change in the data or the statements within 7 days, online or in person; and of a job, other paid work or a CEIDG entry application "z informacją o podjęciu działalności gospodarczej" within 7 days | RP art. 62 ust. 1, 67, 232 (as worded from 14.10.2026) |
| Not reporting | Not a ground for removal in itself. A benefit paid after the right ended is undue if the person had been told of those circumstances, as is one paid on false statements; it is repaid within 30 days of the starosta's decision | RP art. 246 ust. 1–2 |
| Moving | Notify the PUP and name the PUP of the new address | RP art. 62 ust. 3 |
| Removal | By the starosta's decision; among others: no longer meeting art. 2 pkt 1; refusing or breaking off a form of help by one's own fault (90 days); refusing socially useful work (90 days); illness for 180 days without a break; no sick note; no contact at least once in 90 days (90 days); a trip abroad not notified, or longer than 30 days (90 days); refusing the individual action plan (90 days) | RP art. 65 ust. 1; 38 ust. 1 pkt 14 lit. b |
| No removal | Not for a pregnant woman's pregnancy-related illness of 180 days, and not for a parent's (or an adoptive or foster parent's) care of a child for the maternity benefit's period, unless the person asks; not for a founding member of a social cooperative until the day after its funds arrive | RP art. 66 |
| Abroad | Up to 30 days, notified in advance: the status stays, without the benefit for that time; at most 30 days in a calendar year | RP art. 229 |
| Illness | Notify within 2 days of the sick note and deliver it within 7 days; the starosta may accept it later in justified cases | RP art. 239 ust. 2–3 |
| Health insurance | Unemployed persons not insured on another title are compulsorily insured for health | The health care act art. 66 ust. 1 pkt 24 (`research/2026-10-17-health/sources/`); PSZ "Prawa i obowiązki" |

### The benefit

| Topic | Content | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Who | An unemployed person who, in the 18 months before registering, for at least 365 days in all, was employed or did other work at least on the minimum wage with Labour Fund contributions due, among other titles; for each day from registration, except under art. 219 and 220. A period that once gave the right does not count again (except for a supplementary period, art. 226 ust. 3) | RP art. 218 ust. 1, 5 |
| Not at all | After quitting the last job by notice (except for a move, or a termination under the Labour Code art. 55 § 1 or § 1¹, which the text extraction prints as "§ 1 i 11"); after a dismissal for one's own fault without notice; after quitting a job taken on the starosta's referral with Labour Fund money. None of this applies when the last job is not the basis of the right | RP art. 219 |
| Later | After a severance equivalent, for its period; after 90 days if the job ended by agreement (except for a move, bankruptcy, liquidation or redundancy); among others. The benefit's period is shortened by these days, by the days abroad of art. 229, and by intervention work, public works, training and internships within it | RP art. 220 ust. 1–2, 225 ust. 3 |
| How much | From 1.06.2026: 1783,90 zł for the first 90 days, then 1400,90 zł; 120 % after at least 20 years of the counted periods. Indexed each 1 June, but not when prices did not rise. A benefit granted under the old act keeps its amount and period, and is indexed under this act | RP art. 224 ust. 1–2, 228 ust. 1, 436 ust. 1–3; M.P. 2026 poz. 268 |
| How long | 180 days; 365 days for, among others, persons with a disability, Large Family Card holders, those over 50 with 20 years, and single parents. Longer by the maternity benefit's period for a woman who gives birth while on the benefit or within 30 days after it | RP art. 225 ust. 1–2 |
| Income and changes | An income statement within 7 days of each income | RP art. 231 |
| Moving to look for work in the EU | The right to the benefit goes with the person under the coordination rules | RP art. 230 |

The ministry's own portal (psz.praca.gov.pl, modified 2025–2026) matches the act on the points compared here, with two exceptions:
- its page "Utrata statusu" (03.06.2025) lists, as reasons to lose the status, a suspended business and a business whose start date has not come; the act allows the status in both cases (RP art. 2 pkt 1 lit. c–e are alternatives). The page is wrong; the act's rule stands;
- "Przyznanie statusu" and "Prawa i obowiązki" use the CEIDG wording from before 14.10.2026 (Dz.U. 2026 poz. 507 art. 11); the substance is the same.

## Inferences (to be confirmed)

- **A PESEL is not required to register.** The application takes the date and place of birth and an identity document instead (RP art. 55 ust. 3 pkt 3), and the account takes an identity document's data (art. 43 ust. 5 pkt 1 lit. c). Online, the signature decides in practice: the ministry's pages say a trusted profile carries a PESEL and a qualified certificate must be issued in the EU with a "PNO" identifier (psz "Profil zaufany", 24.03.2020; "Konto użytkownika", 06.06.2023; both before the act, not binding). So without a PESEL, the visit is the sure way (OPEN-U1).
- **A residence registration ("meldunek") is not required.** The act asks for the address of residence (RP art. 55 ust. 3 pkt 8) and ties the office to the place of residence (art. 55 ust. 2 pkt 2).
- **Registering as unemployed does not by itself protect a residence permit tied to a job.** The labour market act says nothing on residence. The act on foreigners has its own rules for a lost job (the residence map, FS17; UoC art. 121, 123, 133).
- **For 800+**, someone on the unemployment benefit counts as active, and a registered unemployed person without it gets 3 or 6 months, once in the benefit period (`maps/child-benefit-800.md`; the 800+ act art. 1 ust. 2b–2c, 2 pkt 1a lit. b). This concerns only the foreigners of the 800+ act's art. 1 ust. 2 pkt 2 lit. c–f, and not, among others, a child who is a Polish citizen or has a disability certificate (its ust. 2a).
- **A Ukrainian without temporary protection** registers on their own residence title under art. 1 ust. 3, not under lit. j; 203 art. 41 does not change that. Ask about temporary protection, not the PESEL's status.
- **A residence title outside art. 1 ust. 3** (for example a permit under UoC art. 142 or 186 ust. 1 pkt 6, which art. 1 ust. 3 does not name) gives neither status, since both definitions start from art. 1 ust. 3 (RP art. 2 pkt 1, 24).
