# Sources: registering as unemployed, for a foreigner, doc 19 matter 17

Collected on 17.10.2026 by Claude, for the map `docs/formalka-next/maps/unemployment.md`. Level R1 under doc 19: sources only, with provenance. Production's registry has nothing on labour offices, so nothing here reaches the app.

**Scope:**
- who may register at the county labour office ("powiatowy urząd pracy", PUP) as unemployed ("bezrobotny") or as a job seeker ("poszukujący pracy");
- how, from which day, the duties, and losing the status;
- the unemployment benefit ("zasiłek dla bezrobotnych"): who gets it, how much, how long.

Not covered:
- the forms of help one by one (training, internships, grants);
- the pre-retirement benefits;
- the EU coordination of benefits beyond what the act says (OPEN-U2).

RP below is the labour market act as in force on 17.10.2026.

## What they say (FACT)

### Who may register

| Topic | Content | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Unemployed: the foreigners | Citizens of the EU, the EEA and Switzerland, and UK Withdrawal Agreement beneficiaries; holders of a permanent or EU long-term resident permit; refugees; subsidiary protection; humanitarian or tolerated stay; temporary protection; applicants for international protection with the certificate of the protection act art. 35; temporary permits under UoC art. 127, 137a or 186 ust. 1 pkt 3; temporary permits under UoC art. 114 ust. 1 or 1a or 126 ust. 1, or a work visa; a visa under UoC art. 60 ust. 1 pkt 23 or a permit under art. 186 ust. 1 pkt 9 | RP art. 2 pkt 1 with art. 1 ust. 3 pkt 2 lit. a–l, n, p |
| Unemployed: family members | Family members accompanying an EU, EEA or Swiss citizen; family members of Polish citizens with a temporary permit, or staying legally while their application is pending, if they had a temporary permit before; family members accompanying a Polish citizen | RP art. 2 pkt 1 with art. 1 ust. 3 pkt 3, 5, 6; pkt 5 as changed by Dz.U. 2025 poz. 1794 art. 10 (adds UoC art. 222a), from 27.04.2026 |
| Job seeker only | Everyone the act covers (art. 1 ust. 3), over 18 and looking for work, may register as a job seeker. So those of art. 1 ust. 3 pkt 2 lit. m (among others the permits of UoC art. 144, 151 and 159 ust. 1, for studies, research and family reunification, and student and research visas), lit. o (seasonal work) and pkt 4 (family members of protected persons) may register only as job seekers | RP art. 2 pkt 1, 24; art. 1 ust. 3; UoC art. 144 ust. 1, 151 ust. 1, 159 ust. 1 (their purposes) |
| The other conditions | Not employed and not doing other paid work; not at school (with exceptions); no "stałe źródło dochodu"; able and ready to work full time (half time for a person with a disability); 18 or over, and under 60 (a woman) or 65 (a man); no business entered in CEIDG (unless suspended or not yet started); not in custody; no permanent social assistance benefit; no company functions; not insured socially on another title (except farmers); not insured against unemployment in the EU, EEA or Switzerland; no business abroad | RP art. 2 pkt 1 lit. a–n (lit. d–e as worded from 14.10.2026, Dz.U. 2026 poz. 507 art. 11) |
| A steady income | Among others: a pension or disability pension; a sickness, maternity or rehabilitation benefit after work ends; a foreign pension at least the lowest Polish one; a monthly income above half the minimum wage (interest excepted); a carer's benefit | RP art. 2 pkt 42 |
| One office | A registered unemployed person cannot register as a job seeker or at another office | RP art. 57 ust. 1 |

### Registering

| Topic | Content | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| How | On application: online, by the form on the ministry's system (praca.gov.pl), or in person at the PUP of the place of residence | RP art. 55 ust. 1–2 |
| The data | Among others: citizenship; the PESEL, or without one the date and place of birth, sex and the identity document's type, series and number; the address; education, jobs and periods of work; for a foreigner, the status under art. 1 ust. 3 pkt 2–6 | RP art. 55 ust. 3 pkt 2–3, 8, 24 |
| The documents | Online: digital copies of certificates of education, work certificates and other documents needed for the rights; in person: the identity card, or another identity document, and the same documents to be seen | RP art. 55 ust. 5–6 |
| Statements | Able and ready to work, and no steady income, under criminal liability; the statement that one was told the conditions | RP art. 55 ust. 9–10, 60 |
| From which day | Online: the day a complete application is filed; in person: the day of the visit, after signing the data and the statements | RP art. 61 ust. 1 |
| No registration | When data or a signature is refused or missing; the starosta may allow it in especially justified cases without all documents | RP art. 61 ust. 2–3 |
| Signatures | Online: a qualified, trusted or personal signature | RP art. 44 ust. 3, 61 ust. 2 pkt 3 lit. a |
| The account | The individual account may be set up at the PUP with help; logging in by the state login methods or the mObywatel certificate; a foreigner without a PESEL gives an identity document's data or an EU identifier | RP art. 43 ust. 2, 4–5 |
| Letters | With an account, letters are delivered on it, and count as delivered when opened or after 14 days | RP art. 45 ust. 1, 3 |
| Who decides | The starosta (the PUP acts for the starosta); the higher authority is the voivode. A decision removing the status is enforced at once | RP art. 31, 38 ust. 2 |

### Keeping the status

| Topic | Content | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| How long | 3 years from registration, unless lost earlier; again after 90 days, counted anew | RP art. 63 |
| Changes | Notify the PUP of any change in the data or the statements within 7 days, online or in person; and of a job, other paid work or a CEIDG application within 7 days | RP art. 62 ust. 1, 67, 232 |
| Moving | Notify the PUP and name the PUP of the new address | RP art. 62 ust. 3 |
| Removal | Among others: no longer meeting art. 2 pkt 1; refusing or breaking off a form of help by one's own fault (90 days); refusing socially useful work (90 days); illness for 180 days without a break; no sick note; no contact at least once in 90 days (90 days); a trip abroad not notified, or longer than 30 days (90 days); refusing the individual action plan (90 days) | RP art. 65 ust. 1 |
| Abroad | Up to 30 days, notified in advance: the status stays, without the benefit for that time; at most 30 days in a calendar year | RP art. 229 |
| Illness | Notify within 2 days of the sick note and deliver it within 7 days | RP art. 239 ust. 2 |
| Health insurance | Unemployed persons not insured on another title are compulsorily insured for health | The health care act art. 66 ust. 1 pkt 24 (`research/2026-10-17-health/sources/`); PSZ "Prawa i obowiązki" |

### The benefit

| Topic | Content | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Who | An unemployed person who, in the 18 months before registering, for at least 365 days in all, was employed or did other work at least on the minimum wage with Labour Fund contributions due, among other titles | RP art. 218 ust. 1 |
| Not at all | After quitting the last job by notice (except for a move, or a termination under the Labour Code art. 55 § 1 or § 1¹, which the text extraction prints as "§ 1 i 11"), or after a dismissal for one's own fault without notice | RP art. 219 ust. 1 |
| Later | After a severance equivalent, for its period; after 90 days if the job ended by agreement (except for a move, bankruptcy, liquidation or redundancy); among others | RP art. 220 ust. 1–2 |
| How much | From 1.06.2026: 1783,90 zł for the first 90 days, then 1400,90 zł; 120 % after at least 20 years of the counted periods. Indexed each 1 June | RP art. 224 ust. 1–2, 228; M.P. 2026 poz. 268 |
| How long | 180 days; 365 days for, among others, persons with a disability, Large Family Card holders, those over 50 with 20 years, and single parents | RP art. 225 ust. 1 |
| Income and changes | An income statement within 7 days of each income | RP art. 231 |
| Moving to look for work in the EU | The right to the benefit goes with the person under the coordination rules | RP art. 230 |

## Inferences (to be confirmed)

- **A PESEL is not required to register.** The application takes the date and place of birth and an identity document instead (RP art. 55 ust. 3 pkt 3), and the account takes an identity document's data (art. 43 ust. 5 pkt 1 lit. c). Whether a given PUP's online form works without one is practice (OPEN-U1).
- **A residence registration ("meldunek") is not required.** The act asks for the address of residence (RP art. 55 ust. 3 pkt 8) and ties the office to the place of residence (art. 55 ust. 2 pkt 2).
- **Registering as unemployed does not by itself protect a residence permit tied to a job.** The labour market act says nothing on residence. The act on foreigners has its own rules for a lost job (the residence map, FS17; UoC art. 121, 123, 133).
- **For 800+**, someone on the unemployment benefit counts as active, and a registered unemployed person without it gets 3 or 6 months (`maps/child-benefit-800.md`; the 800+ act art. 1 ust. 2b, 2 pkt 1a lit. b).
