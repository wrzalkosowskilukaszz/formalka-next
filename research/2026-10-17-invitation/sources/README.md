# Sources: inviting a foreigner (zaproszenie), doc 19 matter 23

Collected on 17.10.2026 by Claude, for the map `docs/formalka-next/maps/invitation.md`. Level R1 under doc 19: sources only, with provenance. Production's registry has no invitation procedure, so nothing here reaches it.

**Scope:** who may invite a foreigner to Poland, how the invitation is entered in the voivode's register, what it costs, what the inviter must show and promise, and what it does and does not do for the guest.

Not covered: the visa itself (a consul's decision, under the EU Visa Code and UoC art. 58 and after); invitations for work (the work map); the visa-free stay.

## What they say (FACT)

| Topic | Content | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| What it is for | A foreigner may present an invitation as proof of enough money for the planned stay (accommodation, food) and for the return or transit | UoC art. 49 ust. 1 |
| Who may invite | A Polish citizen living in Poland; an EU, EFTA-EEA or Swiss citizen, a Withdrawal Agreement UK citizen, or such a person's family member, living in Poland with a right of residence or permanent residence; a foreigner who has stayed in Poland legally and without a break for at least 5 years right before the invitation, or holds a permanent residence or EU long-term resident permit; a legal person or organisation seated in Poland | UoC art. 49 ust. 2 |
| An unbroken stay | The 5 years count as unbroken if no break was longer than 6 months and all breaks together were at most 10 months, unless a break was for work abroad under a contract with an employer seated in Poland, for accompanying such a person as a spouse or minor child, for a special personal situation of at most 6 months, or for a placement or classes within studies at a Polish university | UoC art. 49 ust. 3; art. 195 ust. 4 |
| Refusal or annulment | Among others: the inviter does not qualify; the guest is on the list of unwanted foreigners; security or the state's interest; the inviter cannot cover the costs; the housing is not adequate and no other accommodation is shown; the inviter did not keep an earlier invitation's promises (if the new application comes within 3 years of the earlier one's end); false data; another purpose than declared | UoC art. 50 |
| Valid | For the period in it, at most 1 year | UoC art. 51 ust. 1 |
| The application | On a form with the inviter's and the guest's data, the number of foreigners invited in the past year, the inviter's means and housing, and a promise to cover the guest's stay, accommodation, food, any medical treatment, the return or transit, and the costs of a return decision; a notice that the invitation is not a document that allows work; a statement under criminal liability | UoC art. 51 ust. 2 |
| Where | The voivode of the inviter's place of residence or seat enters it or refuses it, by decision; the voivode who made the entry annuls it, by decision | UoC art. 51 ust. 3, 52 ust. 1, 1a |
| Annulment on request | On the inviter's request filed at least 7 days before the invitation's validity starts; it lapses when the decision is final | UoC art. 52 ust. 2–3 |
| The documents | The form; proof that the inviter can cover the costs; proof of title to the flat if the guest stays there, or a document confirming accommodation elsewhere; a return ticket counts as proof for the return | UoC art. 53 |
| Collecting it | A proxy with a special power of attorney may collect it | UoC art. 55 |
| The procedure | Only the inviter is a party (entry and annulment). For the entry, the KPA's simplified procedure applies and the tacit settlement does not. Evidence is what the inviter files with the application plus what the office can establish from data it holds; no new demands after an application on the official form; if new circumstances the party raises matter and would lengthen the case, the office continues outside the simplified procedure and tells the party | UoC art. 55a, 55b; KPA art. 163c § 4, 163d, 163e § 1 |
| Time | Promptly, at the latest one month from the start, in the simplified procedure; periods set by law for actions, suspensions and delays caused by the party or beyond the office's control are not counted | KPA art. 35 § 3a, § 5 |
| No suspension | The special act's suspension of time limits until 4.03.2027 covers residence permits handled by the voivode; it names no invitation | Special act art. 100d ust. 1 |
| Filed at the wrong office | An office that is not competent forwards the application promptly to the competent one and tells the person; an application filed there in time counts as filed in time | KPA art. 65 § 1–2 |
| If promises are broken | The State Treasury and others may sue the inviter for the costs of art. 51 ust. 2 pkt 3 they bore in carrying out their statutory duties, except the costs of issuing and enforcing a return decision | UoC art. 56 |
| The costs of a return decision | Set only when the return decision is enforced, by a Border Guard decision that also names who pays; not set when the foreigner got help with a voluntary return. The inviter bears them when the guest entered during the invitation's period and the grounds for the return arose during it, or after it if the guest's visa was not extended, the guest got no residence permit or protection, and stayed. When the return decision was for work without a permit (art. 302 ust. 1 pkt 4), the entity that gave the work bears them | UoC art. 336 ust. 1–2, 337 ust. 2, 4 |
| The inviter's money | At least 515 zł for each month of the guest's stay, for each of: the inviter, each dependent family member and each invited foreigner; reduced in proportion to the days of the stay when the whole invitation period is shorter than a month | Regulation 2023 § 3 |
| For close family | At least 200 zł a month when the guest is the inviter's descendant, ascendant or spouse, the spouse's parent, a sibling, or the spouse's child | Regulation 2023 § 4 |
| The return | The price of the ticket, at least 200 zł to a neighbouring state, 500 zł to another EU or EFTA-EEA state or Switzerland, 2500 zł elsewhere | Regulation 2023 § 5 |
| Fee | 27 zł | Stamp duty act, annex part I item 28 |
| A refund | The fee is refunded when the official act was not done although it was paid, on the payer's request, and not after 5 years from the end of the year of payment. Since 1.10.2026 a justified request is refunded without a decision within 2 months; a request filed before then keeps the old rules | Stamp duty act art. 9 ust. 1 pkt 1, ust. 2–3; Dz.U. 2026 poz. 846 art. 14, 31, 35 |
| A power of attorney | Filing it costs 17 zł for each power; exempt among others: one certified by a notary or an authorised body that authorises collecting documents, and one given to a spouse, ascendant, descendant or sibling | Stamp duty act, annex part IV |

## Inferences (to be confirmed)

- **An invitation does not give a visa or a right to enter;** it is one proof of means that the consul or the Border Guard weighs (art. 49 ust. 1). The app never says "z zaproszeniem dostaniesz wizę".
- **The 5 years count the whole legal stay right before the invitation, not the age of the current card** (art. 49 ust. 2 pkt 2, ust. 3; art. 195 ust. 4). A temporary card held for 2 years settles nothing by itself: the app asks for the whole legal stay and its breaks.
- **For a parent's 2-month visit, § 4 asks for at least 400 zł (2 × 200 zł) for the parent, plus the return ticket's price, not less than § 5's amount.** Whether the inviter's own and the dependants' 515 zł a month (§ 3) are needed on top is OPEN-I2.
- **The guest must hold the invitation to present it** (art. 49 ust. 1: "Cudzoziemiec może przedstawić zaproszenie"), so the inviter passes it on; the act says nothing of how.
- **A refused entry is a case for the 27 zł refund:** the official act (the entry) was not done although the fee was paid (stamp duty art. 9 ust. 1 pkt 1).
- **A power of attorney only to collect the invitation may be exempt from the 17 zł when a notary or an authorised body certified it** (annex part IV, column 4, pkt 1: "upoważniające do odbioru dokumentów"); the office decides.
- **Filing in another voivodeship does not lose the application:** that office forwards it (KPA art. 65 § 1), at the cost of time.
