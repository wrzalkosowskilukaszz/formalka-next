# Sources: inviting a foreigner (zaproszenie), doc 19 matter 23

Collected on 17.10.2026 by Claude, for the map `docs/formalka-next/maps/invitation.md`. Level R1 under doc 19: sources only, with provenance. Production's registry has no invitation procedure, so nothing here reaches it.

**Scope:** who may invite a foreigner to Poland, how the invitation is entered in the voivode's register, what it costs, what the inviter must show and promise, and what it does and does not do for the guest.

Not covered: the visa itself (a consul's decision, under the EU Visa Code and UoC art. 58 and after); invitations for work (the work map); the visa-free stay.

**Not yet checked by the separate checking agent** (`.claude/agents/official-fact-checker.md`): that check comes before anything here is relied on.

## What they say (FACT)

| Topic | Content | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| What it is for | A foreigner may present an invitation as proof of enough money for the planned stay (accommodation, food) and for the return or transit | UoC art. 49 ust. 1 |
| Who may invite | A Polish citizen living in Poland; an EU, EFTA-EEA or Swiss citizen, a Withdrawal Agreement UK citizen, or such a person's family member, living in Poland with a right of residence or permanent residence; a foreigner who has stayed in Poland legally and without a break for at least 5 years right before, or holds a permanent residence or EU long-term resident permit; a legal person or organisation seated in Poland | UoC art. 49 ust. 2–3 |
| Refusal or annulment | Among others: the inviter does not qualify; the guest is on the list of unwanted foreigners; security or the state's interest; the inviter cannot cover the costs; the housing is not adequate and no other accommodation is shown; the inviter did not keep an earlier invitation's promises (if the new application comes within 3 years of the earlier one's end); false data; another purpose than declared | UoC art. 50 |
| Valid | For the period in it, at most 1 year | UoC art. 51 ust. 1 |
| The application | On a form with the inviter's and the guest's data, the number of foreigners invited in the past year, the inviter's means and housing, and a promise to cover the guest's stay, accommodation, food, any medical treatment, the return or transit, and the costs of a return decision; a notice that the invitation is not a work permit; a statement under criminal liability | UoC art. 51 ust. 2 |
| Where | The voivode of the inviter's place of residence or seat enters it, refuses by decision, or annuls it | UoC art. 51 ust. 3, 52 |
| Annulment on request | On the inviter's request filed at least 7 days before the invitation's validity starts; it lapses when the decision is final | UoC art. 52 ust. 2–3 |
| The documents | The form; proof that the inviter can cover the costs; proof of title to the flat if the guest stays there, or of accommodation elsewhere; a return ticket counts as proof for the return | UoC art. 53 |
| Collecting it | A proxy with a special power of attorney may collect it | UoC art. 55 |
| The procedure | Only the inviter is a party; the KPA's simplified procedure applies (evidence limited to what the inviter gives with the application; no new demands later); the tacit settlement does not | UoC art. 55a, 55b; KPA art. 163b–163g |
| If promises are broken | The State Treasury and others may sue the inviter for the costs they bore (not those of a return decision) | UoC art. 56 |
| The inviter's money | At least 515 zł for each month of the guest's stay, for each of: the inviter, each dependent family member and each invited foreigner; pro rata for less than a month | Regulation 2023 § 3 |
| For close family | At least 200 zł a month when the guest is the inviter's descendant, ascendant or spouse, the spouse's parent, a sibling, or the spouse's child | Regulation 2023 § 4 |
| The return | The price of the ticket, at least 200 zł to a neighbouring state, 500 zł to another EU or EFTA-EEA state or Switzerland, 2500 zł elsewhere | Regulation 2023 § 5 |
| Fee | 27 zł | Stamp duty act, annex part I item 28 |

## Inferences (to be confirmed)

- **An invitation does not give a visa or a right to enter;** it is one proof of means that the consul or the Border Guard weighs (art. 49 ust. 1). The app never says "z zaproszeniem dostaniesz wizę".
- **A foreigner with a temporary permit and less than 5 years in Poland cannot invite** (art. 49 ust. 2 pkt 2).
- **For a parent's 2-month visit, § 4 asks for at least 400 zł (2 × 200 zł) for the parent, plus the return ticket's price, not less than § 5's amount.** Whether the inviter's own and the dependants' 515 zł a month (§ 3) are needed on top is OPEN-I2.
