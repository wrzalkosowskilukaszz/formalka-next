## What they say (FACT)

"The act" is Dz.U. 2025 poz. 1461 with the changes listed in its extract's header.

| Topic | Content | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Who has the right | The insured (mandatory and voluntary). Some uninsured residents: Polish citizens, refugees, people with subsidiary protection, or with a family permit under foreigners act art. 159 ust. 1 pkt 1 lit. c or d, who meet the social-assistance income test. Also children under 18 and people in pregnancy, birth or the postnatal period, in those same groups. Other non-citizens: "na zasadach określonych w przepisach odrębnych i umowach międzynarodowych" | Art. 2 ust. 1–2 |
| The municipality's decision | For the uninsured residents of art. 2 ust. 1 pkt 2, the document is a decision of the head of the municipality where they live. It is issued on application (in an emergency, on the provider's), after documents on residence and status, a family interview by social assistance, and the income test | Art. 54 ust. 1–4 |
| Which foreigners are insured | Non-EU persons with a work visa, a temporary permit **except one under foreigners act art. 181 ust. 1 (a short stay)**, a permanent or EU long-term permit, humanitarian or tolerated stay, or a humanitarian or national-interest visa. Also refugees, people with subsidiary or temporary protection, and asylum applicants with the art. 35 certificate. In each case **only if they are subject to mandatory insurance under art. 66 or insure voluntarily under art. 68** | Art. 3 ust. 1 pkt 2, 2a, 2b, closing words |
| Any non-EU person who works | Insured whenever subject to mandatory insurance under art. 66 ust. 1 pkt 1 (employees, contractors, sole traders and others), whatever their residence title | Art. 3 ust. 1a |
| Foreign students | Non-EU students, doctoral students and interns doing a mandatory traineeship, who are not covered as EU residents or by the Karta Polaka rule (art. 3 ust. 1 pkt 3a): insured **only if they insure voluntarily** under art. 68. The university does not report them: art. 66 ust. 1 pkt 20 excludes them | Art. 3 ust. 2 pkt 1; art. 66 ust. 1 pkt 20 |
| Karta Polaka, Polish origin | Students, interns and language-course participants who hold a valid Karta Polaka or are recognised as of Polish origin: insured if mandatory (the university reports them) or voluntary | Art. 3 ust. 1 pkt 3a; art. 66 ust. 1 pkt 20, 20a |
| Family members | A child (to 18; to 26 while in school or university; with no age limit if severely disabled), a spouse, and ascendants living in the same household. Family members living in Poland are insured through a person in art. 3 ust. 1 pkt 2 or 2a or ust. 1a, unless they must be insured themselves | Art. 5 pkt 3; art. 3 ust. 2 pkt 6 |
| Everyone else | "Ubezpieczeniu nie podlegają, z zastrzeżeniem art. 3, cudzoziemcy przebywający na terytorium Rzeczypospolitej Polskiej", unless a ratified agreement says otherwise | Art. 4 |
| When mandatory insurance counts | After the person is reported and the contribution paid. The right to care follows the report | Art. 67 ust. 1–2 |
| Reporting the family | The insured person must report family members. One who does not report themselves tells the payer (usually the employer) **within 7 days**. Family members have the right from the day of the report | Art. 67 ust. 3, 3a |
| When the right ends | **30 days after the duty ends** (for example, a job ends). After a degree or being struck off the student list, 4 months; after secondary school, 6 months | Art. 67 ust. 4–5 |
| Who reports whom | An employer reports its employees under the social insurance rules. Those not reported by someone else report themselves | Art. 74 ust. 1; art. 76 |
| Voluntary insurance | Anyone not in art. 66 ust. 1 "może ubezpieczyć się dobrowolnie na podstawie pisemnego wniosku złożonego w Funduszu, **jeżeli ma miejsce zamieszkania na terytorium Rzeczypospolitej Polskiej**". After mandatory insurance ends, anyone may do so | Art. 68 ust. 1; art. 69 ust. 4 |
| Its contribution base | Declared monthly income, **at least the average wage** (art. 5 pkt 31: the enterprise-sector average of the previous quarter, announced by GUS in Monitor Polski). For the students and others in art. 3 ust. 2 pkt 1–4: **the amount of the special care allowance** ("specjalny zasiłek opiekuńczy") | Art. 68 ust. 4 pkt 1, 3 |
| The rate | 9% of the base (with exceptions for some businesses) | Art. 79 ust. 1 |
| Cover dates | From the day set in the agreement with the NFZ, to its end, or **after one month of unbroken arrears**. The right to care lasts 30 days after the cover ends | Art. 68 ust. 5, 12 |
| The entry fee | Due after a gap in insurance: 20% of the base for 3 months to a year, 50% for 1 to 2 years, 100% for 2 to 5, 150% for 5 to 10, 200% over 10. **None for art. 3 ust. 2 persons** (foreign students and others). The NFZ may waive it or split it into up to 12 monthly payments | Art. 68 ust. 7–9, 11 |
| Family on voluntary insurance | Reported to the NFZ within 7 days of the agreement's date | Art. 68 ust. 3 |
| Showing the right at a clinic (from 1.07.2026) | The patient gains access when both: they prove identity (ID card, passport, driving licence, a school ID under 18, or the mObywatel document), and the clinic confirms the right **"na podstawie numeru PESEL"** in the NFZ's electronic system (eWUŚ) | Art. 50 ust. 2–3, opening as changed by 2026/791. The health insurance card (art. 49) was repealed by 2026/791 |
| If the system does not confirm | Another document confirming the right, or a **written statement** with the clause "Posiadam prawo do korzystania ze świadczeń opieki zdrowotnej finansowanych ze środków publicznych". For people without a PESEL, the statement holds the data in art. 188 ust. 4 pkt 9 instead | Art. 50 ust. 6–8 |
| A false statement | The person pays for the care, unless they were "w usprawiedliwionym błędnym przekonaniu". If the cause was a missing report, reporting within 30 days removes the charge | Art. 50 ust. 16–18b |
| Emergencies | Care is given without confirmation. Afterwards the person shows a document or makes the statement within 14 days, or 7 days after a hospital stay ends, or may be charged | Art. 50 ust. 11 |
| A baby without a PESEL | Up to 3 months old, confirmed by the PESEL of the person who must report the child | Art. 50 ust. 14 |
| People insured in another EU or EFTA state, or the UK | They use their state's document (the EHIC, or a certificate the NFZ issues to a person living in Poland: "poświadczenie") | Art. 52, 53 |
| Temporary protection with PESEL UKR (from 5.03.2026) | Without insurance, care as for the insured goes only to four groups: victims of torture or rape (with a UdSC certificate), under 18, residents of collective accommodation (with a certificate), and pregnancy, birth or the postnatal period. Some treatments are excluded. In a sudden threat to health, every PESEL UKR holder gets care "niezwłocznie w niezbędnym zakresie" | Protection act art. 112c ust. 1, 3–4 (added by 2026/203). The old rule (special act art. 37) was removed from art. 12 by 2026/203 art. 7 |
| Hospital treatment begun before 5.03.2026 | May continue until discharge, but not past 4.03.2027 | 2026/203 art. 22 |
| The CUKR card | "taki sam dostęp do świadczeń opieki zdrowotnej, jak wszyscy cudzoziemcy, którzy przebywają w Polsce na podstawie zezwolenia na pobyt czasowy" | UdSC CUKR Q&A |
| Residence permits require insurance | Work: insurance under the act, or an insurer's confirmation of cover; it also counts if the person **will** be insured through that work. Study and family reunification: insurance under the act or an insurer's cover | Foreigners act art. 114 ust. 1 pkt 1 and ust. 4a; art. 144 ust. 1 pkt 2 lit. a; art. 159 ust. 1 pkt 2 lit. a |

## Inferences (to be confirmed)

- **A permit alone does not insure.** A non-EU person with a temporary permit who neither works nor is a family member of an insured person is not insured: art. 3 ust. 1 pkt 2 requires art. 66 or art. 68. They may insure voluntarily if they live in Poland (art. 68 ust. 1).
- **The short-stay permit is outside art. 3 ust. 1 pkt 2.** A person with a permit under foreigners act art. 181 ust. 1 is insured only through work (art. 3 ust. 1a). Whether they may insure voluntarily turns on "miejsce zamieszkania" (OPEN-H2).
- **A foreign non-EU student without the Karta Polaka needs their own agreement.** It has the lower base and no entry fee (art. 68 ust. 4 pkt 3, ust. 9). Otherwise they are covered by an employment contract or as a family member.
- **A student under 26 on a civil-law contract may have no insurance from it.** Mandatory insurance under art. 66 ust. 1 pkt 1 needs the conditions for social insurance. The archival page says the client will not report such a student. The social insurance act's rule for students is not collected (OPEN-H3).
- **Without a PESEL, the clinic's system cannot confirm the right.** Confirmation is "na podstawie numeru PESEL" (art. 50 ust. 3). Such a person shows a document (an employer's certificate, the NFZ agreement with a payment proof) or makes the written statement (art. 50 ust. 6, 8). This links the matter to the PESEL case.
- **The residence card is not on the clinic's identity list.** Art. 50 ust. 2 pkt 1 names the ID card, the passport, the driving licence, the school ID and the mObywatel document. A foreigner would show the passport; whether clinics accept the residence card is OPEN-H6.
- **An adult with PESEL UKR outside the four groups is not covered without insurance since 5.03.2026,** except in a sudden threat to health. Work or a voluntary agreement insures them (art. 3 ust. 1 pkt 2a; protection act art. 112c).
- **The CUKR card ends the art. 112c groups.** Art. 112c covers holders of a PESEL with the UKR status, and the CUKR card changes the status (UdSC Q&A). The CUKR holder is then insured through work, family or a voluntary agreement, like any permit holder.
