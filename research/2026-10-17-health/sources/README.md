## What they say (FACT)

"The act" is Dz.U. 2025 poz. 1461 with the changes listed in its extract's header.

| Topic | Content | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Who has the right | The insured (mandatory and voluntary). Some uninsured persons "posiadające miejsce zamieszkania na terytorium Rzeczypospolitej Polskiej": Polish citizens, refugees, people with subsidiary protection, or with a family permit under foreigners act art. 159 ust. 1 pkt 1 lit. c or d, who meet the social-assistance income test and in whose case the circumstance of art. 12 of the social assistance act was not found. Also, **with no income test**, children under 18 and people in pregnancy, birth or the postnatal period, in those groups (Polish children with no residence condition), and some children receiving a family pension who live in an EU or EFTA state or the UK. Other non-citizens: "na zasadach określonych w przepisach odrębnych i umowach międzynarodowych" | Art. 2 ust. 1–2 |
| The municipality's decision | For the uninsured residents of art. 2 ust. 1 pkt 2, the document is a decision of the head of the municipality where they live; it "powinna zawierać numer PESEL". It is issued on application (in an emergency, on the provider's), or ex officio on the NFZ's application, after documents on residence and status, a family interview, the income test and the art. 12 social assistance check. **The right lasts 90 days** from the day in the decision (the application, or the emergency care), unless the person becomes insured meanwhile | Art. 54 ust. 1–5, 7–8 |
| Which foreigners are insured | Non-EU persons with a work visa, a temporary permit **except one under foreigners act art. 181 ust. 1 (a short stay)**, a permanent or EU long-term permit, humanitarian or tolerated stay, or a visa for humanitarian reasons, the state's interest or international obligations. Also refugees, people with subsidiary or temporary protection, and asylum applicants with the art. 35 certificate. In each case **only if they are subject to mandatory insurance under art. 66 or insure voluntarily under art. 68** | Art. 3 ust. 1 pkt 2, 2a, 2b, closing words |
| Any non-EU person who works | Insured whenever subject to mandatory insurance under art. 66 ust. 1 pkt 1 (employees, contractors, sole traders and others), whatever their residence title | Art. 3 ust. 1a |
| Foreign students | Non-EU students, doctoral students and interns doing a mandatory traineeship (other than non-EU persons living legally in another EU or EFTA state or the UK, art. 3 ust. 1 pkt 3, and the Karta Polaka group, pkt 3a): **on this title, insured only if they insure voluntarily** under art. 68, which needs residence in Poland (art. 68 ust. 1). The university does not report them: art. 66 ust. 1 pkt 20 excludes them. They are insured on any other title: work (art. 3 ust. 1 pkt 2 or ust. 1a with art. 66) or as a family member (ust. 2 pkt 6) | Art. 3 ust. 2 pkt 1; art. 66 ust. 1 pkt 20; art. 68 ust. 1 |
| Karta Polaka, Polish origin | Students, interns and language-course participants who hold a valid Karta Polaka or are recognised as of Polish origin: insured if mandatory or voluntary. The duty arises "z dniem złożenia oświadczenia o niepodleganiu obowiązkowi ubezpieczenia zdrowotnego z innego tytułu", and the university reports them. Family-member status exempts from these titles | Art. 3 ust. 1 pkt 3a; art. 66 ust. 1 pkt 20, 20a, ust. 2; art. 73 pkt 4; art. 75 ust. 4 |
| Family members | A child (to 18; to 26 while in school or university; with no age limit if severely disabled), a spouse, and ascendants living in the same household. Family members living in Poland are insured through a person in art. 3 ust. 1 pkt 2 or 2a or ust. 1a, unless they must be insured themselves | Art. 5 pkt 3; art. 3 ust. 2 pkt 6 |
| Everyone else | "Ubezpieczeniu nie podlegają, z zastrzeżeniem art. 3, cudzoziemcy przebywający na terytorium Rzeczypospolitej Polskiej", unless a ratified agreement says otherwise | Art. 4 |
| When mandatory insurance counts | After the person is reported and the contribution paid. The right to care follows the report | Art. 67 ust. 1–2 |
| Reporting the family | The insured person must report family members. One who does not report themselves tells the payer **within 7 days** ("najczęściej pracodawcy", the NFZ page), and the payer reports them. Grandparents may report a grandchild (ust. 3a). Family members have the right from the day of the report | Art. 67 ust. 3, 3a; NFZ page |
| When the right ends | **30 days after the duty ends** (for example, a job ends). After a degree or being struck off the student list, 4 months; after secondary school, 6 months. The right continues, with the family, while a sickness or accident benefit is paid (ust. 6) and while a pension application is pending (ust. 7); a regulation may extend it in an epidemic (ust. 8). For voluntary insurance: art. 68 ust. 12 | Art. 67 ust. 4–8 |
| Who reports whom | Persons in social insurance are reported under the social insurance rules (art. 74 ust. 1); the NFZ page: "dla pracowników - pracodawca". Schools and universities report pupils and students (art. 75 ust. 4). Those not reported by someone else report themselves | Art. 74 ust. 1; 75 ust. 4; 76; NFZ page |
| Voluntary insurance | Anyone not in art. 66 ust. 1 "może ubezpieczyć się dobrowolnie na podstawie pisemnego wniosku złożonego w Funduszu, **jeżeli ma miejsce zamieszkania na terytorium Rzeczypospolitej Polskiej**". After mandatory insurance ends, "każda osoba może ubezpieczyć się na zasadach określonych w art. 68", so with that residence condition | Art. 68 ust. 1; art. 69 ust. 4 |
| Its contribution base | Declared monthly income, **at least the average wage** (art. 5 pkt 31: the enterprise-sector average of the previous quarter, announced by GUS in Monitor Polski). For the students and others in art. 3 ust. 2 pkt 1–4: **the amount of the special care allowance** ("specjalny zasiłek opiekuńczy") | Art. 68 ust. 4 pkt 1, 3 |
| The rate | 9% of the base, "z zastrzeżeniem art. 79a, art. 80, art. 82 i art. 242" (art. 79a and 80 are other rates for some businesses and farmers; 82 and 242 not read) | Art. 79 ust. 1 |
| Cover dates | From the day set in the agreement with the NFZ, to its end, or **after one month of unbroken arrears**. The right to care lasts 30 days after the cover ends | Art. 68 ust. 5, 12 |
| The entry fee | Due after a gap in insurance: 20% of the base for 3 months to a year, 50% for 1 to 2 years, 100% for 2 to 5, 150% for 5 to 10, 200% over 10. **None for art. 3 ust. 2 persons** (foreign students and others). "W uzasadnionych przypadkach, na wniosek", the NFZ may waive it or split it into up to 12 instalments | Art. 68 ust. 7–9, 11 |
| Family on voluntary insurance | Reported to the NFZ within 7 days of the agreement's date, or of the circumstance that requires the report | Art. 68 ust. 3 |
| Showing the right at a clinic (from 1.07.2026) | The patient gains access when both: they prove identity (ID card, passport, driving licence, a school ID under 18, or the mObywatel document), and the clinic confirms the right **"na podstawie numeru PESEL"** in the NFZ's electronic system (the NFZ page calls it eWUŚ; the act does not name it). Telemedicine and the patient account have their own routes (ust. 2a–2b). Uninsured persons of art. 2 ust. 1 pkt 2 still show the municipality's decision (ust. 1 pkt 2) | Art. 50 ust. 1 pkt 2, ust. 2–3 (ust. 2 opening as changed by 2026/791). The health insurance card (art. 49) was repealed by 2026/791 |
| If the system does not confirm | After showing the identity document, another document confirming the right; **and only if the person has none**, a written statement with the clause "Posiadam prawo do korzystania ze świadczeń opieki zdrowotnej finansowanych ze środków publicznych". For people without a PESEL, the statement holds the data in art. 188 ust. 4 pkt 9 instead (the series and number of the ID card, passport or another identity document). In in-patient care the statement covers the stay, at most to the end of the following month (ust. 7a) | Art. 50 ust. 6–8, 7a; art. 188 ust. 4 pkt 9 |
| A statement made without the right | The person pays for the care, **except primary care (POZ)**, unless they were "w usprawiedliwionym błędnym przekonaniu". If the cause was a missing report, reporting within 30 days of the care, or of the NFZ's notice of proceedings, removes the charge. The NFZ's President may waive, defer or split the debt, and **opens no proceedings for 500 zł or less** | Art. 50 ust. 16 (with art. 15 ust. 2 pkt 1), 17, 18a–18b, 22, 23 |
| Emergencies | Care is given without confirmation, also when the person's health prevents a statement. Afterwards the person shows a document or makes the statement within 14 days of the start of care, or 7 days after a hospital stay ends, or may be charged. A document or statement given **up to 1 year after those periods** still may not be a reason to refuse relief from the costs or their refund | Art. 50 ust. 11–12 |
| A baby without a PESEL | Up to 3 months old, confirmed by the PESEL of the person who must report the child | Art. 50 ust. 14 |
| People insured in another EU or EFTA state, or the UK | The NFZ issues the person entitled under the coordination rules a "poświadczenie"; that or a document of the foreign competent institution confirms the right (art. 53 ust. 1); in an emergency or at a birth without it, art. 50 ust. 11–15 and 18–22 apply (ust. 2). The NFZ page names the EHIC and gives the poświadczenie "w przypadku zamieszkiwania na terenie RP"; the act sets no such condition | Art. 52 ust. 1, 53 ust. 1–2; NFZ page |
| Temporary protection with PESEL UKR (from 5.03.2026) | Without insurance, care as for the insured goes only to four groups: victims of torture or rape (with a UdSC certificate), under 18, residents of collective accommodation (with a certificate), and pregnancy, birth or the postnatal period. Some treatments are excluded. In a sudden threat to health, every PESEL UKR holder gets care "niezwłocznie w niezbędnym zakresie" | Protection act art. 112c ust. 1, 3–4 (added by 2026/203). Also: war-injured foreigners brought to Poland for treatment (ust. 2), and no PESEL UKR document is needed when the right is confirmed under art. 50 ust. 3 (ust. 5). The old rule was removed from art. 12 by 2026/203 art. 7, and special act art. 34–37 were repealed by 2026/203 art. 17 pkt 17, both from 5.03.2026 |
| Hospital treatment begun before 5.03.2026 | A citizen of Ukraine treated in hospital under special act art. 37 may continue until discharge, but not past 4.03.2027 | 2026/203 art. 22 |
| The CUKR card | "taki sam dostęp do świadczeń opieki zdrowotnej, jak wszyscy cudzoziemcy, którzy przebywają w Polsce na podstawie zezwolenia na pobyt czasowy" | UdSC CUKR Q&A; special act art. 42r ust. 1 |
| Residence permits require insurance | The temporary residence and work permit (art. 114): insurance under the act, or an insurer's confirmation of cover; it also counts if the person **will** be insured through that work. Study and family reunification: insurance under the act or an insurer's cover | Foreigners act art. 114 ust. 1 pkt 1 and ust. 4a; art. 144 ust. 1 pkt 2 lit. a; art. 159 ust. 1 pkt 2 lit. a |
| The NFZ's steps for voluntary insurance | Go to the NFZ voivodeship branch, fill the application, sign two copies of the agreement. Bring an identity document and a proof of the last insurance period. Then file ZUS ZZA at ZUS, and ZUS ZCNA for family members | NFZ page |
| eWUŚ problems | The most common cause is a missing report: the employer did not report the employee, or the workplace changed its form. Other possible causes: errors in the report, and unpaid contributions (the voluntarily insured, business owners). For family members, the person did not ask the payer to report them. A student who worked on an employment contract is deregistered and must be reported again | NFZ page |
| Help | NFZ helpline 800 190 590; from abroad +48 22 125 66 00 | NFZ page |
| Checking one's own right | The patient account (IKP), "Moje konto", "Twoje ubezpieczenie zdrowotne" | NFZ page |
| Foreign students (the archival page) | Sign a voluntary agreement with the NFZ branch for one's place of residence. Bring the passport with the residence document (visa, card) and proof of student status | pacjent.gov.pl (archival) |

## Inferences (to be confirmed)

- **A permit alone does not insure.** A non-EU person with a temporary permit who neither works nor is a family member of an insured person is not insured: art. 3 ust. 1 pkt 2 requires art. 66 or art. 68. They may insure voluntarily if they live in Poland (art. 68 ust. 1).
- **The short-stay permit is outside art. 3 ust. 1 pkt 2.** A person with a permit under foreigners act art. 181 ust. 1 is insured through work (art. 3 ust. 1a), and possibly as the family member of an insured person living in Poland (art. 3 ust. 2 pkt 6 does not exclude them; the residence question is OPEN-H2). Whether they may insure voluntarily also turns on "miejsce zamieszkania" (OPEN-H2).
- **A foreign non-EU student without the Karta Polaka needs their own agreement.** It has the lower base and no entry fee (art. 68 ust. 4 pkt 3, ust. 9). Otherwise they are covered by an employment contract or as a family member.
- **A student under 26 on a civil-law contract may have no insurance from it.** Mandatory insurance under art. 66 ust. 1 pkt 1 needs the conditions for social insurance. The archival page says the client will not report such a student. The social insurance act's rule for students is not collected (OPEN-H3).
- **Without a PESEL, the clinic's system cannot confirm the right.** Confirmation is "na podstawie numeru PESEL" (art. 50 ust. 3). Such a person shows a document (an employer's certificate, the NFZ agreement with a payment proof) or makes the written statement (art. 50 ust. 6, 8). This links the matter to the PESEL case.
- **The residence card is not on the clinic's identity list.** Art. 50 ust. 2 pkt 1 names the ID card, the passport, the driving licence, the school ID and the mObywatel document. A foreigner would show the passport; whether clinics accept the residence card is OPEN-H6.
- **An adult with PESEL UKR outside the four groups is not covered without insurance since 5.03.2026,** except in a sudden threat to health. Work or a voluntary agreement insures them (art. 3 ust. 1 pkt 2a; protection act art. 112c).
- **The CUKR card ends the art. 112c groups.** Art. 112c covers holders of a PESEL with the UKR status, and the CUKR card changes the status (UdSC Q&A). The CUKR holder is then insured through work, family or a voluntary agreement, like any permit holder.
