### The work permit, seen from the person's side

| Topic | Content | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Who applies | The employer, online only, through praca.gov.pl; an application filed otherwise is left unexamined. The employer is the only party to the proceedings | WA art. 8 ust. 1–3, art. 12 pkt 1 |
| Who issues | The voivode of the employer's seat or permanent residence | WA art. 26 ust. 1 |
| For how long | At most 3 years; at most 1 year when the employer has run the business for less than a year or the work is at most half time or 20 hours a week | WA art. 15, 34 ust. 1 |
| Conditions | Pay not lower than that of comparable workers and not lower than the minimum wage; between a quarter and full time | WA art. 30 ust. 1 |
| What it names | The employer, the person, the position or type of work, the hours, the lowest pay, the type of contract, and its validity | WA art. 32 ust. 1 |
| Refused because of the person | Among others: the person lacks the qualifications for a regulated profession; a final conviction for document offences; an entry in the list of unwanted foreigners; within 2 years before the application, the person had a permit or declaration, came to Poland to work and did not work, unless for justified reasons | WA art. 13 ust. 1 pkt 2, 10 |
| What the employer must do for the person | Put the permit's terms in the contract; send the contract (in Polish) to the office before work starts; pay at least the minimum wage; raise pay with the hours; inform the person of the steps in the proceedings; hand the person the decisions to issue, change or revoke the permit, and the refusal; pay any wages owed | WA art. 17 ust. 1 pkt 1–7, ust. 5 (pkt 2 as given by 734 art. 5, from 20.06.2026) |
| No new permit needed | A change of the employer's seat, name or legal form; a transfer of the workplace; a new job title with the same duties; other work for up to 30 days a year after notice; changes under the Labour Code art. 91 or 231a; more hours, up to full time, with pay raised in proportion; and since 8.07.2026 an employment contract in place of a civil contract, or the labour inspection's finding of an employment relationship | WA art. 18 ust. 1, art. 33 (ust. 2 pkt 3 from 2026/473 art. 8) |
| The employer's notices | Within 7 days, if the person did not start within 2 months of the permit's start, stopped for more than 2 months, or ended more than 2 months early | WA art. 19, 20 ust. 1 |
| Revocation | Among others, when the office receives those notices (unless the employer explains and declares the permit will be used), or the employer gave false data | WA art. 22 ust. 1, 3 |
| Continuity | If the employer applies for the next permit for the same position before the current one ends, with no formal gaps, the work is legal until the decision is final (periods suspended at a party's request do not count). The same applies when the person applies for a temporary residence and work permit to continue that work | WA art. 21 ust. 1, 3 |
| When it ends by law | When the person gets an EU long-term resident or a permanent residence permit, or a temporary residence and work permit for the same employer and position | WA art. 24 |
| The contract's force | The duty to work lapses for the time the person does not meet the conditions to work; both sides keep a claim for damages where a refusal or revocation came from a lack of due care | WA art. 25 |
| Fees (paid by the employer) | Work permit 200 zł (up to 3 months) or 400 zł (more than 3 months); posting 800 zł; seasonal work 100 zł; declaration 400 zł | Regulation 2025/1622 § 2 |

## Inferences (to be confirmed)

- **A tourist visa never allows work, even with a permit,** because FA art. 60 ust. 1 pkt 1 is not in WA art. 3 ust. 3 pkt 1. A visa-free stay does allow work with a permit or declaration, except for the three states listed.
- **The person cannot apply for a work permit or a declaration,** because the employer applies and is the only party (WA art. 8 ust. 1, 12 pkt 1, 67 ust. 2 pkt 1). What the person can do is check the documents the employer must hand over (art. 17 ust. 1 pkt 7, 68 ust. 1 pkt 5) and, possibly, the individual account (OPEN-W1).
- **A change of employer needs a new permit or declaration,** because both are issued for a named employer and position (art. 32, 62) and art. 18, 33 and 69 list the changes that do not need one; a new employer is not among them.
- **The legality of work turns on two things at once:** a stay basis that allows work, and a permit, declaration or exemption. Either alone is not enough (art. 2 pkt 3, art. 3 ust. 3).
