### The work permit, seen from the person's side

| Topic | Content | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Who applies | The employer, online only, through praca.gov.pl; an application filed otherwise is left unexamined. The employer is the only party to the proceedings | WA art. 8 ust. 1–3, art. 12 pkt 1. The act names the system of the labour market act art. 26 ust. 1 pkt 7 lit. b; "praca.gov.pl" is the voivode's page's name for it |
| Who issues | The voivode of the employer's seat or permanent residence | WA art. 26 ust. 1 |
| For how long | At most 3 years; at most 1 year when the employer has run the business for less than a year or the work is at most half time or 20 hours a week | WA art. 15, 34 ust. 1. A temporary agency worker's permit runs at most as long as the temporary work act art. 20 allows (art. 34 ust. 2) |
| Conditions | Pay not lower than that of comparable workers and not lower than the minimum wage; between a quarter and full time | WA art. 30 ust. 1 |
| What it names | The employer, the person, the position or type of work, the hours, the lowest pay, the type of contract, and its validity | WA art. 32 ust. 1 |
| Refused because of the person | Among others: the person lacks the qualifications for a regulated profession; a final conviction for document offences; an entry in the list of unwanted foreigners; within 2 years before the application, the person had a permit or declaration, came to Poland to work and did not work, unless for justified reasons | WA art. 13 ust. 1 pkt 2, 10 |
| What the employer must do for the person | Put the permit's terms in the contract; send the contract (in Polish) to the office before work starts; pay at least the minimum wage; raise pay with the hours; inform the person of the steps in the proceedings; hand the person the decisions to issue, change or revoke the permit, and the refusal; pay any wages owed | WA art. 17 ust. 1 pkt 1–7, ust. 5 (pkt 2 as given by 734 art. 5, from 20.06.2026) |
| No new permit needed | A change of the employer's seat, name or legal form; a transfer of the workplace; a new job title with the same duties; other work for up to 30 days a year after notice; changes under the Labour Code art. 91 or 231a; more hours, up to full time, with pay raised in proportion; and since 8.07.2026 an employment contract in place of a civil contract, or the labour inspection's finding of an employment relationship | WA art. 18 ust. 1, art. 33 (ust. 2 pkt 3 from 2026/473 art. 8) |
| The employer's notices | Within 7 days, if the person did not start within 2 months of the permit's start, stopped for more than 2 months, or ended more than 2 months early | WA art. 19, 20 ust. 1 |
| Revocation | Among others, when the office receives those notices, or the employer gave false data. The exception (the employer explains and declares the permit will be used) covers only not starting and stopping (art. 19 pkt 1–2), not an early end | WA art. 22 ust. 1, 3 |
| Continuity | If, while the permit is valid, the employer applies for the next permit for the same position or type of work, and the application has no formal gaps or they are filled in time, the work on the current permit's terms is legal from its end until the decision is final (periods suspended at a party's request do not count). The same applies when the person applies, to continue that work, for a temporary residence permit under FA art. 114 ust. 1 or 1a, 126 ust. 1, 127, 137a or 142 ust. 3, or for an EU long-term resident permit | WA art. 21 ust. 1, 3 |
| When it ends by law | When the person gets an EU long-term resident or a permanent residence permit, or a temporary residence permit under FA art. 114 ust. 1, 126 ust. 1 and 3 or 127 for work with the same employer and position | WA art. 24 |
| The contract's force | The duty to work lapses for the time the person does not meet the conditions to work; both sides keep a claim for damages where a refusal or revocation came from a lack of due care, unless special rules or the contract say otherwise | WA art. 25 |
| Fees (paid by the employer) | Work permit 200 zł (up to 3 months) or 400 zł (more than 3 months); posting 800 zł; seasonal work 100 zł; declaration 400 zł | Regulation 2025/1622 § 2; WA art. 10 ust. 1, 63 ust. 1 |

### The declaration (oświadczenie)

| Topic | Content | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| For whom | Citizens of Armenia, Belarus, Moldova and Ukraine (since 1.12.2025). A Georgian citizen working on a declaration entered before then may continue until it ends. Ukrainian citizens staying legally also have free access with a notice (above) | WA art. 3 ust. 8 pkt 1, 61 ust. 1 pkt 1; regulation 2025/1617 § 2–3; 2026/203 art. 41 |
| Conditions | Filed by the employer online; work in Poland, not seasonal, not a board function; at most 24 months; start no later than 6 months after filing | WA art. 61 ust. 1 |
| Entry | The starosta of the employer's seat enters it within 7 working days, or 30 days if the case needs explanation; the starosta may set a later start, not before the day after the entry. A refusal is a decision. Only the employer is a party. The office does not have to notify a delay under KPA art. 37 (art. 67 ust. 1; for permits, art. 7 ust. 4) | WA art. 64, 65 ust. 5, 66, 67 ust. 1, ust. 2 pkt 1; art. 7 ust. 4 |
| What the employer must do for the person | Put its terms in the contract; send the contract to the office before work starts; pay at least the minimum wage; raise pay with the hours; **hand the person the entered declaration**; pay any wages owed | WA art. 68 ust. 1 pkt 1–5, ust. 3 (pkt 2 from 734 art. 5) |
| The employer's notices | The start of work within 7 days; a failure to start within 14 days of the start date; an early end, which cancels the entry by law | WA art. 70 ust. 1–2, 5 |
| No new declaration needed | A change of the employer's seat, name or form; a transfer of the workplace; more hours up to full time; a new job title with the same duties; since 8.07.2026, an employment contract in place of a civil contract, or the labour inspection's finding | WA art. 69 (pkt 5 from 2026/473 art. 8) |
| Continuity into a permit | After at least 3 months on an employment contract under the declaration, if the employer applies for a work permit for the same position on an employment contract before the declaration's end date, and the application has no formal gaps or they are filled in time, the work is legal, on terms not worse, until the permit is issued or the refusal is delivered; not while the proceedings are suspended at a party's request (ust. 2). The same applies to the person's own application for a temporary residence and work permit to continue with that employer | WA art. 71 ust. 1, 2, 4 |

### Illegal work and penalties

| Topic | Content | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| What illegal work is, for the person | Working while staying illegally, on a stay basis that does not allow work, or without the required permit or declaration | WA art. 2 pkt 3 |
| What illegal entrusting is, for the employer | Also: on other terms or in another position than the permit or declaration says (unless the law allows the change), or without a written contract | WA art. 2 pkt 2 |

## Inferences (to be confirmed)

Changed after the check: the tourist-visa point and "only the employer applies" are now FACT rows above (WA art. 3 ust. 3 pkt 1 with biznes.gov.pl 005940; art. 8 ust. 1, 12 pkt 1, 61 ust. 1, 67 ust. 2 pkt 1). Two inferences went too far and are corrected below.

- **A change of employer needs a new permit or declaration,** because both are issued for a named employer and position (art. 32, 62) and art. 18, 33 and 69 list the changes that do not need one. Two exceptions are in the act: the workplace passing to another employer (art. 18 ust. 1 pkt 2, 69 pkt 2), and a seasonal permit extended for work for another Polish employer (art. 58 ust. 1).
- **For work on a permit or a declaration, legality turns on two things at once:** a stay basis that allows work, and the permit or declaration (art. 2 pkt 3, art. 3 ust. 3). It does not hold for the regulation's cases, which need only a legal stay (art. 3 ust. 6, REG 2025/1620 § 1), nor for Ukrainian citizens under 2026/203 art. 41.
