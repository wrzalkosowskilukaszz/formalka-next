# Sources: recognition of a foreign diploma or school certificate, doc 19 matter 22

Collected on 17.10.2026 by Claude, for the map `docs/formalka-next/maps/recognition.md`. Level R1 under doc 19: sources only, with provenance. Production's registry has no recognition procedure, so nothing here reaches it.

**Scope:**
- a foreign university diploma: when it counts in Poland by law, and nostrification at a university otherwise; NAWA's written information;
- a foreign school-leaving certificate: for university admission (PSWN art. 326a) and as proof of education (the school superintendent, USO art. 93–93h);
- confirming education without documents, for refugees, temporary protection and some others.

This closes the school map's OPEN-S3 (USO art. 93, 93a) for older pupils and adults.

Not covered:
- regulated professions (doctor, nurse, lawyer and others): each has its own act and body; NAWA's list is quoted as a pointer only;
- professional qualifications acquired in the EU (Dz.U. 2026 poz. 166);
- scientific degrees beyond PSWN art. 328;
- international agreements on recognition (the act points to them; none was read).

**Not yet checked by the separate checking agent** (`.claude/agents/official-fact-checker.md`): that check comes before anything here is relied on.

## What they say (FACT)

### A university diploma

| Topic | Content | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| By law | A diploma from an authorised university in the higher education system of an EU, OECD or EFTA-EEA state confirms in Poland: first-cycle level (3-year or first-cycle studies of at least 3 years); second-cycle level (second-cycle studies); second-cycle level for long-cycle studies of at least 4 years, if they count as second-cycle in the issuing state | PSWN art. 326 ust. 1 |
| Further study | If the diploma gives the right, in its state, to second-cycle studies or a doctorate, it gives the same in Poland | PSWN art. 326 ust. 2 |
| Not | A diploma from an institution outside any state's system, not accredited on the day of issue, or with an unaccredited programme, or a programme run against the state's rules, confirms nothing | PSWN art. 326 ust. 3 |
| NAWA's information | The NAWA director gives, on request, written information about a foreign diploma, the level of studies and the university's status | PSWN art. 326 ust. 4 |
| Other diplomas | Recognised as equivalent to a Polish diploma and professional title under an international agreement, else by nostrification; then the Polish title may be used | PSWN art. 327 ust. 1–2 |
| Who runs it | On application, a university with research category A+, A or B+ in the discipline; a certificate at the end | PSWN art. 327 ust. 4–5 |
| Fee | At most 50 % of a professor's salary; the university sets the exemptions. A university that lacks competence refuses by an order open to complaint, and charges no fee | PSWN art. 327 ust. 6–7 |
| Without a diploma | For a person with temporary protection, refugee status or subsidiary protection, a temporary permit under UoC art. 159 ust. 1 pkt 1 lit. c or d, or a Polish citizen harmed by armed conflict, disaster or another humanitarian crisis, the completion of studies at a level may be confirmed in a separate procedure (from 5.03.2026) | PSWN art. 327 ust. 3 (Dz.U. 2026 poz. 203 art. 15) |
| The application | To a chosen university: the diploma, documents on the course of study, outcomes and length, the document of admission to the studies, and a statement of the place and date of birth; copies may be certified by the university | Regulation 2018 § 2 |
| Formal gaps | At least 14 days to fill them, else the application is left unexamined | Regulation 2018 § 3 |
| Translations | On request, an ordinary translation; in justified cases a sworn one (or a foreign translator's, certified by the Polish consul) | Regulation 2018 § 4 |
| Differences | The university compares the programme and may require exams or practice | Regulation 2018 § 5 |
| Time | A decision within 90 days of a formally complete application, not counting the time for translations, exams or practice | Regulation 2018 § 6 |
| Paying | Into the university's account or at its cash desk, within a time it sets, at least 14 days | Regulation 2018 § 7 |
| Degrees | A scientific degree from the EU, OECD or EFTA-EEA is equivalent; others by agreement or nostrification at a unit with category A+ or A | PSWN art. 328 |

### A school-leaving certificate, for university admission

| Topic | Content | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| By law | Certificates from a school in the EU, OECD or EFTA-EEA education system that give the right to apply for first-cycle or long-cycle studies there; IB and EB diplomas; certificates covered by international agreements | PSWN art. 326a ust. 1 (from 1.07.2025) |
| A field | A certificate that opens a given field in its state opens the same or a similar field in Poland | PSWN art. 326a ust. 2 |
| Others | Another certificate gives the right to apply in Poland if it gives that right in its own state | PSWN art. 326a ust. 3 |
| NAWA | On request, with an identity document and the certificate, the NAWA director gives written information confirming the right to apply; on a consul's request in a student visa case, NAWA confirms it | PSWN art. 326a ust. 4–5 |
| Denounced agreements | For a certificate from a state that denounced a recognition agreement with Poland, NAWA applies the agreement as it stood before | PSWN art. 326a ust. 6 |

### A school certificate as proof of education (the school superintendent)

| Topic | Content | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| By law | Secondary education is confirmed by certificates from an EU, OECD or EFTA-EEA school system, and by IB and EB diplomas | USO art. 93 ust. 1 (wording from 1.07.2025) |
| Agreements | Certificates from schools recognised by their state are recognised on the terms of international agreements | USO art. 93 ust. 2 |
| A decision | Unless agreements say otherwise, such certificates may be recognised by an administrative decision as primary, lower secondary, vocational or secondary education, or the right to continue education, except the right to apply for first-cycle or long-cycle studies (that is PSWN art. 326a) | USO art. 93 ust. 3 (wording from 1.07.2025) |
| Without documents | When producing the original or a duplicate, or authenticating it, meets obstacles hard to remove, for a refugee or a person with subsidiary protection or a temporary permit under UoC art. 159 ust. 1 pkt 1 lit. c or d, a certificate from a state that denounced a recognition agreement, or a person harmed by armed conflict, disaster or another crisis: the education may be confirmed by decision | USO art. 93a |
| Which superintendent | The one of the applicant's place of residence; without residence in Poland, the one of the seat of the institution where the certificate is to be filed | USO art. 93b ust. 1 |
| How | The superintendent compares the foreign schooling with Polish public schools; may ask others, in particular consulates | USO art. 93c |
| A check interview | When the certificate is not enough, the superintendent may hold an interview checking the level; for art. 93a, the interview is held unless the documents show the level | USO art. 93d–93f |
| Its fee | The commission members' and the translator's pay; 200 zł per member, and a commission of 3 to 5; exempt: art. 93a applicants and recipients of certain social assistance allowances in the last 2 years; instalments possible; a refund (less up to 10 %) after a missed interview for chance or health reasons, none after a no-show without 3 days' notice | USO art. 93g; regulation 2025 § 6, 9, 10 |
| The documents | The original or a duplicate, legalised by the Polish consul, the issuing state's education authority, or that state's accredited mission; from a Hague Convention state, the original or a notarised copy with an apostille; a course record if the certificate has none; all with a Polish translation | Regulation 2025 § 2 |
| Without documents (art. 93a) | Any document showing the level, or the applicant's statement; for a non-Polish citizen, also a document confirming the right to stay | Regulation 2025 § 3 |
| Translations | By a sworn translator (Polish, or one registered in an EU, EFTA-EEA or OECD state), the Polish consul, or the issuing state's accredited mission; if that is very hard, the superintendent may accept another reliable translation | Regulation 2025 § 4 |

## What the pages add, and where they differ from the act

| Point | Page | Act |
|---|---|---|
| NAWA's information | Applications only online through the SYRENA system since 3.10.2023; issued electronically, in Polish, free of charge; sworn translations of all documents except those in English, Russian, Ukrainian or Belarusian; "najszybciej jak to możliwe" | PSWN art. 326 ust. 4 sets no channel, fee or time (PAGES) |
| Long-cycle studies | NAWA (EU, OECD, EFTA page): at least 4-year long-cycle studies confirm second-cycle level | Art. 326 ust. 1 pkt 3 adds the condition "jeżeli jest uważany za równorzędny dyplomowi ukończenia studiów drugiego stopnia w państwie wydania" |
| A title | NAWA: confirming a level of education "nie jest jednoznaczne z posiadaniem polskiego tytułu zawodowego"; that needs an agreement or nostrification | Matches art. 326 ust. 1 and 327 ust. 1–2 (INFERENCE that art. 326 confirms the level, not the title) |
| School certificates | NAWA refers questions on school certificates to the "Ministerstwo Edukacji Narodowej" | The ministry is now named the Ministry of Education (the regulation of 2025, its footnote 1). The act gives the decision to the school superintendent (USO art. 93b) |
| Regulated professions | NAWA: outside the EU and EFTA, first recognise the diploma, then seek the professional right under the profession's own act; a list of professions and their bodies | Not read (OPEN-Q1) |
| An employer | NAWA: in an unregulated profession the employer decides and may ask for proof of equivalence or for information on the level | Not read; the employer's choice (PAGES) |

## Inferences (to be confirmed)

- **For a job in an unregulated profession, NAWA's free written information may be enough;** nostrification (with its fee and up to 90 days) matters when a Polish title or a regulated profession needs it.
- **A diploma from an OECD state (for example the USA, Canada, Japan, Korea, Mexico, Turkey) confirms a level of education by law,** like an EU one; whether a given state is an OECD member was not checked here (OPEN-Q2).
- **A Ukrainian, Belarusian or Georgian diploma needs an agreement or nostrification;** whether an agreement covers it was not read (OPEN-Q3).
- **Since 1.07.2025 the school superintendent no longer decides the right to apply for university studies;** that is PSWN art. 326a, with NAWA's information.
- **The check interview costs at least 600 zł** (3 members at 200 zł) plus any translator, for those not exempt (INFERENCE from § 6 and § 9).
