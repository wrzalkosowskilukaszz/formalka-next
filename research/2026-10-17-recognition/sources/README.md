# Sources: recognition of a foreign diploma or school certificate, doc 19 matter 22

Collected on 17.10.2026 by Claude, for the map `docs/formalka-next/maps/recognition.md`. Level R1 under doc 19: sources only, with provenance. Production's registry has no recognition procedure, so nothing here reaches it.

**Scope:**
- a foreign university diploma: when it counts in Poland by law, and nostrification at a university otherwise; NAWA's written information;
- a foreign school-leaving certificate: for university admission (PSWN art. 69, 70, 323, 326a) and as proof of education (the school superintendent, USO art. 93–93h);
- confirming education without documents, for refugees, temporary protection and some others.

This closes the school map's OPEN-S3 (USO art. 93, 93a) for older pupils and adults.

Not covered:
- regulated professions (doctor, nurse, lawyer and others): each has its own act and body; NAWA's list is a pointer, and the nursing act's own route (art. 35a) is quoted only to show that one exists;
- professional qualifications acquired in the EU (Dz.U. 2026 poz. 166);
- scientific degrees beyond PSWN art. 328;
- the texts of international agreements on recognition: the ministry's page that lists them is kept, the agreements were not read.

**Checked by the separate checking agent** (`.claude/agents/official-fact-checker.md`) on 17.10.2026: its findings and what was done are in `CHECK.md`. The corrections were made in the lab after re-reading each provision; they are not independently verified.

## What they say (FACT)

### A university diploma

| Topic | Content | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| By law | A diploma from an authorised university in the higher education system of an EU, OECD or EFTA-EEA state confirms in Poland: first-cycle level (3-year studies, or first-cycle studies of at least 3 years); second-cycle level (second-cycle studies); second-cycle level for long-cycle studies of at least 4 years, if they count as equal to a second-cycle diploma in the issuing state | PSWN art. 326 ust. 1 |
| Further study | Any foreign diploma that gives, in the issuing university's state, the right to second-cycle studies or to apply for a doctorate gives in Poland the right to second-cycle or postgraduate studies, or to apply for a doctorate | PSWN art. 326 ust. 2 |
| Not | A diploma from an institution outside any state's system, not accredited on the day of issue, or with an unaccredited programme, or a programme run against the state's rules, confirms nothing | PSWN art. 326 ust. 3 |
| NAWA's information | The NAWA director gives, on request, written information about a foreign diploma, the level of studies and the university's status | PSWN art. 326 ust. 4 |
| Other diplomas | Any diploma of studies abroad may be recognised as equivalent to a Polish diploma and professional title under an international agreement on equivalence, else by nostrification; then the Polish title may be used | PSWN art. 327 ust. 1–2 |
| Who runs it | On application, a university with research category A+, A or B+ in the discipline; a certificate at the end | PSWN art. 327 ust. 4–5 |
| Fee | At most 50 % of a professor's salary; the university sets the exemptions. The act defines the professor's salary as the minimum monthly base salary set by the minister's regulation, which is 9650 zł for pay due from 1.01.2026; so at most 4825 zł today (computed). A university that lacks competence refuses by an order open to complaint, and charges no fee | PSWN art. 327 ust. 6–7, art. 22 ust. 4; Dz.U. 2026 poz. 525 § 1–3 |
| Without a diploma | For a person with temporary protection, refugee status or subsidiary protection, a temporary permit under UoC art. 159 ust. 1 pkt 1 lit. c or d, or a Polish citizen harmed by armed conflict, disaster or another humanitarian crisis, the completion of studies at a level may be confirmed in a separate procedure (this wording from 5.03.2026; the earlier one covered refugees and some others too) | PSWN art. 327 ust. 3 (Dz.U. 2026 poz. 203 art. 15) |
| The application | To a chosen university: the diploma, documents on the course of study, outcomes and length, the document of admission to the studies, and a statement of the place and date of birth; copies may be certified by the university | Regulation 2018 § 2 |
| Formal gaps | At least 14 days to fill them, else the application is left unexamined | Regulation 2018 § 3 |
| Translations | On request, an ordinary translation of the documents of § 2 ust. 2 pkt 1–3; in particularly justified cases, for the documents of pkt 2, a sworn one (or a foreign translator's, certified by the Polish consul) | Regulation 2018 § 4 |
| Differences | The university compares the programme and may require exams or practice | Regulation 2018 § 5 |
| Time | A decision within 90 days of a formally complete application, not counting the time for translations, exams or practice | Regulation 2018 § 6 |
| Paying | Into the university's account or at its cash desk, within a time it sets, at least 14 days | Regulation 2018 § 7 |
| Without a diploma: the procedure | To a chosen university with the category: documents on the studies, grades, exams, qualifications, practice or employment, and on the status (the regulation still names only refugee status, subsidiary protection and the art. 159 permit), with a statement of completion and of the place and date of birth; at least 14 days for gaps; translations on request; the university may require exams or practice; 90 days, not counting translations, exams or practice; a fee paid within at least 14 days | Regulation 2018 § 9–14 |
| Degrees | A scientific degree from the EU, OECD or EFTA-EEA is equivalent, and so is a doctorate of the European University Institute; others by agreement or nostrification, which is excluded for a degree from an institution outside any state's system or without the right to award it | PSWN art. 328 ust. 1–3a |

### A school-leaving certificate, for university admission

| Topic | Content | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| By law | Certificates from a school in the EU, OECD or EFTA-EEA education system that give the right to apply for first-cycle or long-cycle studies there; IB and EB diplomas; certificates covered by international agreements | PSWN art. 326a ust. 1 (from 1.07.2025) |
| A field | A certificate of ust. 1 pkt 1 or 4 that opens a given field in its state opens the same or a similar field in Poland | PSWN art. 326a ust. 2 |
| Others | Another certificate gives the right to apply in Poland if it gives that right in its own state | PSWN art. 326a ust. 3 |
| NAWA | On request, with an identity document and the certificate, the NAWA director gives written information confirming the right to apply; on a consul's request in a student visa case, NAWA confirms it | PSWN art. 326a ust. 4–5 |
| Denounced agreements | For a certificate from a state that denounced a recognition agreement with Poland, in the case of USO art. 93a pkt 1a (producing or authenticating the document meets obstacles hard to remove), NAWA applies the agreement as it stood before | PSWN art. 326a ust. 6 |
| Admission | A first-cycle or long-cycle candidate needs a certificate of art. 326a ust. 1, or another certificate (ust. 3) confirmed under art. 326a ust. 4, that is, with NAWA's written information | PSWN art. 69 ust. 2 pkt 4 (Dz.U. 2025 poz. 622 art. 10 pkt 2, from 1.07.2025) |
| Foreigners: a certificate of art. 326a ust. 1 | For a foreigner who is not an EU citizen, the university checks the language of the studies at a level it sets, not below B2, on a document listed by regulation | PSWN art. 70 ust. 5a, 5e–5f |
| Foreigners: any other certificate | The university requires the certificate with NAWA's written information (or, in the cases of USO art. 93a, NAWA's information alone), checks the language at B2 at least, and holds an entrance exam on knowledge or aptitude, which may count for up to 100 % | PSWN art. 70 ust. 5b, 5e |
| Foreigners: second-cycle studies | A non-EU foreigner with a diploma of art. 326 ust. 1, or one recognised under an agreement or by nostrification: the B2 check. Any foreigner with another foreign diploma: the diploma with NAWA's written information, and the B2 check | PSWN art. 70 ust. 5c–5d |
| The admission decision | A foreigner's admission decision (art. 323 ust. 1 pkt 6) is issued only when these documents were shown and, for a certificate outside art. 326a ust. 1, the entrance exam was passed | PSWN art. 323 ust. 1, 1a–1d |
| Public notice | The university publishes the B2 rule, the documents it accepts, the NAWA requirement and the entrance exams in its admission rules | PSWN art. 70 ust. 5g |

### A school certificate as proof of education (the school superintendent)

| Topic | Content | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| By law | Secondary education is confirmed by certificates from an EU, OECD or EFTA-EEA school system, and by IB and EB diplomas | USO art. 93 ust. 1 (wording from 1.07.2025) |
| Agreements | Certificates from schools recognised by their state are recognised on the terms of international agreements | USO art. 93 ust. 2 |
| A decision | Unless agreements say otherwise, such certificates may be recognised by an administrative decision as primary, lower secondary, vocational or secondary education, or the right to continue education, except the right to apply for first-cycle or long-cycle studies (that is PSWN art. 326a) | USO art. 93 ust. 3 (wording from 1.07.2025) |
| Without documents | When producing the original or a duplicate, or authenticating it, meets obstacles hard to remove, for a refugee or a person with subsidiary protection or a temporary permit under UoC art. 159 ust. 1 pkt 1 lit. c or d, a certificate from a state that denounced a recognition agreement, or a person harmed by armed conflict, disaster or another crisis: the education may be confirmed by decision, except the right to apply for first-cycle or long-cycle studies (wording from 1.07.2025) | USO art. 93a |
| Cases and decisions from before 1.07.2025 | A case on the recognition of secondary education and the right to apply for studies, begun and not ended by a final decision by 30.06.2025, keeps the old art. 93–93c and 93f. A final decision on that right issued by 30.06.2025, or later in such a case, stays valid and may be shown in admission to first-cycle or long-cycle studies | Dz.U. 2025 poz. 622 art. 12 |
| Which superintendent | The one of the applicant's place of residence; without residence in Poland, the one of the seat of the institution where the certificate is to be filed | USO art. 93b ust. 1 |
| How | The superintendent compares the foreign schooling with Polish public schools; may ask others, in particular consulates | USO art. 93c |
| A check interview | When the certificate is not enough, the superintendent may hold an interview checking the level; for art. 93a, the interview is held unless the documents show the level | USO art. 93d–93f |
| Its fee | The sum of the commission members' and the translator's pay; 200 zł per member; the commission has 3 to 5 persons including its chair, and the act lists the chair apart from the members (whether the chair is paid: OPEN-Q6). Exempt: art. 93a applicants and recipients of certain social assistance allowances in the last 2 years. Instalments if the applicant's important interest favours it. After a missed interview for chance or health reasons the fee or a part "może podlegać zwrotowi" (less up to 10 %); none after a no-show without 3 days' notice | USO art. 93f ust. 3, 5; 93g; regulation 2025 § 6, 9, 10 |
| The documents | The original or a duplicate, legalised by the Polish consul, the issuing state's education authority, or that state's accredited mission; from a Hague Convention state, the original or a notarised copy with an apostille; a course record if the certificate has none; all with a Polish translation | Regulation 2025 § 2 |
| Without documents (art. 93a) | Any document showing the level, or the applicant's statement; for a non-Polish citizen, also a document confirming the right to stay | Regulation 2025 § 3 |
| Translations | By a sworn translator (Polish, or one registered in an EU, EFTA-EEA or OECD state), the Polish consul, or the issuing state's accredited mission; if that is very hard, the superintendent may accept another reliable translation | Regulation 2025 § 4 |
| A post-secondary school | A non-Polish citizen may attend a public or non-public post-secondary school only with a Polish language certificate of at least B1 (from 1.09.2025) | Prawo oświatowe art. 165 ust. 5a (Dz.U. 2025 poz. 622 art. 7) |

### A regulated profession (a pointer only)

| Topic | Content | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| A nurse or midwife from outside the EU | Besides recognition, the minister of health may, by decision, consent to practise for a set scope of duties, period and place of employment with a healthcare provider, on conditions (among them a statement of sufficient Polish, the employer's certificate, at least 3 years' practice in the last 5, and a legalised or apostilled diploma); the regional council then grants the right to practise after a Polish B1 document | Nursing act art. 35a ust. 1, 3, 28 |

## Inferences (to be confirmed)

- **For a job in an unregulated profession, NAWA's free written information may be enough;** nostrification (with its fee and up to 90 days) matters when a Polish title or a regulated profession needs it.
- **A diploma from an OECD state (for example the USA, Canada, Japan, Korea, Mexico, Turkey) confirms a level of education by law,** like an EU one; whether a given state is an OECD member was not checked here (OPEN-Q2).
- **A Ukrainian diploma issued after 20.06.2006, a Belarusian one issued after 29.09.2005, or a Georgian one needs nostrification for a Polish title,** on the ministry's page (the Ukrainian 2005 agreement gives academic recognition only; Georgia is not listed); the agreements' own texts were not read (OPEN-Q3).
- **The university procedure without a diploma is paid:** art. 327 ust. 6 sets a fee for "postępowanie" after ust. 4–5 name both procedures, and § 14 says how it is paid; only the superintendent's route has the art. 93a exemption.
- **The regulation lags the act on who may use the procedure without a diploma:** § 9 ust. 2 pkt 1 lit. f names only refugee status, subsidiary protection and the art. 159 permit, while art. 327 ust. 3 now also names temporary protection and Polish citizens.
