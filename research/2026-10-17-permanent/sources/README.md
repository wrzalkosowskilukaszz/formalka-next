## What they say (FACT)

### The permanent residence permit

| Topic | Content | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| What it is | Granted "na czas nieoznaczony", on application, on one of the grounds of art. 195 ust. 1 | UoC art. 195 ust. 1 |
| The grounds | Among others:<br>- a child of a foreigner with a permanent or EU long-term resident permit, in their parental care, born after that permit, or born during the parent's temporary permit, humanitarian or tolerated stay or protection (pkt 1);<br>- a child of a Polish citizen in their parental care (pkt 2);<br>- a person of Polish origin who intends to settle (pkt 3; the repatriation act art. 5);<br>- a spouse of a Polish citizen: married at least 3 years before applying, and just before applying 2 years of unbroken stay on a temporary permit for that marriage, or as a refugee, a subsidiary protection or humanitarian holder (pkt 4);<br>- a victim of human trafficking, with three conditions (pkt 5);<br>- unbroken stay just before applying: 5 years as a refugee, subsidiary or humanitarian holder; 10 years on a tolerated stay under art. 351 pkt 1 or 3; 4 years on a temporary residence and work permit for a profession needed by the economy (art. 114 ust. 1a), with a stable and regular income (pkt 6);<br>- asylum (pkt 8);<br>- a valid Karta Polaka and the intention to settle (pkt 9);<br>- some UK citizens who were posted workers until 31.12.2020 (pkt 10) | UoC art. 195 ust. 1 |
| Unbroken stay | No break over 6 months, and all breaks together at most 10 months in the periods counted. Breaks for work abroad for an employer based in Poland, accompanying such a person, a special personal situation of up to 6 months, or a Polish university's practice or classes are not counted | UoC art. 195 ust. 4 |
| Refusal to start | Among others: staying illegally, on a humanitarian visa of art. 60 ust. 1 pkt 23, or on an EU long-term resident permit; detention or a ban on leaving; a prison sentence; a return decision's voluntary period not yet over; being outside Poland on the day of filing | UoC art. 196 ust. 1 (ust. 5 repealed from 27.04.2026) |
| Refusal | Among others: the grounds not met; an entry in the list of unwanted foreigners or in SIS; security; false data or documents; a marriage of convenience; tax arrears without a relief; unpaid costs of a return decision | UoC art. 197 ust. 1 |
| Withdrawal | Among others: security; false data; a final sentence in Poland for an intentional crime of at least 3 years; leaving Poland for over 6 years. A permit based on marriage can be withdrawn after a divorce within 2 years of the grant | UoC art. 199 ust. 1, 3 |
| Expiry | By law, on the grant of an EU long-term resident permit, or on Polish citizenship | UoC art. 200 |

### The EU long-term resident permit

| Topic | Content | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| What it is | Granted "na czas nieoznaczony", on application, after a legal and unbroken stay in Poland of at least 5 years just before applying | UoC art. 211 ust. 1 |
| The three conditions | A stable and regular income for the person and the family they keep; health insurance in the sense of the act on publicly financed health care, or an insurer's cover of treatment costs in Poland; a confirmed knowledge of Polish | UoC art. 211 ust. 1 pkt 1–3 |
| Income | Higher than the income that entitles to social assistance benefits, for the person and each family member they keep (art. 140 ust. 2), and held for the 3 years just before applying (2 years on the Blue Card route of art. 212 ust. 1 pkt 1). The social assistance criteria from 1.01.2025: 1010 zł for a person living alone, 823 zł per person in a family | UoC art. 211 ust. 2, 140 ust. 2; regulation Dz.U. 2024 poz. 1044 § 1 pkt 1 |
| Polish | One of: a document of at least B1 from the minister's list (the state certificate, ECL, TELC, a university certificate after a Polish course, a sworn translator's certificate); a certificate from a school in Poland taught in Polish (not a post-secondary school) or a Polish university diploma taught in Polish; the same from abroad. Not required under 16 | UoC art. 211 ust. 3–5; regulation Dz.U. 2023 poz. 1076 and its annex |
| The language documents, transitional | A post-secondary school certificate obtained by 30.06.2025 counted only in cases started from 1.07.2025 to 30.06.2026 | Dz.U. 2025 poz. 622 art. 30 ust. 2–3 |
| What counts toward the 5 years | Among others: periods in other EU states on a Blue Card, a researcher's document or international protection, with at least 2 years in Poland on a highly qualified permit (pkt 1–1c); a protection procedure over 18 months (pkt 2); **half** of a stay on a study visa or permit (art. 60 ust. 1 pkt 9–10, art. 144, 187 pkt 1 lit. b) or during a protection procedure (pkt 3) | UoC art. 212 ust. 1 |
| What does not count | Among others: a posted worker or cross-border provider; a humanitarian visa of art. 60 ust. 1 pkt 23; "w okresie jego nauki na terytorium Rzeczypospolitej Polskiej"; diplomats; intra-corporate transfer permits; a refused protection procedure; small border traffic | UoC art. 212 ust. 2 |
| Unbroken stay | In Poland: no break over 6 months, all breaks at most 10 months in the 5 years, with the same exceptions as for the permanent permit | UoC art. 212 ust. 3–4 |
| Refusal to start | Among others: an illegal stay; a stay for studies or vocational training; to start or continue education; on humanitarian or tolerated stay, asylum or **temporary protection**; while applying for protection or asylum; a posted worker; detention; a prison sentence; a return decision's voluntary period; being outside Poland | UoC art. 213 ust. 1 (ust. 2 repealed from 27.04.2026) |
| Refusal | The conditions of art. 211 ust. 1 not met; security | UoC art. 214 |
| Withdrawal | Among others: obtained unlawfully; a real and serious threat; leaving Poland for over 6 years; leaving the EU for 12 months in a row (24 for a former highly qualified permit holder and their family); an EU long-term resident permit in another member state | UoC art. 215 ust. 1 |
| Expiry | By law, on Polish citizenship | UoC art. 217 |

### Filing and the case, both permits (from 27.04.2026)

| Topic | Content | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Who decides | The voivode of the place of stay, by a decision | UoC art. 201 ust. 1, 218 ust. 1 |
| When to file | No later than the last day of the legal stay. Not for a child born in Poland to a parent with a permanent or EU long-term resident permit, or a child of a Polish citizen in their care (permanent permit) | UoC art. 202 ust. 1, 3; 218a ust. 1 |
| For a child or a person without capacity | A parent or guardian, the court-appointed guardian, or a curator files | UoC art. 202 ust. 2, 218a ust. 2 |
| How | Only online, through MOS, on its form. Filed any other way: left unexamined. The voivode helps with filing | UoC art. 203c, 203d ust. 6, 219c, 219d ust. 6 |
| What to attach | A current photo and a digital copy of every page of the travel document; for the EU long-term resident also a digital copy of a current legal title to the flat where the person will stay. Other documents may be attached as files | UoC art. 203d ust. 1–2, 219d ust. 1–2; regulations Dz.U. 2026 poz. 488 § 3–5, 487 § 4–5 (a JPG of at least 684 × 883 pixels, up to 2.5 MB; PDF files up to 10 MB each, 50 MB in all) |
| The flat (EU long-term resident) | A loan-for-use agreement ("umowa użyczenia") does not count, unless the lender is a descendant, ascendant, spouse, the spouse's parent or a sibling | UoC art. 219a ust. 1–2 |
| Signing | The applicant signs with a qualified signature or a trusted profile; a parent, guardian or curator filing for the person may also use a personal signature | UoC art. 203d ust. 3, 219d ust. 3 |
| When it counts as filed | When it is signed and the person has the official confirmation of receipt (UPO) that MOS generates | UoC art. 203d ust. 4–5, 219d ust. 4–5 |
| An outage on the last day | If MOS does not work, the application is filed by the 3rd working day after the fault is fixed and counts as filed on the outage's first day | UoC art. 225c ust. 1 |
| In person | The voivode calls the person, at least 7 days ahead, to show the travel document (for the EU resident also the flat's title), give fingerprints and a signature specimen. No fingerprints under 6, no signature under 13; no call for a child under 6 or for someone a doctor's certificate says cannot come | UoC art. 203b, 203e, 203h, 219b, 219e, 219h |
| Documents | The voivode calls for documents with at least 14 days to deliver, and for the originals of files attached | UoC art. 203f, 219f |
| Discontinuance | If the person does not come when called, or does not give fingerprints, the signature, the travel document (or for the EU resident the flat's title) while there | UoC art. 203i, 219i |
| The filing certificate | Filed during a legal stay and without formal defects (or fixed in time): the voivode issues a certificate through MOS, free of charge, and the stay is legal until the decision is final. Not while the case is suspended at the person's request | UoC art. 206 ust. 1–9, 222a ust. 1–8 |
| The decision's time limit | 6 months, from the last of: a complete application, or the documents delivered or their deadline passed; the appeal stage 90 days | UoC art. 210, 222d |
| The suspension | Until 4.03.2027 the voivode's time limits for these permits do not run, and the inactivity rules do not apply | Special act art. 100d ust. 1, 3 (other folder) |
| Fees | Stamp duty 640 zł for either permit. The permanent permit is exempt for a close family member of a repatriate, an asylum holder, a Karta Polaka holder who intends to settle, and the UK route of art. 195 ust. 1 pkt 10. Card fee 100 zł. The stamp duty is not refunded after a refusal or a discontinuance (from 5.03.2026) | Stamp duty act annex part III ust. 3–4; card fee regulation § 2; UoC art. 207b, 223a |
| Cases filed before 27.04.2026 | Continue under the old rules | Dz.U. 2025 poz. 1794 art. 12 |

### The card

| Topic | Content | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Validity | The permit is indefinite; the card is not. A card for a permanent permit is valid 10 years, for an EU long-term resident permit 5 years, and so is each next card | UoC art. 243 |
| The next card | Apply at least 30 days before the card expires | UoC art. 230 ust. 2 |
| Karta Polaka route | The card is issued after the person returns their Karta Polaka | UoC art. 229 ust. 7 |
| A lost card | Notify the issuing authority within 3 days | UoC art. 232 ust. 1 |

## Inferences (to be confirmed)

- **Someone under temporary protection (PESEL UKR) cannot apply for the EU long-term resident permit** while on that protection (art. 213 ust. 1 pkt 1 lit. e). Whether the time on protection counts later toward the 5 years is not stated in these articles: OPEN-P3.
- **Permanent does not mean the card never expires.** The person must apply for the next card (art. 230 ust. 2, 243); missing it leaves them without a valid card while the permit itself stays (INFERENCE from art. 243 and 195 ust. 1).
- **The Podlaski pages' document lists predate MOS in parts** (photos, copies), so the app quotes the act for what to attach and a voivode's page only for the typical documents.
