# Interview plan: Phase 1 problem interviews (registration and PESEL)

**Status:** a draft. The owner approves it, and decides recruitment channels, incentives (any cost needs approval) and data retention. No prototype is shown: these are problem interviews, which the audit allowed now (GO).

**Purpose:** confirm, refute or re-rank the loss points (L1–L14) and assumptions (A1–A18) in doc 18, from people's real past cases. Nothing here tests the app.

**Time:** 50 minutes with the "other matters" question (§3.4).

## 1. Who

| Segment | Number | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Registered an address in Poland in the last 12 months, EU/EFTA/Swiss citizen | 3 | The 30-day group; online route available |
| The same, non-EU citizen (visa, visa-free, or a residence card) | 5 | The 4-day group; in person only; the declared-period rule |
| Applied for PESEL **without** registering, in the last 12 months | 2 | The legal-basis and in-person problems (Q7, Q8) |
| Helped someone else register (a friend, family member, employer's HR, NGO) | 2 | Helpers fill in forms for others; the "dla kogoś" path |
| A landlord who rented to a foreigner | 2 | The owner's confirmation is a step outside the newcomer's control (Q3–Q5) |
| **Experts** (separate short interviews): a registration clerk (delegatura BAiSO or another municipality), and an adviser from an NGO or a helpline | 1 + 1 | What they see at the desk and on the phone. They are not users |

That is 14 people plus 2 experts. Include people with lower digital skills, people who do not read Polish, and at least two who use a screen reader or have low vision. Nationality and language are recorded only as the person describes them, and never used to infer anything else (product rule 4).

**Channels** (owner's choice): NGOs and Migrant Info Points (with their permission), universities' international offices, employers' HR, landlords via rental platforms' community pages. Never through our own app.

## 2. Consent and data

- Written or recorded verbal consent, in the person's language.
- **Never collected:** document numbers, copies or photos of documents or letters, addresses, PESEL.
- **Recording:** audio only, if the person agrees. It stays off this repository, which is public at the time of writing. Notes are anonymised the same day: a participant code, no names.
- **Deletion:** recordings are deleted after the notes are checked, by a date the owner sets (OPEN).
- **Interpreters:** sworn or NGO interpreters only, under the same consent. Any cost needs the owner's approval.

## 3. The guide (45 minutes)

1. **Context (5 min).** "Opowiedz o ostatnim razie, kiedy meldowałeś(-aś) się albo starałeś(-aś) się o PESEL." Do not mention Formalka or any app.
2. **The timeline (20 min).** Draw it together on paper, from "you knew you would move" to "you had the number or the certificate". For each step:
   - What happened? Where were you: at home, at the office, at the landlord's?
   - Who helped? What did you read: gov.pl, a group, a friend?
   - What did you get wrong, or nearly wrong? What cost a second visit?
   - How long did it take, and what did it cost? Count time and money only; no documents.
3. **Probes,** only if the stage comes up. Never lead with them.
   - **Deadline (L1, L2).** "Kiedy dowiedziałeś(-aś) się, ile masz czasu? Od kiedy liczyłeś(-aś)?"
   - **Owner (L5, L6).** "Jak wyglądała rozmowa z właścicielem o meldunku?"
   - **Form (L7, L8).** Show the blank official temporary-stay form. "Które pole było trudne? Co wpisałeś(-aś) w datę końcową?"
   - **Office (L9).** "Skąd wiedziałeś(-aś), do którego urzędu iść? Czy rezerwowałeś(-aś) wizytę?"
   - **PESEL (L11, L12).** "Kto poprosił Cię o PESEL? Czy ktoś podał podstawę prawną?"
   - **After (L13).** "Jak dowiedziałeś(-aś) się, że masz PESEL? Co zrobiłeś(-aś) z zaświadczeniem?"
4. **Other matters (5 min, for doc 19's order).**
   - "Jakie inne sprawy urzędowe załatwiałeś(-aś) w pierwszym roku w Polsce?"
   - "Która była najtrudniejsza, najdroższa albo najbardziej stresująca, i dlaczego?"

   Record each matter in the doc 19 §3 numbering, with the person's own words for why it was hard.
5. **Trust and help (10 min).**
   - "Komu ufałeś(-aś), a komu nie? Dlaczego?"
   - "Czego nigdy nie wpisał(a)byś do aplikacji w telefonie?"
   - "Gdyby ktoś miał Ci przypominać o terminach, kto i jak?"
6. **Close (5 min).** Ask what they would tell a friend arriving next week. Thank them; pay the incentive if the owner approved one.

**Landlords** get their own short guide on the same pattern: the last time a tenant asked for registration; their fears; what they signed; what they were told by whom.

**Experts** get a separate guide:
- the most common reasons people come back;
- the most common errors on the forms;
- what they wish people knew before coming;
- what they would never want an app to tell people.

## 4. Analysis

- **Coding.** Each note is coded by journey stage (S0–S9), loss point (L1–L14 or "new") and assumption (A1–A18). Two people code; they compare a quarter of the notes.
- **Evidence rule, fixed in advance:**
  - a loss point counts as **confirmed** when at least 3 of the 12 newcomer and helper interviews report it unprompted, or at least 1 expert reports it as frequent;
  - it counts as **not seen** after 12 interviews with no report;
  - anything in between stays **possible**.
- **Assumptions** are marked supported, contradicted or untested, with the interview codes as evidence.
- **Report.** Interviews are reported as interviews and never mixed with desk research (doc 03 §2). Quotes are paraphrased unless the person agreed to a verbatim quote.

## 5. What the interviews decide

They decide:
- which loss points the Phase 1 moderated sessions must cover;
- whether any restricted function (doc 18 §7) may be opened for the sessions;
- whether the landlord needs content of their own.

They do not decide new features: none before Phase 1.
