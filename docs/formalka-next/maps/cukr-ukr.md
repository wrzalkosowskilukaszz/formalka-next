# Map: the UKR status, PESEL UKR and the CUKR card

**Status:** a lab map by Claude, 17.10.2026. It is not independent, and nothing in it is built or shown to people. Wave 3 of doc 19. Checked by the separate checking agent on 17.10.2026, at the owner's request (`research/2026-10-17-ukr/sources/CHECK.md`); the corrections are changed in the lab, not independently verified.
- Doc 19 kept wave 3 at sources only, because the rules move fast. The owner asked for this map on 17.10. **It is valid as of 17.10.2026 and must be re-checked against the sources before any use.**
- **Temporary protection now runs to 4.03.2028** (Council Implementing Decision (EU) 2026/1912 art. 1, read on 17.10.2026: "do dnia 4 marca 2028 r."; `eu-2026-1912-pl-2026-10-17.txt`). The UdSC CUKR Q&A still says the status ends on 4.03.2027.
- **4.03.2027 for CUKR** is UdSC's date to file by ("będziesz mógł złożyć do 04.03.2027 r."). The act, art. 42x, does not close filing on that date: it makes the stay legal for those who file by then. Whether filing will be possible after 4.03.2027, now that the status runs longer, is OPEN-C8. The app treats 4.03.2027 as the safe date, attributed to UdSC.

## 1. Who it is for, by the status the person states

The CUKR card is for a Ukrainian citizen: art. 42c begins "Obywatelowi Ukrainy". The old definition that extended "obywatel Ukrainy" to a non-Ukrainian spouse and minor children (the special act's former art. 1 ust. 2) was repealed from 5.03.2026 (W203 art. 17 pkt 1), and no transitional rule keeping it was found. UdSC still names "obywatele Ukrainy i niektórzy członkowie ich rodzin" (QA): for a family member without Ukrainian citizenship, a lawyer (OPEN-C9). Since 5.03.2026 the UKR status also covers other foreigners under temporary protection (UEL art. 8 pkt 24a lit. d; QA). The app never infers the status from nationality or language (rule 4). It asks about the status, which is on the person's PESEL record.

| What the person says | Route |
|---|---|
| "Przyjechałem niedawno z Ukrainy, nie mam PESEL" | PESEL UKR at any municipal office within 30 days of crossing the border (PROT art. 110a; UEL art. 44a). Temporary protection is now granted only to those who have met their military obligations in Ukraine, with proof where relevant, except people already protected in Poland without a break. The dates differ: the decision's text gives art. 2 "od dnia 31 lipca 2026 r." and the exception for those protected "przed dniem 30 lipca 2026 r. lub w tym dniu"; UdSC's page gives 5.08.2026 and 4.08.2026; a corrigendum exists and could not be read (OPEN-C12). The app names the condition and hands off; it never judges it |
| "Mam PESEL UKR" | Living under the status, and the choice about the CUKR card (§2, U3) |
| "Miałem UKR, ale dostałem kartę pobytu (zwykłą)" | The status ended (PROT art. 109b ust. 1 pkt 4); no CUKR (QA). Points to the residence map |
| "Mój status zmienił się na NUE" | The status was changed on 1.09.2026 for people with a PESEL given on a statement who did not confirm their identity by 31.08.2026; their legal stay under art. 106 ended (W203 art. 25). What they can do now is OPEN-C1: a lawyer or NGO |
| "Dziecko urodziło się w Polsce" | The CUKR card only after the mother has one (SA art. 42d; QA) |

## 2. The journey (stages U0–U9)

| Stage | Goal | Steps (phone / **off the phone**) | Basis (FACT) | Where people get lost |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **U0** Crossing the border | Keep the protection | Phone: record the crossing date. **Off: apply for PESEL UKR at any municipal office** | Within 30 days of crossing, or the protection ends (PROT art. 110a ust. 2, 109b ust. 1 pkt 1). The military-obligations condition for new arrivals (EU 2026/1912 art. 2; its date is OPEN-C12) | **UL1:** the 30 days pass |
| **U1** PESEL UKR at the office | Complete register data | **Off: in person, on paper: passport, a photo, fingerprints (not under 6), signature specimen (from 12); contact data and consent give an automatic trusted profile** | PROT art. 110a, 110c; UEL art. 44a ust. 3, 6, 9, 11, 19 | **UL2:** data left incomplete: later, MOS blocks the CUKR application and does not say why (QA) |
| **U2** Living under the status | Stay legal | Phone: the rules that end the status. **Off: work (the employer notifies the labour office within 7 days)** | Legal stay (PROT art. 106); work without a permit (art. 116); the employer's notice (act on employing foreigners art. 5a, from W203); the status ends after more than 30 days abroad (art. 109b ust. 1 pkt 2) | **UL3:** a trip of more than 30 days ends the status. **UL4:** an identity confirmation was due (W203 art. 25–26; OPEN-C2 for new travel documents) |
| **U3** The choice | Decide between staying on the status, the CUKR card, or an ordinary permit | Phone: the facts side by side, never a recommendation | QA (the comparison); SA art. 42i (a pending temporary residence permit case started on the person's application ends by law when a CUKR application is filed; a permanent or long-term-resident case does not); W203 art. 45 (some ordinary permits are open to status holders) | **UL5:** filing CUKR while not eligible, after a temporary residence case was pending: both are lost (QA warns) |
| **U4** Eligibility | Know whether CUKR is possible | Phone: four questions | Ukrainian citizenship; UKR on 4.06.2025, on the application day, and unbroken for 365 days (SA art. 42c pkt 1–3); also on the day the card is issued and periods do not add up (QA) | **UL6:** a past trip broke the status; the 4.06.2025 date was missed (QA examples) |
| **U5** Prerequisites | Be able to file | **Off: a trusted profile or a qualified signature (a personal signature only for the representative of a person without full legal capacity); register data complete (any municipal office checks it)** | SA art. 42f ust. 5, 42g ust. 1–2; UEL art. 44c ust. 6; PROT art. 110c; W203 art. 23 (a trusted profile held on 4.03.2026 counts). The QA and procedure pages offer the personal signature to everyone: a page finding | **UL7:** MOS says only "go to the municipal office" (QA) |
| **U6** Fees | Two payments, two payees | **Off: 340 zł stamp duty to the city of the voivode's seat; 100 zł card fee to the voivode** | SA art. 42s, 42n; stamp duty act art. 12 and annex part III ust. 2; card fee regulation Dz.U. 2022 poz. 1583 § 2, 3; QA. The card-fee relief is excluded (SA art. 42k). Whether the stamp duty act's exemptions reach art. 42s: OPEN-C10 (QA says "obowiązkowe dla każdego") | **UL8:** one transfer, or the wrong payee |
| **U7** Filing | Filed by 4.03.2027 | Phone: the date. **Off: MOS: the form, a photo, both payment proofs; sign; UPO** | SA art. 42f ust. 1, 3; QA; filed by 4.03.2027, the stay is legal until pickup or the card's cancellation, or until a refusal or a discontinuance is final (SA art. 42x) | **UL9:** paper, e-mail or e-Doręczenia: left unexamined (SA art. 42f ust. 7; QA) |
| **U8** Waiting | Stay safe meanwhile | Phone: what changes and what does not | The status stays until pickup; travel up to 30 days (QA); a move is notified to the same voivode (QA; SA art. 42o ust. 2); the card is issued or refused within 180 days of a complete application (SA art. 42l, from 4.05.2026; before, 90 days); UdSC says it cannot estimate it now (QA); whether art. 100d suspends it is OPEN-C6 | **UL10:** a trip of more than 30 days while waiting (INFERENCE from art. 109b) |
| **U9** Pickup and after | Collect within 60 days, then keep the permit | **Off: collect in person.** Phone: the duties | Within 60 days of the office's notice, or the card is cancelled and the fees are lost (SA art. 42r ust. 2). Valid 3 years from issue; a child's card as long as the mother's (art. 42p). Status UKR becomes CUKR, and UKR benefits end (QA). Notify each change of address within 15 working days (art. 42u). The permit can be withdrawn by the voivode's decision after at least 6 months abroad (art. 42t ust. 1 pkt 3, ust. 2). Work and business are open (QA). A refusal: appeal within 14 days to the Szef UdSC through the voivode (QA; SA art. 42o ust. 4) | **UL11:** the 60 days pass. **UL12:** loss of collective accommodation is a surprise (QA) |

**The critical moments:**
1. **U3–U4, the choice and eligibility.** An ineligible application can end an ordinary case for nothing.
2. **4.03.2027.** UdSC's date to file CUKR by; the status itself now runs to 4.03.2028 (EXT). The QA's "status ends 4.03.2027" is outdated.
3. **U9, the 60 days.** Missing them loses the card and the fees.

## 5. What a blueprint would need (PROPOSED; needs a production guide first)

- **A procedure in production's registry for the CUKR card.** Today the registry has the UKR notes only inside the PESEL and residence guides.
- **Facts:** `ukr.status` (UKR, none, CUKR, NUE, permit, unknown), `ukr.on_2025_06_04`, `ukr.unbroken`, `ukr.pending_application`, `ukr.for` (self, child born in Poland, child arrived), `ukr.border_crossed` (date).
- **Deadlines:**
  - `pesel-ukr` (30 days from the crossing);
  - `cukr-window` (a fixed date, 4.03.2027: UdSC's filing date; SA art. 42x protects the stay of those who file by then);
  - `pickup` (60 days from an event `pickup_notice`).
- **The compare level as a reading level** (a new kind: it is read, not done).

## 6. Upkeep and validation

- **Volatility.** In 2026 the rules moved across three acts and an EU decision. W203 art. 54 has three later dates left (1.01.2027, 5.03.2027, 1.09.2027), none touching this map; the date set by announcement passed on 4.05.2026 (M.P. 2026 poz. 371). The EU decision of 30.07.2026 changed the end date with no Polish amendment, so a weekly check of the acts alone would have missed it: the check needs UdSC's news page too. A weekly check is decision D3 in the overnight report. Re-check this map before any use after 17.10.2026.
- **Confirmers:**
  - a migration lawyer (every rule);
  - UdSC or a voivode (the fee payees, pickup, timelines);
  - an NGO serving Ukrainian newcomers (UL1–UL12 and the language).
- **Moderated test:** in Ukrainian, on CU2, CU3 and CU4, with the doc 18 §6.1 thresholds. A wrong eligibility verdict, or a missing art. 42i warning, is a critical error.
