# Map: buying real estate in Poland as a foreigner

**Status:** a lab map by Claude, 17.10.2026. It is not independent, and nothing in it is built or shown to people. Doc 19 matter 21 (wave 6). Production's registry has no procedure for this. **Checked by the separate checking agent on 17.10.2026** (`research/2026-10-17-real-estate/sources/CHECK.md`); the corrections were made in the lab after re-reading each provision, and are not independently verified.

**Why it matters:**
- A purchase that needed the interior minister's permit and did not have it is void (FACT, REA art. 6 ust. 1). Without the permit no legal act and no land register entry can be made (FACT, art. 5), so a notary will not draw up the deed; the person may learn this late, after a preliminary contract and a deposit (INFERENCE).
- **A flat needs no permit, except in the border zone** (FACT, REA art. 8 ust. 1 pkt 1 and ust. 3). REA does not define the zone; that it is the border act's zone, listed in 2005, is a reading (INFERENCE). That list includes Gdańsk, Gdynia, Sopot, Szczecin and Świnoujście (FACT from the regulation, read by a character table and compared with the rendered pages; OPEN-R2). A non-EEA buyer of a flat there needs a permit (INFERENCE).
- Citizens and entrepreneurs of the EEA and Switzerland need no permit (FACT, art. 8 ust. 2); the act's temporary exceptions for them ended in 2009 and 2016 (INFERENCE from the accession date, 1.05.2004, Dz.U. 2004 nr 90 poz. 864).
- The permit takes months: the act gives the defence minister 14 days from receiving the request to object, which the interior minister extends to 2 months in particularly justified cases, at the objecting body's request (FACT, art. 1 ust. 1a); the ministry's FAQ (27.06.2022) speaks of 2–4 months or more (PAGES). It costs 1570 zł, and the promesa 98 zł (FACT, the stamp duty act).

**Sources:** `research/2026-10-17-real-estate/sources/` (README there), cited as:
- REA: the act of 24.03.1920 on the acquisition of real estate by foreigners, consolidated Dz.U. 2017 poz. 2278, with Dz.U. 2026 poz. 1099 from 19.11.2026;
- BORDER: the border act art. 12 and the 2005 list of the zone's municipalities;
- REG: the regulation of the Minister of Internal Affairs (MSW) of 20.06.2012 (the application and its documents);
- FEES: the stamp duty act, with Dz.U. 2026 poz. 846 (refunds from 1.10.2026);
- KPA: the administrative procedure code (filing, time limits, reconsideration);
- PAGES: MSWiA's permit page (01.10.2026) and its FAQ (27.06.2022).

Labels: FACT, INFERENCE, OPEN (OPEN-R1 to R7 in the sources README), PROPOSED.

**Content rules for this matter (PROPOSED):**
- **The first question is "Czy potrzebuję zezwolenia?", before any contract.** The app never says a purchase is fine until the property type, the place (border zone) and the person's status are known; "Nie wiem" on any of them leads to "zapytaj notariusza przed umową", not to a guess.
- **Citizenship is asked only for the EEA rule** (art. 8 ust. 2), by the document; the residence status by the permit's name and date (art. 8 ust. 1 pkt 2–3). Never inferred from language (rule 4).
- **No legal advice on contracts, taxes or prices.** The app names the notary and the ministry; matter 12 (taxes) is set aside.
- **No action claimed:** never "złożyliśmy wniosek do MSWiA".

| Stage | Goal | Steps (phone / **off the phone**) | Basis | Where people get lost |

| **R3** Waiting | Answer the ministry | Phone: the case's letters (e-delivery or hybrid, PAGES); a reminder to keep the seller's will current up to the decision (PAGES) | KPA art. 35; REA art. 1 ust. 1a, 3b (FACT) | **RL6:** the seller withdraws; the case is discontinued at the applicant's request or on the owner's declaration not to sell (FACT, art. 3b), and the fee is refunded on application when no permit is issued; from 1.10.2026 within 2 months (FACT, stamp duty art. 9; Dz.U. 2026 poz. 846) |
| **R4** The permit | Buy within its validity | **Off: the notarial deed within 2 years, with the permit (and proof of any special conditions)** | REA art. 3 ust. 2, 5 (FACT) | **RL7:** lets the 2 years pass (FACT, art. 3 ust. 2) |

## 3. The critical moments

1. **Before signing a preliminary contract** (R0): whether a permit is needed. A void purchase is the worst outcome.
2. **The documents' age** (R1): 6 months for the property documents, 3 months for the company documents (REG § 3–4).
3. **2 years from the permit's issue** (R4); **1 year** for a promesa.
4. **2 years from the opening of the succession,** for a will's heir not entitled by law (art. 7 ust. 3): the permit must be obtained on an application filed within them.

## 5. User flow (PROPOSED)

**One case:** "Zakup nieruchomości". It links to the residence cases (the permit and its date) and the civil status map (marriage, inheritance).

### 5.1 Intake (each with "Nie wiem")

| Order | Question | Options | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Co chcesz kupić? | mieszkanie; garaż; dom lub działkę; grunt rolny; udziały w spółce; Nie wiem | REA art. 8 ust. 1, 3e |
| 2 | W jakiej gminie? | a municipality; Nie wiem | REA art. 8 ust. 3; BORDER |
| 3 | Jakie masz obywatelstwo (dokument)? | UE, EOG lub Szwajcaria; Wielka Brytania; inne; Nie wiem | REA art. 8 ust. 2 |
| 4 | Jakie masz zezwolenie na pobyt i od kiedy? | pobyt stały (data); rezydent UE (data); pobyt czasowy; wiza lub ruch bezwizowy; Nie wiem | REA art. 1a ust. 2, 8 ust. 1 pkt 2–3 |
| 5 | Czy masz męża lub żonę z obywatelstwem polskim i czy nieruchomość wejdzie do waszego majątku wspólnego? | tak; nie; Nie wiem | REA art. 8 ust. 1 pkt 3 |
| 6 | Czy to spadek, zapis albo zakup od kogoś z rodziny? | spadek lub zapis po kimś, po kim dziedziczył(a)byś z ustawy; testament lub zapis po kimś innym; zakup od kogoś, po kim dziedziczył(a)byś z ustawy; nie; Nie wiem | REA art. 7, 8 ust. 1 pkt 4 |

### 5.2 The levels

0 **Czy potrzebuję zezwolenia** → 1 **Dokumenty** → 2 **Wniosek** → 3 **Czekanie** → 4 **Akt notarialny**.

### 5.3 Each screen

| Screen | Content |
|---|---|
| **Teraz** | "Sprawdź, czy potrzebujesz zezwolenia, zanim podpiszesz umowę", "Zbierz dokumenty (nieruchomość: nie starsze niż 6 miesięcy; firma: 3 miesiące)", "Złóż wniosek do MSWiA". Never "Zezwolenie wydane" |
| **Mapa** | Levels 0–4 |
| **Teczka** | The seller's statement, the property documents with their dates, proof of ties, sworn translations, the fee receipt, the permit |
| **Kalendarz** | Each document's age (6 months for the property, 3 for a company); the permit's 2 years; the promesa's 1 year; a will's 2 years |
| **Czat** | The rule for the person's case, with its article |

### 5.4 Handoffs

| When | To | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| The permit, the promesa, a discontinuance | MSWiA, Department of Permits and Licences | REA art. 1; PAGES |
| The contract and the deed | A notary | REA art. 5 |
| Agricultural land | The agricultural act's body (OPEN-R1) | REA art. 1a ust. 6 |
| A refusal | A lawyer: reconsideration, then the administrative court | KPA art. 127 § 3 (FACT); the court route not read |

## 6. What production would need (PROPOSED)

- **A procedure in the registry** with REA art. 1, 1a, 3, 3d, 5–8, the fees, and the border zone's list as data (after OPEN-R2 is closed). Doc 19's gate for this matter.
- **Change monitoring:**
  - Dz.U. 2026 poz. 1099 from 19.11.2026 (the notary's repository), and the two regulations it waits for: the fee and storage period (REA art. 8a ust. 1d) and the format (Prawo o notariacie art. 92a § 7) (OPEN-R6);

  - the stamp duty rates and refunds (art. 9, as changed by Dz.U. 2026 poz. 846 from 1.10.2026);
  - the MSWiA page's channels (ePUAP, OPEN-R7).
