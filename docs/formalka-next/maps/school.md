# Map: school and preschool for a child from abroad

**Status:** a lab map by Claude, 17.10.2026. It is not independent, and nothing in it is built or shown to people. Doc 19 matter 16 (wave 5). Production's registry has nothing on schools. **Not yet checked by the separate checking agent:** that check comes before anything here is relied on.

**Why it matters:**
- A child from abroad attends a public preschool and school "na warunkach dotyczących obywateli polskich", whatever the family's residence status (FACT, Prawo oświatowe art. 165 ust. 1–2). Status matters only for adult and post-secondary schools (FACT, art. 165 ust. 3–5).
- The district primary school admits the child by law, during the year too, and places it by its foreign documents, its age and the parent's opinion, or by an interview when there are no documents (FACT, art. 130 ust. 2, 5; the regulation § 4, 11, 12).
- Preschool and other schools go through recruitment on dates each municipality sets by the end of January (FACT, art. 154 ust. 1). A late arrival meets places already filled.
- The special act's rules for Ukrainian children mostly ended on 31.08.2026 (FACT, the special act art. 51–57). One that remains lets a head remove from the list a child who does not live in the municipality and is absent for two months (FACT, art. 55c).
- For most foreigners from outside the EU, 800+ is paid only while the school or preschool duty is confirmed in the education register (FACT, `maps/child-benefit-800.md`).

**Sources:** `research/2026-10-17-school/sources/` (README there), cited as:
- PO: Prawo oświatowe, consolidated Dz.U. 2026 poz. 820;
- REG: the regulation of 23.08.2017 on educating foreigners and Polish citizens schooled abroad, consolidated Dz.U. 2023 poz. 2301, amended by Dz.U. 2026 poz. 1139 (from 12.09.2026);

**Content rules for this matter (PROPOSED):**
- **Never "dziecko nie może iść do szkoły" because of the family's status.** For children the act sets no status condition. The status question appears only for adults, post-secondary schools and vocational courses.
- **No computed recruitment dates.** Each municipality sets its own by the end of January. The app says so and asks the person to check the municipality's or the school's page, then stores the dates the person enters.
- **The class is the head's decision.** The app explains on what it rests (the documents, the age, the parent's opinion, an interview) and never names a class.
- **"Nie wiem" on documents is normal.** The regulation provides for a child without them.

By the child's age and what the family says, never by its language.

## 3. The critical moments

1. **Arrival in the middle of the year** (SL4): the district school admits by law; preschool depends on free places.
2. **January** (S2): the municipality publishes the dates and criteria for the next school year.
3. **A refusal** (SL5): 3 days at each step in recruitment, 7 days under the regulation; then the administrative court.
4. **A long absence** (SL8): a trip abroad of a month or more.
5. **The child turns 7, or 6** (S0): the school duty, or the preschool year, starts with that calendar year's school year.

## 5. User flow (PROPOSED)

**One case:** "Szkoła lub przedszkole dla dziecka". It links to the address registration case (the address), the PESEL case, and, for families getting 800+, the 800+ case.

### 5.1 Intake (each with "Nie wiem")

| Order | Question | Options | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | W którym roku urodziło się dziecko? | the year; Nie wiem | PO art. 31, 35 (the calendar year counts) |
| 2 | Gdzie mieszkacie (gmina, ulica)? | free text; Nie wiem | PO art. 131 ust. 1, 133 ust. 1; the district school |
| 3 | Czy dziecko chodziło do szkoły lub przedszkola za granicą? | tak, mam dokumenty; tak, bez dokumentów; nie; Nie wiem | REG § 2 pkt 3, 12 |
| 4 | Czy dziecko mówi po polsku? | tak; trochę; nie; Nie wiem | PO art. 165 ust. 7 |
| 5 | Do której szkoły chcecie się zapisać? | rejonowa; inna; Nie wiem | REG § 4 |
| 6 | Czy dziecko jest obywatelem Ukrainy i przyjechało po 24.02.2022? | tak; nie; Nie wiem | UA art. 55c; asked because the act names it |

### 5.2 The levels

0 **Która szkoła** → 1 **Dokumenty** → 2 **Zapis** → 3 **Klasa** → 4 **Pomoc w nauce** → 5 **Co roku** → 6 **Koniec szkoły podstawowej**.

### 5.3 Each screen

| Screen | Content |
|---|---|
| **Teraz** | "Zgłoś dziecko do szkoły rejonowej", "Sprawdź terminy rekrutacji w gminie", "Zapytaj dyrektora o dodatkowy polski". Never "Dziecko przyjęte" before the school says so |
| **Mapa** | Levels 0–6 |
| **Teczka** | The foreign certificate, the statement of the years of schooling, the notification or application, any refusal and its reasons |
| **Kalendarz** | The dates the person enters from the municipality; the deadlines written on a refusal; 30 September (notice of schooling abroad) |
| **Czat** | The duty, the terms, the help at school, with their articles |

### 5.4 Handoffs

| When | To | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Which is the district school; preschool places | The municipality | PO art. 31 ust. 10, 39 ust. 5 |
| Enrolment, the class, Polish lessons | The head of the school | PO art. 130 ust. 2; REG § 4, 11, 17 |
| A deferral or an early start | A counselling centre ("poradnia psychologiczno-pedagogiczna") | PO art. 36 ust. 2, 6 |
| A refusal | The appeal committee or the head, then the administrative court | PO art. 158 ust. 8–9; REG § 15 |
| Another school named for language learners | The education superintendent ("kurator oświaty") | PO art. 166 ust. 2 |
