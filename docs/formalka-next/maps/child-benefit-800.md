# Map: the child benefit 800+ for foreigners

**Status:** a lab map by Claude, 17.10.2026. It is not independent, and nothing in it is built or shown to people. Doc 19 matter 13 (wave 5). Production's registry has nothing on benefits. **Checked by the separate checking agent on 17.10.2026** (`research/2026-10-17-benefits/sources/CHECK.md`); the corrections are changed in the lab, not independently verified.

**Why it matters:**
- Most foreigners from outside the EU now get 800+ for a month only if they were "aktywne zawodowo" in Poland in the month before, and only while the child meets the preschool, school or education duty, confirmed in the education register (FACT, the 800+ act art. 1 ust. 2a, 2d). The dates differ by group (FACT):
  - lit. c–f: from 1.02.2026, applied first to the period from 1.06.2026 (Dz.U. 2025 poz. 1301 art. 14, 29 pkt 2; for art. 23 ust. 3a, Dz.U. 2026 poz. 203 art. 31);
  - PESEL UKR (lit. ca): from 1.02.2026 under the special act art. 26 ust. 1a, then from 5.03.2026 under the 800+ act (1301 art. 10 pkt 13, 23; 203 art. 30);
  - lit. a, b and g: not subject to these conditions.
- ZUS checks this every month and withholds payment when a register does not confirm it (FACT, art. 1 ust. 2f, 21 ust. 1a, 23 ust. 3a; ZUS 22.09.2026).
- Everything happens online and on the ZUS profile, including the letters, which count as delivered when opened or 14 days after they are placed there (FACT, art. 13 ust. 5, 13a ust. 5–6). ZUS may also call a person in: "osobiście, w wyznaczonym terminie" for anyone, within 3 working days for a PESEL UKR holder (FACT, art. 23 ust. 1a, 3b).

**Content rules for this matter (PROPOSED):**
- **Never "dostaniesz 800+" or "nie dostaniesz".** ZUS decides month by month. The app names each condition and what the person has told it of each.
- **The status is asked, never inferred** (rule 4): the residence card's annotation, temporary protection, a Blue Card. Citizenship matters only where the act names it: a child who is a Polish citizen is exempt from the work condition (art. 1 ust. 2a).
- **No income or contribution computed.** The 50 % of the minimum wage is quoted; whether a job meets it is ZUS's check.
- **The ZUS profile is where letters arrive.** The app says so every time a step depends on a letter.

## 3. The critical moments

1. **The month after a job ends** (BL11): no benefit for the next month unless another title counts. An unemployment benefit counts as activity with no limit. Without it, a registered unemployed person insured under art. 66 ust. 1 pkt 24 gets 3 months, or 6 for three or more children, once a period.
2. **The start of the school year** (BL4): the child's record in SIO decides the payment.
3. **A ZUS call** (BL9, BL12): it arrives only on the ZUS profile.
4. **The application window** (BL7): from 1 February, by 30 April.
