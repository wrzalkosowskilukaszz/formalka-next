**Problem.** For the health insurance sources, the nine acts amending the act on health care benefits were checked by searching each for the articles the map would cite (art. 2, 3, 66–69, 81, 240). The working notes concluded that the others "do not touch" the cited articles. Dz.U. 2026 poz. 203 art. 7 in fact repeals art. 12 pkt 13 and art. 50 ust. 2 pkt 1a. The first is the care for Ukrainian citizens under the old special act; the second, their identity documents at the clinic. Both are cited in the map. The gap was found before anything was written to the repository, when the check was widened to list every article each amendment changes.

**Cause.** The search was shaped by expectation: it looked for the articles already on the list, so a change to an article added to the list later (art. 12, art. 50) could not show up.

**Fix.**
- `tools/sources/touched.mjs` lists the amending acts after a date (as `amendments.mjs` does) and, for each, the articles of the amended act that its "W ustawie z dnia …" article names. Quoted new wording is skipped, so an article quoted inside it is neither a change nor the next article. `--cite` flags the changes that touch the extract's articles.
- Its test (`tools/sources/touched.test.mjs`) covers quoted articles, page breaks, the other closing quote marks some PDFs use, and ranges.
- On the health act it flags 2025 poz. 1083 (art. 66, 68, 69, 81), 2026 poz. 203 (art. 12, 50) and 2026 poz. 791 (art. 49, 50, 51, 240). The extract header lists all three.

**Limits.** It reads only amending articles that open with "W ustawie z dnia …" and, for a regulation, amending paragraphs that open with "W rozporządzeniu … z dnia … w sprawie …". Other wordings are reported as "read it by hand". Changes to an annex name no article, so they are listed apart and flagged for reading by hand. It may over-list (an article named in an instruction's own text), which is the safe side.

**Found in use (17.10, civil status sources).** The first version failed on short titles ("o cudzoziemcach") and on titles that amending acts write with a dash ("– Prawo o aktach…", "– Kodeks…"). Both are changed in the lab, with tests; not independently verified.

**Found in use (17.10, vehicle sources).** The tool threw "not an act title" for regulations, so the amendment of the licence documents regulation (Dz.U. 2026 poz. 1162) was read by hand, and only for the points expected. The extract header then said 1162 did not change the quoted parts, when it changes the quoted § 5 ust. 6 and § 14 ust. 4. A separate checking agent found it (`research/2026-10-17-vehicles/sources/CHECK.md`). The tool now reads regulations too, listing the paragraphs (§) each amending paragraph changes, with tests; changed in the lab, not independently verified. It may over-list a paragraph named in a footnote that falls inside the amending text.

**Found in use (17.10, the work map).** The checking agent found a rule no tool listed: Dz.U. 2026 poz. 203 art. 41 extends the work act's art. 3 ust. 1 pkt 12 and art. 5a to Ukrainian citizens staying legally without temporary protection, for 3 years from 5.03.2026. It changes no wording; it applies "ustawy zmienianej w art. 19". The same holds for Dz.U. 2025 poz. 1794 art. 12 and 14 (the old wording for cases opened earlier). `touched.mjs` now also lists such articles ("applies the amended act … read it by hand"), with a test. Run on the work act, it lists 203 art. 41 and 1794 art. 12, 14 and 17.

**Found in use (17.10, the recognition map).** `--cite 93-93j` flagged nothing although Dz.U. 2025 poz. 622 art. 1 changes art. 93, 93a, 93b, 93c, 93f and 93h: a range with a lettered end was read as one odd id and matched nothing, without a word. Three earlier runs had such ranges (`1-24d` for the Karta Polaka act, `225a-225i` for the act on foreigners, `51-57a` for the special act). The tool now reads lettered ranges and throws on a part it cannot read, with a test; changed in the lab, not independently verified. Rerun on 17.10: the Karta Polaka act has no amendment since its consolidated text; the other two flag only acts their sources and checks already cover (`research/2026-10-17-school/sources/CHECK.md`, `research/2026-10-17-permanent/sources/`).

**Found in use (17.10, the business map).** The tool listed Dz.U. 2025 poz. 1794 art. 12, 14 and 17 for the FE act as "read it by hand" (art. 14 keeps the stamp wording of FE art. 4 ust. 2 pkt 5 for older EU long-term resident cases), and Dz.U. 2025 poz. 769 art. 35 for PP. The extract header named none of them; the separate checking agent found it (`research/2026-10-17-business/sources/CHECK.md`). The output was read, but nothing made sure each flagged line reached the header. `touched.mjs --header <extract>` now fails when the header does not name a flagged change: "poz. <position>" followed by "art." (or "§") with its number, before the next "poz.". It counts the transitional rules of an act that changes a cited article, and those that name one. With a test; changed in the lab, not independently verified. Run on the business extract, it also found Dz.U. 2026 poz. 507 art. 17–21, 23–28 and 30, and Dz.U. 2025 poz. 1794 art. 13, 16 and 18, unnamed; all were read and are now listed (none changes a quoted rule).

**Found in use (17.10, the recognition map, its check).** The header named Dz.U. 2025 poz. 622 art. 10, so `--header` passed, but only its pkt 8 (PSWN art. 326a) was read. The same article's pkt 2, 3 and 5 rewrote art. 69, 70 and 323: admission with a certificate outside art. 326a ust. 1 requires NAWA's information, and foreigners a B2 document and an entrance exam. The map called NAWA's information optional. A tool cannot know which changed articles matter to a matter; the rule can: cite every article the point changes that the matter touches (`--cite` with art. 69, 70, 323 here), and read each one. Rerun that way, the tool also flagged Dz.U. 2024 poz. 1897 art. 13 (art. 323 ust. 1 pkt 2). Found by the separate checking agent (`research/2026-10-17-recognition/sources/CHECK.md`).
