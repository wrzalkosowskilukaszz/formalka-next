**Problem.** The Karta Polaka map offered the settlement benefit to a holder applying for permanent residence (KP art. 8a), and quoted the rule that the minister stops it when applications reach 75 % of the year's reserve (art. 24c ust. 2). It did not say that this had happened: the interior minister's notice of 14.01.2026 stops the examination of applications filed from 15.01.2026, and the ministry's list shows no later notice up to 29.09.2026. A person told to apply would wait for money that the act does not pay this year.

**Cause.** The sources were the act and the offices' pages on the card itself. The rule that can stop the benefit was read as a rule, not as a question about today; the ministry's notices were not searched.

**Fix.** The README has a row "Now: on hold" with the notice, the map says so in its first bullets, KS8 and the flow, and the notice and a voivode's page are kept as sources (`research/2026-10-17-karta-polaka/sources/CHECK.md`).

**How it is caught now.** By reading: the checking agent searches the bodies' notices. A rule in an act that lets a notice stop, start or date something (a budget limit, an announcement of technical readiness, as in Dz.U. 2025 poz. 1794 art. 17) is a question to answer for the date of the map, with the notice found or its absence stated.
